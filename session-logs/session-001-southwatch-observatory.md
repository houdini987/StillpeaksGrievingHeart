# Session 001 — Southwatch Observatory

## High-Level Summary

The party began the campaign around Frostline Lodge and the route toward Southwatch Geomantic Observatory, receiving the first hard evidence that Stillpeak's tremors were unnatural, rhythmic, and spreading.

## Key Events

- Party assembled as a level-5 group: Erny the Mountainborn, Bilbo Laggins, Maelreth, Alina, and Sorin Nighthollow
- A dying hunter delivered a journal to Erny, naming Southwatch and the geomancers as important to understanding what was happening
- The journal described pulsing ground, abnormal wildlife behavior, and a sense that the mountain was not merely suffering ordinary geological disturbance
- The party traveled to Southwatch Geomantic Observatory to seek interpretation of the hunter's journal
- The party encountered the observatory exterior and worked their way inside
- The observatory elder reviewed the journal and recognized the signs as serious and actionable
- The geomancers explained enough of geomancy for the party to understand that the tremors were patterned and meaningful rather than random
- The party received the Tremorscope as the primary navigation and interpretation tool for the ascent
