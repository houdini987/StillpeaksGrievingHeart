# Session 04 — Stillpeak's Grieving Heart

## High-Level Summary

The party progressed through Packet 05, resolved the Silent Echo Cairn in play, crossed into the Stonefold Ramps, defeated a single vibration-maddened hill giant, and paused for a short rest before the Wind-Carved Spine.

### Karagvorn Line / Approach Pressure

- The party continued beyond the Karagvorn Line under sustained mountain pressure.
- Environmental pressure beyond baseline traversal was emphasized: wind, cold, unstable footing, and the sense that the mountain itself was reacting.

### Silent Echo Cairn

- The party approached the Silent Echo Cairn and inspected its central stone and surrounding symbols.
- Erny touched the center stone.
- Sorin examined the symbols at the cairn and focused on the fire symbol.
- Sorin touched the fire symbol.
- Maelreth considered the cairn with an Insight check to understand the pattern / intent of the symbols.
- The party’s small sphere / tremorscope-like object began vibrating in response to the site, giving a hook that the cairn was resonating with the same tremor-pattern phenomena affecting the mountain.
- Sorin activated the fire / flame glyph using actual fire.
- Erny used Lay on Hands on the heart symbol, successfully activating the heart portion of the riddle.
- Sorin touched a lit torch to the flame symbol, reinforcing the fire activation beat.
- The flame boon was adjusted in play to be a mild warming effect rather than a larger power spike.
- The final branch symbol was resolved simply: touching it with wood activated it.
- The party solved the Silent Echo Cairn by activating the fire, heart, and branch symbols.
- The party received minor boons from the cairn.
- The dwarven stone whistle was not found / recovered. It remains missed content unless the party revisits the cairn.
- The party took a short rest after the cairn sequence.

### Stonefold Ramps

- The party moved from the cairn toward the Stonefold Ramps.
- They developed a strategy to climb the ramps under dangerous conditions.
- Maelreth used Misty Step in the lower section of the Stonefold Ramps and secured rope for the others.
- A rock was launched / came crashing down the hill, forcing dodge / traversal checks.
- The party took multiple rock impacts during the ramp traversal.
- Maelreth’s rope remained secured below on the Stonefold Ramps and became a tactical carryover.
