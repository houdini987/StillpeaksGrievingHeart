# Session 06 — Stillpeak's Grieving Heart

## High-Level Summary

The party opened the concealed dwarven summit door, crossed from the exterior summit face into Stillpeak, recovered in the Last Breath Refuge, investigated the first evacuation evidence of the Exodus Stair, fought opportunistic intruders in the upper works, and reached the Chain-Lift Void. During the shaft encounter, Erny fell to the bottom of the shaft and the party engaged the Chain-Lift Crawler and Shaft Scuttlers across vertical terrain. The Chain-Lift Crawler was defeated. The party advances to level 8.

## Play-by-Play

### Summit Door / Threshold

- Maelreth and Erny used their mountain attunement to interact with the concealed dwarven summit door.
- The door was opened through resonance rather than a long puzzle.
- The party entered Stillpeak through a narrow upper egress route.
- Exterior pressure released after entry: the physical timer stopped, Mountain Toll pressure ended for the moment, and cold/summit hypoxia pressure became recoverable inside.

### Last Breath Refuge

- The party entered the first interior chamber, later understood as an exit-side recovery chamber rather than a welcoming entrance hall.
- The chamber contained cracked stone benches, broken cistern channels, pressure-seal levers, survivor markings, dead dwarven lighting fixtures, wrong-colored seam-glow, and a survivor slate.
- Erny examined the survivor slate and understood it as a family-based survivor record rather than a military or noble roster.
- Maelreth examined the colored seam-glow and understood that the colors were not a clean magical code or designed lighting system; they were a later resonance symptom leaking through the stone.
- The party completed a short rest in the Last Breath Refuge.
- Short rest cleared active summit hypoxia, exterior cold pressure, and current Mountain Toll burden effects.
- During the rest, the party received memory-whispers of the ancient evacuation: survivor counting, sealing, children, lower lamps, and guilt over those left below.

### Mountain Attunement

- Erny and Maelreth are confirmed as mountain-attuned.
- In Packet 6, their attunement initially helped them sense resonance more clearly.
- During the Chain-Lift Void approach, both began experiencing headaches and intrusive resonance pressure, establishing the first active downside of the connection.
- Attunement strain has begun but has not yet become a hard standing penalty.

### Broken Muster Niches

- The party moved from the Last Breath Refuge into the Broken Muster Niches.
- They explored or discussed the record, wounded, tool, and prayer niches.
- The record niche showed evacuation tallies, bronze tags, family marks, and records that began orderly but deteriorated.
- The tool niche showed emergency engineering supplies such as wedges, braces, pitons, stone dust, and pry-bars used to force or brace pressure doors during evacuation.
- The prayer niche showed a crowned queenly figure beside a mountain arch, her face obscured by soot and scratch marks.
- Erny rolled double natural 20s on a Religion check in the prayer niche.
- Erny understood that the soot was protective mourning rather than desecration, that the figure was loved, and that grief or memory of her had become dangerous.
- The party received the warning: "Do not say her name in the stair. He hears grief before he hears words."
- The queen's death, immortal king truth, and final grief-source solution were not revealed.

### Ice Mephit Squatters

- Four ice mephits emerged from the broken cistern/drainage side infrastructure.
- They were framed as opportunistic squatters, not grief spirits or dwarven ghosts.
- The party defeated or resolved the mephit encounter.
- The mephit nest yielded salvage: mixed coins, two small garnets, a silvered dwarven belt clasp, and a Cold-Seal Lens.

### Chain-Lift Void

- The party reached the Chain-Lift Void: a vertical shaft with broken stair segments, maintenance ledges, a central chain, dead lamp infrastructure, colored seam-glow, and a jammed lift platform below.
- Erny threw a torch down the shaft, revealing movement below and the jammed lift platform.
- Maelreth fired Eldritch Blasts down the shaft and hit the Chain-Lift Crawler, revealing it and escalating the resonance response.
- The Chain-Lift Crawler and three Shaft Scuttlers were placed on the map.
- Erny fell to the bottom of the shaft and was separated from the party at the top.
- The rest of the party remained at the top of the shaft during the initial separation, making vertical rescue/support the core tactical problem.
- The Chain-Lift Crawler was defeated.
- The crawler's nest / jammed lift debris yielded salvage: 42 gp, one potion of healing, one usable climber's kit missing one piton, a silver ring engraved in Dwarvish with "steady hands, safe return," and one Lift-Brake Pin.
- Optional dangerous salvage available if not yet claimed: Unbroken Anchor Ring.
