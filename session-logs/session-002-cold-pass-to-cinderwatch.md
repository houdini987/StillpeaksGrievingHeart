# Session 002 — Cold Pass to Cinderwatch

## High-Level Summary

The party entered the Cold Pass and began experiencing the first sustained environmental distortion, confirming that the mountain's condition was unnatural and escalating.

## Key Events

- Party began traversal into Cold Pass using the Tremorscope for directional guidance
- Environmental anomalies emerged: unnatural frost drift, silence pressure, and altered snow behavior
- The party encountered a frozen deer that collapsed into powder-fine frost when disturbed, confirming that something was fundamentally wrong with the environment
- Tremorscope readings showed intermittent, faint signals rather than stable patterns
- The party followed environmental clues toward Cinderwatch
- Arrival at Cinderwatch revealed a diminished and strained settlement rather than a healthy mountain outpost

## Combat Event

- Encounter with Ice Mephits triggered during anomaly investigation
- Combat reinforced the idea that environmental corruption was producing or attracting hostile entities

## State at End of Session

- Party reached Cinderwatch and established a temporary safe haven
- Environmental corruption confirmed as non-natural and not standard necromancy
- Tremorscope signals still unclear but guiding generally upward

## Advancement
