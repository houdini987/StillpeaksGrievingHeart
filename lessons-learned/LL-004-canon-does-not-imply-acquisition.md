# LL-004 — Canon does not imply acquisition

## Context

Canon packets often include items, clues, or interactions that may not occur in actual play.

## Gotcha / Failure Mode

AI assumes the party has items or knowledge that they never actually acquired.

## Rule Going Forward

Always check session logs before assuming the party has any item, boon, clue, or relationship.
