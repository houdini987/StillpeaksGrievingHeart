# Scene Packet 8 Patch — Ruined Throne Room Threshold

## Canon Status

This patch is canon for Scene Packet 8 / Chapter 8: Throneward Descent.

It clarifies what physically exists immediately before the final boss arena.

This patch supersedes any shorthand that treats Beat 5 as only a generic cracked threshold, antechamber, or sealed doorway immediately adjacent to the geode arena.

## Correct Spatial Relationship

The final approach before the boss arena should pass through the **ruined royal throne room**.

The old public throne room still exists, but it is broken, emptied, and partly swallowed by crystal. It is not the final boss chamber.

Behind the old King's throne is a rear royal passage leading toward the King's private chambers / mountain-heart spaces. That rear passage is the route the party follows to discover the final geode arena.

The final boss arena is deeper beyond the public throne room: a warehouse-sized organic geode cavity formed by the implosion around the grief-engine.

### Spatial Sequence

1. **Implosion Threshold / Ruined Public Throne Room**
   - the party enters the old royal throne room
   - the room is burst and damaged by the old critical-mass event
   - the Crystal Tremorborn / Shardlings may emerge here or from the threshold wounds feeding into it
   - broken court architecture, containment marks, royal seals, and prayer/engineering lines show the failed response
   - the old public throne remains visible as a ruined landmark

2. **Behind the King's Throne**
   - a rear doorway, royal passage, or private access hall opens behind the old throne
   - green-white active light drains toward this passage after the threshold encounter
   - the hallway should feel like it was never meant for the public
   - this is the cliffhanger / end-night image location if the boss is delayed

3. **King's Private Chamber / Geode Arena**
   - the rear passage leads into the actual final arena
   - the final arena is not the ordinary throne room
   - it is the imploded private/mountain-heart chamber where the Mourning King, Dhurak, Veyra, beams, and throne-engine exist

---

## Why This Matters

This spatial model improves playability and visual logic:

- the party reaches the expected throneward destination before the final reveal
- the ruined public throne room gives a strong end-night cliffhanger location
- the boss arena remains surprising because it is found beyond the throne, not visible immediately upon entering the throne room
- the old throne room can show royal failure without forcing the final fight into a conventional rectangular throne room
- the final arena can remain the organic geode-bowl ground-zero cavity already established in canon

## Beat 5 Environment Correction

For Beat 5, prefer this environment framing:

**Environment:** The party reaches the ruined public throne room of Stillpeak. It is no longer a place of court or ceremony. The chamber has been burst by the old implosion: royal geometry bent outward, floor seams split, containment braces twisted, prayer marks and engineering lines buried under crystal overgrowth. The old throne still stands as a broken landmark at the far end of the room. Behind it, a rear royal passage leads toward the King's private chambers and the true source.

This room may still function mechanically as the Implosion Threshold.

The threshold encounter should occur in or around this ruined throne room, not in a generic hallway unless the DM needs a simpler map.

## Beat 5 End-Night Correction

If ending the night before the final boss, end with the party in the ruined throne room after the threshold fight.

The green-white light from broken shard bodies and floor seams should drain toward the rear passage behind the old throne.

The final reveal should be withheld until the party follows that passage.

## Final Arena Preservation

This patch does not change the final boss arena.

The final arena remains:

- warehouse-sized
- organic and geode-like
- a bowl-shaped crystal blast scar
- not a normal throne room
- centered on the Mourning King as a living vessel, the throne-engine, Dhurak, Veyra, and the two green beams

The old public throne room and the final geode arena are two distinct spaces connected by the private passage behind the throne.
