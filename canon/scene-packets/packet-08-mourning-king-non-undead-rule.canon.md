# Scene Packet 8 Supplement — Mourning King Non-Undead Rule

## Canon Status

This supplement is canon for Scene Packet 8 / Chapter 8 and overrides any shorthand that describes the Mourning King as a normal lich or undead creature.

## Core Rule

The Mourning King is **not truly undead**.

Use the lich chassis only as a Roll20 convenience for boss durability, control, and legendary-style structure. Canonically, he is a living-preserved monarch: throne-bound, crystal-sustained, and held far beyond a natural life by the green beam engine.

## Divine Sense / Evil Detection Boundary

Erny has repeatedly checked throughout the adventure for signs of evil, and the module should stay consistent with that theme.

The Mourning King should not register as undead, fiend, celestial, or straightforward evil. If Erny or another character uses divine sense-style detection near him, the correct read is:

- overwhelming grief
- ancient royal will
- crystal preservation
- oath-pressure
- refusal to release
- wrongness, but not a clean evil aura

This keeps Stillpeak's horror centered on grief, greed, denial, corruption, and preservation past mercy rather than a simple undead infestation.

## Druid Titan Boundary

Dhurak and Veyra are also not undead or fiends. Their corruption is Abbathor-touched crystal greed and centuries of warped Stone Druid power.

## Mechanical Consequence

Do not blindly apply undead-specific lich assumptions unless the DM chooses to for Roll20 convenience.

Avoid:

- phylactery language
- necromantic soul-devouring
- undead hunger
- simple evil lich framing

Prefer:

- throne-engine preservation
- crystal stasis
- grief-resonance command
- royal authority turned into compulsion
- living mercy denied

## Legendary Resistance Rule

The Mourning King has **0 legendary resistances**.

Do not import the default lich legendary resistance trait.

His true defenses are:

- green beam protection from Dhurak and Veyra
- the throne-engine
- the geode arena pressure
- grief-resonance control
- the action economy created by two Druid titan beam anchors

This choice supports the theme that he is not a stock lich or evil undead boss. Once the beams are broken, the party's choices and control effects should be allowed to matter.

## Legendary Action Rule

The Mourning King keeps **reduced, simple legendary actions** so he remains present as the central grief-engine without reverting into a stock lich.

Use **2 legendary actions per round**, not 3.

Legendary actions should be limited to grief/crystal control and battlefield pressure. They should not be full lich spellcasting, necromancy, or raw damage nova tools.

Recommended menu:

### Crystal Command

One crystal seam, shard cluster, or throne-root within 60 feet flares. One creature the King can perceive must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or have its speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

### Grief Pulse

One creature within 60 feet hears the King's refusal to release. The target must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or take 7 (2d6) psychic damage and have disadvantage on the next opportunity attack it makes before the end of its next turn.

### Throne Flare

The throne-engine brightens through the green beams. Until the start of the King's next turn, the first time the King takes damage, reduce that damage by 5. Use this only while at least one beam remains active.

These options are intentionally modest. The King's main protection remains the beam system and the Druid titans, not legendary resistance or lich-style dominance.
