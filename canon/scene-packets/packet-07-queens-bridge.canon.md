## 2. Narrative Setup

The Queen's Bridge spans a vast hollow inside Stillpeak's heart. It was once a sacred or civic crossing used in formal procession. The queen fell here during a tremor. The fall was witnessed, accidental from the witnesses' perspective, and catastrophic.

The bridge is now saturated with memory. Spirits relive different moments along the span: procession, tremor, failed rescue, and immediate aftermath. The party must cross the bridge to continue toward the next stage, but witnessing the tragedy draws the attention of the chamber and awakens a foreign predator beneath the span.

The Hollow Below is not the grief-source. It is an ancient tenant of the wound, an opportunistic darkness that took residence under the bridge and feeds on fear, grief, and repeated witness.

**Ingestion Boundary:** This file presents the Chapter 7 table-facing truth. It should not reveal the hidden Chapter 8 truth that the tremor was secretly induced by Dhurak Stonevein and Veyra Deeproot through Abbathor-touched crystal corruption.

Use as the party steps through the final arch from the Witness Halls.

>
> Far below, darkness swallows every falling sound before it can return.
>
> And on the bridge, the dead are already walking.

## 4. Environment Overview

### Bridge Features

- Broad ceremonial bridge spanning a deep inner cavern.
- Continuous but damaged crossing; the bridge must not be broken into disconnected spans.
- Cracked rails, broken parapets, central fall-scar, and unstable stonework.
- Crystal formations along both sides and far-side anchor points.
- Dead lamp posts, ceremonial sconces, and processional brackets.
- Open chasm below.
- Far-side threshold marked by royal/civic symbols and a lower throneward route.

### Visual Logic

The bridge should not look like a random crossing. It was important before the tragedy. It should have architectural dignity even in ruin.

For maps and image prompts, depict a damaged but intact ceremonial span. Use cracks, fractured tiles, edge gaps, broken rail sections, and a central fall-scar for danger. Do **not** depict the route as severed, collapsed across, or split into disconnected islands.

### Spirit Density

Spirit density is highest here. The ghosts are not generic dead. They are the queen's procession, witnesses, attendants, guards, priests, and immediate responders.

## 5. Running the Set Piece

### Structure

Run the Queen's Bridge in five phases:

1. **Procession Memory** — the party sees the sacred/civic procession.
2. **Recognition Trigger** — the spirits notice the party; crystals flare; The Hollow Below awakens.
3. **Initiative Crossing** — combat and bridge hazards run while the memory continues.
4. **Fall Revelation** — the tremor and queen's fall become unambiguous.
5. **Far-Side Aftermath** — the party crosses and receives the throneward handoff.

### Initiative Trigger

The party should not be able to safely retreat or sprint across before the set piece begins. Once they are emotionally/physically drawn close enough, the spirits turn to look at them, crystals flare, and initiative starts.

### Memory-First Rule

Combat should not erase the reveal. Every round or major phase should show another part of the tragedy.

## 6. Bridge Hazard System — Tremor Memory Track

Start the **Tremor Memory Track** at 0 when initiative begins.

Add 1 when:

- a creature fails a bridge movement check by 5 or more
- thunder or force damage is used carelessly against bridge stone or crystal
- a character shouts grief, panic, or the queen's name if later established
- The Hollow Below uses Memory-Lure
- a creature falls prone or is restrained on a broken bridge section
- the DM wants the memory to escalate

At **3 Tremor Memory**, trigger one Tremor Memory Event and reset the track to 0.

### Tremor Memory Events

Choose or roll:

1. **Bridge Lurch:** All creatures on the bridge make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw** or fall prone and slide 5 feet toward the nearest edge or crack.
2. **Stone Shear:** A 10-foot section becomes difficult terrain. A creature in that section makes **DC 14 Acrobatics or Athletics** or takes 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.
3. **Ghost Overlap:** A spirit reenactment crosses through living bodies. Each creature in a 15-foot line makes **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw** or loses reactions until its next turn.
4. **Crystal Flare:** Crystal light pulses. Erny and Maelreth make **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or take 9 (2d8) psychic damage; others take 4 (1d8) on a failure.
5. **Gap Opens:** A crack widens. Crossing it requires **Athletics or Acrobatics DC 15**.
6. **The Fall Repeats:** Advance immediately to the next fall-memory spoken segment.

## 7. Phase 1 — Procession Memory

**Purpose:** Establish that the bridge was sacred/civic and the queen was crossing in ceremony.

**Read-Aloud:**

> The first spirits appear not in panic, but in order.
>
> A procession crosses the bridge: lanterns, banners, bowed heads, armored guards, robed attendants, and at their center a queen in pale ceremonial cloth.
>
> No one is fleeing.
>
> Not yet.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Religion DC 14:** This is sacred/civic procession, not military escort.
- **History DC 14:** The queen's presence suggests public ritual or royal vow.
- **Insight DC 13:** The mood is reverent and calm, before disaster.
- **Perception DC 13:** Notice spirit onlookers lining the sides and far threshold.

**Success:** The party understands this was an honored event.

**Failure:** The party sees ceremony but may not understand its significance until later.

---

## 8. Phase 2 — Recognition Trigger / Roll Initiative

**Purpose:** Start the encounter without letting the party bypass the bridge.

**Trigger:** The party moves onto or near the near span, studies the memory too closely, attempts to address the procession, or starts across.

**Read-Aloud:**

> The procession reaches the center of the span.
>
> The queen turns her head, though the memory has not done that before.
>
> One by one, the other spirits follow.
>
> Lantern-bearers. Guards. Priests. Witnesses.
>
> They look at you.
>
> The crystals lining the bridge flare in answer, each vein brightening like an opened eye.
>
> For one breath, the bridge is still.
>
> Then something beneath the span moves.
>
> Not stone settling. Not the memory. Not the queen.
>
> A darkness that has been hiding under the grief reaches up through the cracks.
>
> Roll initiative.

**Encounter Package:**

- 1 **The Hollow Below** under the bridge.
- 2 **Crystal Shardlings** emerge from far-side crystal formations when initiative begins.
- Tremor Memory Track active.
- Spirit reenactment continues.

Use stat blocks from `packet-07-monsters.canon.md`.

## 9. Phase 3 — Initiative Crossing

**Purpose:** Make crossing dangerous while preserving the memory reveal.

### Bridge Movement Checks

Require checks when a creature:

- crosses a broken section
- is pulled by The Hollow Below near an edge
- dashes across unstable stone
- tries to help another creature while on a fractured span
- crosses through a Tremor Memory Event

Suggested checks:

- **Acrobatics DC 14:** Cross cracked or tilting stone.
- **Athletics DC 14:** Leap, grab, or pull another creature back.
- **Survival DC 13:** Identify safest immediate route across.
- **Perception DC 13:** Spot a stable rail, handhold, or intact seam.

Failure by 1–4: lose movement, fall prone, or take 1d6 bludgeoning/piercing damage.

Failure by 5+: add 1 Tremor Memory and place the creature at an edge/gap risk.

### Useful Player Counterplay

Reward:

- rope use
- pitons or climber's kit
- Resonance Chalk
- Lift-Brake Pin or other dwarven mechanical tools from Packet 6
- Soot-Black Prayer Bead during memory/fear/grief effects
- teamwork actions to anchor or steady each other

### Spoken Round Cues

Use one at initiative count 20 each round or when pacing needs the memory to advance.

**Round Cue A — The Bridge Starts Remembering:**

> Beneath the fight, the bridge continues its memory.
>
> Spirit feet move through living legs. Lanterns pass through broken stone. The queen walks toward a place where the bridge is no longer whole.

**Round Cue B — The Hollow Below Pulls Against the Memory:**

> The darkness under the bridge does not join the memory.
>
> It feeds beside it.
>
> Its tendrils climb through cracks like roots seeking warmth.

**Round Cue C — Shardlings Wake the Far Side:**

> Across the span, the two crystal creatures skitter along the far-side stone, their bodies ticking in time with the bridge's pulse.

## 10. Phase 4 — Fall Revelation

The fall must become clear during the encounter. Do not leave the cause ambiguous.

Use these revelation beats as the party advances across the bridge or as Tremor Memory Events occur.

### Revelation Beat 1 — The Tremor

> The tremor comes without warning.
>
> Not blades. Not arrows. Not betrayal.
>
> The mountain moves.
>
> The bridge drops beneath her feet.

### Revelation Beat 2 — Failed Rescue

> Hands reach for her.
>
> One catches torn cloth. One catches air. One guard throws himself to the stone so hard his armor sparks against the bridge.
>
> Someone screams for rope.
>
> Someone else is already looking down, knowing the fall is too deep.

### Revelation Beat 3 — The Fall

> For one heartbeat, the queen hangs between bridge and darkness.
>
> Then the cloth tears.
>
> The lanterns above her go white.
>
> She falls beyond their reach, down into the hollow heart of Stillpeak.
>
> The scream that follows does not come from her.
>
> It comes from the mountain.

### Revelation Beat 4 — Immediate Aftermath

> Then order breaks.
>
> Dwarves scatter in every direction.
>
> Some run toward the deep royal road, shouting that the king must be told.
>
> Some race for lower stairs with ropes and lanterns, though the dark below the bridge has already swallowed the light.
>
> Some stay where they are, praying, ordering, screaming, or simply staring down.
>
> The fall is over.
>
> The catastrophe is only beginning.

### Interpretation Checks

Allow during or after the encounter:

- **Insight DC 13:** The fall appeared to witnesses as accident, not visible betrayal.
- **History DC 14:** The scattering is immediate crisis response, not battle formation.
- **Religion DC 14:** The procession was sacred/civic and the aftermath becomes mourning panic.
- **Perception DC 13:** Several spirits rush toward a throneward/lower royal route.
- **Arcana DC 15:** The crystals flare in response to grief, not conscious command.

## 11. Phase 5 — Far-Side Mourning Threshold

**Purpose:** End Chapter 7 after the bridge with one short story scene.

**Environment:** The far side of the bridge is a threshold chamber, stairhead, or broken royal approach. It contains dropped procession relics, torn cloth, cracked prayer beads, dead lanterns, and route markings that point downward toward throneward spaces.

**Read-Aloud:**

> The far side of the bridge does not feel safe.
>
> It feels after.
>
> Torn strips of ceremonial cloth lie caught in cracks. Dead lanterns are scattered where hands dropped them. Prayer beads lie broken underfoot, blackened by age and worry.
>
> Spirits rush through the threshold in fragments of panic: one toward the bridge, one toward lower stairs, one deeper into the royal dark.
>
> None of them stay long enough to be saved.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Dwarvish DC 12:** Read a marker indicating a lower royal/throneward route.
- **History DC 14:** Understand the passage is royal/civic, not residential.
- **Religion DC 14:** Recognize dropped objects as sacred/processional remnants.
- **Insight DC 13:** Spirits are reliving immediate aftermath, not guarding the passage.
- **Perception DC 13:** Notice spirits rushing downward with repeated phrases about warning the king or reaching the cavern floor.

**Spirit Fragments:**

> "Tell the king—no, not like that—do not let him hear it from the bridge."

> "Ropes. Lower ropes. She fell below the lantern reach."

> "No one goes alone. The cavern is too deep."

> "Throneward. Someone must tell him. Someone must survive telling him."

> "Do not say her name while running. Do not carry grief before the throne."

**Chapter Endpoint Read-Aloud:**

> The last spirit does not flee toward the bridge.
>
> He runs deeper.
>
> Down a stair marked by a cracked royal sigil, clutching a torn strip of white procession cloth in both hands.
>
> "Throneward," he whispers, though no breath moves through him. "Someone must tell him. Someone must survive telling him."
>
> The spirit vanishes into the dark below.
>
> The crystals along the stair answer with a slow pulse.
>
> Not a direction.
>
> An invitation.
>
> Or a warning.

## 13. DM Reference Sidebar

### Must-Hit Truths

- The queen fell because of a tremor.
- The event occurred during a sacred/civic procession.
- The fall was witnessed.
- Chapter 7 shows what the witnesses experienced, not the hidden induced-tremor cause.
- Dwarves scattered immediately to warn the king and search below.
- The route ahead points throneward.

### Must-Avoid

- Do not imply visible murder or open betrayal.
- Do not reveal that the tremor was secretly induced.
- Do not reveal Dhurak Stonevein, Veyra Deeproot, Abbathor, king immortality, or the final solution.
- Do not make The Hollow Below responsible for the fall.
- Do not make this a geomancer scene.

### Monster Use

Use:

- **The Hollow Below** as primary enemy.
- **2 Crystal Shardlings** only when The Hollow Below activates.
- No additional grief-crystal elementals on the bridge unless the party is overwhelming the encounter and you need terrain complications; prefer hazards over more monsters.
