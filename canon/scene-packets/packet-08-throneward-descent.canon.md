## 2. Narrative Setup

The party leaves the Queen's Bridge behind and follows the royal route downward.

The city does not open into exploration. It narrows into consequence.

Everything in Chapter 8 should feel like the mountain is becoming less architectural and more anatomical. Formal royal stonework persists, but each beat shows more crystal intrusion. Dead lamps remain visible, but they are less important now; the crystals are no longer just contaminating the old kingdom. They are consuming it.

The chapter must deliver lore before the final boss. The party should understand what Dhurak, Veyra, and the king became before they enter the King's Chamber.

## 3. Read-Aloud Scene Introduction

Use after the party completes the Far-Side Mourning Threshold and commits to the throneward route.

> The stair beyond the bridge does not descend like a servant's passage or a miner's cut.
>
> It descends like a promise made by kings.
>
> Royal sigils crack beneath your boots. Dead lanterns hang in ordered rows. Carved stone lines guide the route downward with a certainty that feels almost cruel.
>
> Behind you is the bridge where the queen fell.
>
> Ahead, the crystals grow thicker.
>
> Not brighter.
>
> Hungrier.

## 4. Environment Overview

### Spatial Model

Packet 8 should use a controlled linear footprint:

1. **Throneward Descent** — royal approach route.
2. **Advisor Records** — unavoidable lore: Dhurak and Veyra introduced.
3. **Druid-Centric Reveal** — hidden tremor truth and Abbathor-touched corruption.
4. **Mining Decree / Greed Spiral** — king's culpability and deeper mining orders.
5. **Implosion Threshold** — transformation, containment failure, evacuation crisis.
6. **King's Chamber / Crystal Boss Arena** — final encounter.

Do not build a sprawling royal complex.

### Crystal Escalation

Increase crystal saturation every beat.

- Early: thin seams through formal royal stone.
- Middle: crystal growths interrupt records and reliefs.
- Late: crystal roots overwrite rooms, stairs, and memory surfaces.
- Final: the King's Chamber is a crystal engine with throne, beams, bosses, and floor all connected.

### Lore Delivery

Lore should be unavoidable but not a lecture. Use:

- broken carvings
- royal decrees
- Stone Druid tablets
- crystal-memory flashes
- inscriptions split by crystal roots
- reliefs rewritten by mineral growth
- short spirit or echo fragments

---

## 5. Running the Chapter

### Pacing Rule

Chapter 8 should move through escalating culpability:

1. The party knows the route leads to the king.
2. The party learns the king had two trusted Stone Druid advisors.
3. The party learns those Druids were corrupted through greed.
4. The party learns the tremor was secretly induced.
5. The party learns the king's denial enabled deeper mining.
6. The party sees the implosion's aftermath.
7. The party fights the grief-engine those choices created.

### Reveal Boundary

By the final boss, the party should already understand the beams and the Druids' role. The final chamber should not need a lore monologue.

## 6. Encounter Modules

### BEAT 1 — Throneward Descent

**Purpose:** Transition from Queen's Bridge aftermath into royal descent.

**What the Party Is Doing:** Following throneward route markers into the lower royal spaces.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Dwarvish DC 12:** Read royal/throneward route signs.
- **History DC 14:** Recognize this as a formal royal approach rather than a residential or mining route.
- **Perception DC 13:** Notice crystals growing more thickly along seams, corners, and sigil cuts.
- **Arcana or Religion DC 15:** Sense that the resonance is less like memory now and more like an active system.
- **Survival DC 13:** Identify passable portions of the cracked descent.

**Success:** The party understands they are approaching royal source spaces and that the crystal presence is intensifying.

**Failure:** They still proceed, but the next lore chamber must do more orientation work.

**Spoken Cue:**

> The royal stair was built to make descent feel orderly.
>
> The crystals disagree.
>
> They split the careful geometry in crooked green-white lines, turning every carved sigil into a wound that still glows.

---

### BEAT 2 — The Advisor Records

**Purpose:** Introduce Dhurak Stonevein and Veyra Deeproot as trusted Stone Druid confidants.

**Environment:** A royal records chamber, advisor alcove, or carved gallery. It contains tablets, relief panels, ceremonial tools, and broken seals naming the king's trusted Stone Druid counselors.

**Read-Aloud:**

> The stair opens into a chamber of records.
>
> Not common ledgers. Not evacuation tallies.
>
> These are royal tablets: heavy stone leaves set into the walls, each carved with names, titles, duties, and oaths.
>
> Two names appear again and again beside the king's seal.
>
> Dhurak Stonevein, Fault-Tender.
>
> Veyra Deeproot, Root-Seer.
>
> Trusted hands of stone. Trusted eyes below.
>
> The crystals have grown through their names more thickly than all the rest.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Dwarvish DC 12:** Read the names and titles.
- **History DC 14:** Understand they were high-trust royal advisors, not minor functionaries.
- **Religion or Nature DC 14:** Recognize Stone Druid roles as mountain/stone interpreters rather than ordinary priests.
- **Investigation DC 15:** Notice their records were altered, revisited, or partially sealed after the queen's fall.
- **Arcana DC 15:** The crystal growth is strongest around their names and symbols.

**Unavoidable Lore:** Dhurak and Veyra were trusted Stone Druid confidants of the king.

**Spoken Discovery Cue:**

> These were not distant scholars.
>
> They stood close enough to advise grief before it became command.

---

### BEAT 3 — The Druid-Centric Reveal

**Purpose:** Reveal that Dhurak and Veyra were self-deceived heretics corrupted by Abbathor-touched crystal greed and that they secretly induced the bridge tremor.

**Environment:** A druidic stone chamber, root-crystal observatory, sealed confidence room, or fault-listening station beneath the royal route. The room should feel less civic and more secret.

**Read-Aloud:**

> The next chamber was not meant for crowds.
>
> The ceiling lowers. The walls curve inward. Stone listening bowls sit cracked along the floor, each one aimed toward the deep faults beneath the bridge.
>
> Druidic carvings wind through the chamber like roots.
>
> But something else has overwritten them.
>
> Crystal veins push through the symbols, green and hungry, splitting old vows with new growth.
>
> At the center, two carved figures kneel with their hands pressed to the stone.
>
> The bridge is carved above them.
>
> The queen is carved above the bridge.
>
> And beneath their hands, the fault-line is open.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Nature DC 14:** Identify stone-druidic fault and root symbolism.
- **Religion DC 15:** Recognize Abbathor-touched greed imagery hidden beneath respectable stone-language.
- **Arcana DC 15:** Understand the crystals amplified fault pressure rather than merely recording it.
- **Investigation DC 15:** Read the sequence: preparation, induced tremor, concealment.
- **Insight DC 13:** The Druids were self-deceived, not cackling villains; they believed power was wisdom.

**Unavoidable Lore:** The Chapter 7 bridge memory was incomplete. The queen did fall during a tremor, and witnesses believed it was an earthquake, but Dhurak and Veyra secretly induced it.

**Spoken Discovery Cue:**

> The bridge memory was not a lie.
>
> The city felt the mountain move.
>
> But here, carved below the royal road, the stone remembers hands that helped it move.

---

### BEAT 4 — The Mining Decree / Greed Spiral

**Purpose:** Reveal the king's culpability: grief and denial made him accept deeper crystal mining in hopes of restoring the queen.

**Environment:** A royal decree chamber, command archive, or broken throneward record hall. The room should blend royal command with crystal overgrowth.

**Read-Aloud:**

> Royal command tablets line the chamber in rows.
>
> Some are cracked. Some are buried halfway under crystal. Some remain perfectly legible, as if the mountain wanted these words preserved.
>
> The king's seal appears at the bottom of each decree.
>
> Mine deeper.
>
> Preserve the resonance.
>
> Trust the Fault-Tender.
>
> Trust the Root-Seer.
>
> Her voice remains below.
>
> Her return requires courage.
>
> No retreat from the heart.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Dwarvish DC 12:** Read the decrees.
- **Insight DC 13:** The king was grieving, not rational.
- **History DC 14:** These orders overrode normal civic caution and containment limits.
- **Investigation DC 15:** The decrees escalate over time from recovery to obsession.
- **Religion DC 15:** Recognize greed in a non-gold form: possession of the dead, refusal of release.

**Unavoidable Lore:** The king was manipulated, but he also chose denial. He permitted deeper crystal mining because he wanted the queen restored.

**Spoken Discovery Cue:**

> This was not greed for coin.
>
> It was worse.
>
> It was the command that grief should be obeyed because it wore a crown.

---

### BEAT 5 — The Implosion Threshold

**Purpose:** Show containment failure, transformed Druids, evacuation chaos, and the beginning of the king's unnatural preservation.

**Environment:** A cracked threshold, containment chamber, or collapsed antechamber before the King's Chamber. It should be almost swallowed by crystal.

**Read-Aloud:**

> The final threshold is not ruined.
>
> It is burst.
>
> Stone bends outward from the center of the chamber in frozen waves. Metal braces twist like soft wire. Prayer marks, engineering lines, and royal seals all vanish beneath crystal grown too quickly to be natural.
>
> In the walls, memory catches and breaks.
>
> Dwarves running.
>
> Priests shouting restraint rites.
>
> Engineers hammering braces into stone already moving under their hands.
>
> Two towering silhouettes unfolding from crystal.
>
> And beyond them, deeper still, a green light fastening itself to a throne.

**Suggested Checks:**

- **Arcana DC 15:** Identify critical mass / resonance implosion evidence.
- **Religion DC 15:** Understand the preservation became profane possession.
- **Investigation DC 14:** Read containment failure sequence.
- **Perception DC 13:** See two giant transformation impressions in crystal walls.
- **History DC 14:** Connect this failure to the later evacuation.

**Unavoidable Lore:** The crystal accumulation transformed Dhurak and Veyra into monstrous crystal-earth titans and sustained the king unnaturally. The kingdom's evacuation followed the disaster.

**Spoken Discovery Cue:**

> The kingdom did not flee because the queen died.
>
> It fled because the living would not let death finish its sentence.

---

### BEAT 6 — King's Chamber / Crystal Boss Arena

**Purpose:** Final encounter and campaign resolution.

Use detailed file: `packet-08-final-encounter.canon.md`.

Required elements:

- The Mourning King locked into his throne's crystal embrace.
- Dhurak Stonevein on one side of the arena.
- Veyra Deeproot on the other side.
- A glowing green beam from each Druid titan into the king.
- All three bosses active from the start.
- Maelreth and Erny's over-attunement affliction at full intensity.
- Destroying each Druid weakens the king and reduces affliction pressure.
- Destroying both beams creates the king's clarity window.
- Primary resolution: mercy death.
- Slim relic-based salvation possibility.

**Entry Read-Aloud:**

> The King's Chamber is no longer a chamber.
>
> It is a crystal wound with walls.
>
> The throne stands at the far end, half-buried in green light. Crystal ribs curl around it like a cage grown from the mountain's own bones.
>
> Inside that embrace sits the king.
>
> Crown fused to crystal. Beard threaded with mineral veins. Hands locked to the throne as if command itself has become a shackle.
>
> To his left, a towering crystal-stone giant turns, fault-lines glowing through its body.
>
> To his right, another rises from rootlike crystal growths, its body threaded into the floor.
>
> From each monster, a green beam burns into the king.
>
> The throne pulses.
>
> The king opens his eyes.
>
> And the mountain grieves through him.

## 8. DM Reference Sidebar

### Must-Hit Truths

- Dhurak and Veyra were trusted Stone Druid advisors.
- They were corrupted through Abbathor-touched crystal greed.
- They secretly induced the tremor that killed the queen.
- The witnesses believed it was a sudden earthquake.
- The king's grief and denial allowed deeper mining.
- Critical mass transformed the Druids and sustained the king.
- The beams visibly sustain the Mourning King.
- Mercy death is the primary clean ending.

### Must-Avoid

- Do not make the final boss battle the first time the party hears the lore.
- Do not make the queen culpable.
- Do not imply the mountain itself is evil.
- Do not make salvation easy.
- Do not add non-boss monsters until they are deliberately designed.
