# Implosion Threshold

## Crystal Tremorborn

**Roll20 Chassis:** Earth Elemental for AC/saves/token convenience only.

**HP:** about 75–85.

**Role:** Tremor foreshadowing, hallway breaker, transition alarm, debuff bruiser. It should unsettle footing more than chunk hit points.

### Trait — Tremor-Body

When the Crystal Tremorborn first takes damage each round, nearby loose stone and crystal shake.

Creatures within 5 feet must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or treat the ground within 5 feet of the Tremorborn as difficult terrain until the start of the Tremorborn's next turn.

### Action — Crystal Slam

The Tremorborn makes one heavy crystal-stone slam.

- Melee Weapon Attack using the Earth Elemental's normal attack bonus.
- Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning/piercing damage.
- The target must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution saving throw** or become **crystal-rattled** until the end of its next turn.

**Crystal-Rattled:** While crystal-rattled, the target cannot take reactions, and the first time it moves before the end of its next turn, it treats the first 10 feet of movement as difficult terrain.

Simpler rider option: on failed save, target is knocked prone instead.

Do **not** use Earth Elemental two-slam Multiattack unless deliberately escalating because the party is overwhelming the segue encounter.

### Optional Recharge — Fault Pulse

Recharge 5–6.

- 20-foot line, 5 feet wide.
- Creatures in the line make **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.
- Failure: 10 (3d6) bludgeoning/piercing damage and knocked prone.
- Success: half damage and not prone.
- The fissure becomes difficult terrain.

## Crystal Shardling

**Roll20 Chassis:** Magma Mephit.

**Role:** Quick harasser and visual proof that the corruption creates lesser life. Inconvenient, not an attrition engine.

### Reskin Rules

- Claws become jagged crystal cuts.
- Fire Breath becomes green-white crystal flare / dead-lamp burst.
- Fire/heat language becomes searing crystal pressure.
- Remove flight if the hallway/map makes it awkward.
- Do not add extra HP or damage.

### Death Burst Guidance

Use the Magma Mephit death burst only if the party is fresh or the fight feels too easy.

Otherwise omit it, or use the lighter fallback:

**Shard Pop:** When a Shardling dies, creatures within 5 feet make **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** or take 3 (1d6) piercing damage.

# King's Chamber Final Encounter

## Core Final Encounter Rule

The Mourning King is not the active combat boss.

Dhurak and Veyra are the active combatants. The King is the throne-bound vessel/battery they are protecting.

---

## The Mourning King

**Roll20 Chassis:** Lich chassis only if a chassis is needed for durability or saving throws.

**Canon Type:** Not undead. Not fiend. Not a straightforward evil aura. Unique living-preserved monarch / crystal-bound vessel.

**Combat Role:** Inert vessel, grief-focus, throne-bound objective.

### Core Rules

- The Mourning King is fastened to the throne and held prisoner by the throne-engine.
- He is not in command of Dhurak or Veyra.
- He does not take normal combat turns while beams remain active.
- He has **0 legendary actions**.
- He has **0 legendary resistances**.
- He does not cast normal lich, wizard, or monarch spells while bound.
- He can speak, but his speech is fragmented, grief-struck, and not tactical command.

### Divine Sense / Detection

If Erny or another character uses divine sense-style detection, the King should not register as undead, fiend, celestial, or clean evil.

Correct read:

- overwhelming grief
- ancient royal will
- crystal preservation
- oath-pressure
- refusal to release
- wrongness, but not a clean evil aura

### Beam Protection

While green beams remain active, the King's true defenses are the beams, throne-engine, and Druid interception.

Preferred damage absorption if needed:

- Both beams active: first 30 damage the King takes each round is absorbed.
- One beam active: first 15 damage the King takes each round is absorbed.
- Both beams broken: this protection ends.

### Druid Sacrifice / Interception

If a party member attacks the King while one or both Druid beams remain active, a living Druid titan intercepts by default.

- If both Druids are alive, choose the one whose beam best fits the visual path.
- If only one Druid is alive, that Druid intercepts.
- Redirect the attack/effect to the intercepting Druid, or have that Druid take the damage/effect in the King's place.
- This does not require the Druid's reaction unless the DM wants the stricter version.
- Once both Druids are destroyed and both beams break, interception ends.

Visual language: green beam yanks taut, crystal roots lunge across the throne, a fault-wall erupts, or the Druid titan tears itself into the attack path.

### After Beams Break

When both beams break:

- the crystals binding him fracture
- the throne-engine loses its hold
- the King reaches a clarity/release window
- the encounter should pivot toward mercy, resolution, or the narrow relic-salvation possibility
- do not turn this into a second full caster phase

## Dhurak Stonevein, the Fault-Tender

**Roll20 Chassis:** Stone Golem or other durable earth creature for AC/HP/saves only.

**HP:** about 180.

**AC:** about 17.

**Legendary Resistance:** 1/day.

**Role:** Tremor Stone Druid, forced movement, fissures, bowl destabilization, anti-positioning.

**Beam Role:** One green beam to the Mourning King remains active while Dhurak lives. Destroying Dhurak breaks his beam.

### Action — Stone Druid Strike

Dhurak strikes the ground, a creature, or both through the same motion.

- One melee attack using the chosen chassis's attack bonus.
- Hit: about 16–18 damage, split between bludgeoning/piercing and crystal force.
- On hit, target makes **DC 15 Strength saving throw** or is pushed 10 feet, preferably downhill toward the bowl center, a crack, or a beam lane.

### Recharge — Fault-Tender Stomp

Recharge 5–6.

Dhurak slams the ground and wakes the geode bowl.

- 30-foot line or 15-foot cone, whichever is easier on the map.
- Creatures in the area make **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw**.
- Failure: 21 (6d6) bludgeoning/piercing damage and knocked prone.
- Success: half damage and not prone.
- The affected area becomes difficult terrain.
- Creatures already on bowl slopes may also need the normal bowl-slope slide check.

### Reaction — Fault Resonance

Trigger: Dhurak takes meaningful damage, usually **20 or more damage from one source**, a critical hit, or a focused strike the DM wants the room to answer.

- Dhurak uses his reaction; at most once per round.
- One cracked line of floor within 30 feet of Dhurak or the attacker trembles.
- One creature on or near that line makes **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.
- Failure: slides 10 feet toward the bowl center or falls prone, DM choice.
- Success: no effect.

Use only while Dhurak's beam remains active. Do not use if Fault-Tender Stomp already made the round tactically busy.

### Optional Button — Bridgefall Pull

Use only if the table needs more movement pressure.

- One creature within 60 feet makes **DC 15 Strength or Wisdom saving throw**.
- Failure: pulled 10 feet toward a crack, beam lane, slope, or hazardous crystal line and loses reactions until the start of its next turn.

## Veyra Deeproot, the Root-Seer

**Roll20 Chassis:** Stone Golem or other durable earth creature for AC/HP/saves only.

**HP:** about 150.

**AC:** about 17.

**Legendary Resistance:** 1/day.

**Role:** Root Stone Druid, restraint, beam reinforcement, preservation pressure, anti-mobility.

**Beam Role:** One green beam to the Mourning King remains active while Veyra lives. Destroying Veyra breaks her beam.

### Action — Crystal-Root Lash

Veyra lashes out with crystal roots or commands roots through the floor.

- One ranged or melee spell attack within 60 feet using the chosen chassis attack bonus or **+7**.
- Hit: about 13–16 piercing/psychic damage.
- On hit, target speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

### Recharge — Crystal Root Snare

Recharge 5–6.

Veyra sends crystal roots through the floor.

- Choose up to three creatures within 60 feet standing on stone/crystal.
- Each makes **DC 15 Strength saving throw**.
- Failure: restrained by crystal roots and takes 10 (3d6) piercing/psychic damage.
- Success: speed reduced by 10 feet until end of next turn.
- Restrained creature may use an action to make **DC 15 Athletics or Acrobatics** to escape.

### Reaction — Root Preservation

Trigger: Veyra takes meaningful damage, usually **15 or more damage from one source**, a critical hit, or a focused strike the DM wants the roots to answer.

- Veyra uses her reaction; at most once per round.
- Crystal roots tighten around one creature within 30 feet standing on stone/crystal, usually the attacker if that makes sense.
- Target makes **DC 14 Strength saving throw**.
- Failure: speed becomes 0 until the start of that creature's next turn.
- Success: speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of that creature's next turn.

Use only while Veyra's beam remains active. Do not use in the same round as Crystal Root Snare unless the party is clearly overwhelming the fight.

### Optional Button — Green Beam Reinforcement

Use sparingly if the fight needs more king-protection.

When the Mourning King takes damage, Veyra flares her beam.

- Reduce that damage by 10–15.
- Or force the attacker to make **DC 15 Wisdom save** or take 7 (2d6) psychic damage.

If Veyra uses Green Beam Reinforcement as a reaction, she cannot also use Root Preservation that round.

## Attuned Offense Backlash Reminder

While at least one green beam remains active, offensive actions by Erny or Maelreth against any boss can trigger Attuned Offense Backlash as defined in `packet-08-final-encounter.canon.md` and `packet-08-attunement-affliction.canon.md`.

Default summary:

- Cap at once per round, not per hit.
- All crystals flash white-green.
- Bowl tremors and shards fall.
- All party members make **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.
- Failure: 7 (2d6) piercing/force damage.
- Success: half damage.
- Characters on slopes may also slide.
- One beam broken: backlash weakens / becomes discretionary.
- Both beams broken: backlash ends.

## Balance Guardrails

- Threshold fight should be short and light.
- No final-arena minions by default.
- Do not run Dhurak/Veyra as Stone Golem multiattack bruisers.
- Do not give Dhurak/Veyra normal druid spell lists.
- Do not give the King normal turns, spellcasting, legendary actions, or legendary resistance while bound.
- The party must deal with the Druid anchors before the King can be meaningfully resolved.
- Once beams break, pivot toward clarity, mercy, or narrow relic-salvation — not a new boss phase.
