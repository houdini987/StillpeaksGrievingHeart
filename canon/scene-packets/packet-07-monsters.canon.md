# Scene Packet 7 Monster Canon — Bridgeward Way Threats

## Canon Status

This file is a canon monster supplement for Scene Packet 7 / Chapter 7: Bridgeward Way.

It is intentionally segmented from the main Packet 7 scene text to avoid the file-size bloat problem encountered with Packet 6.

This file currently defines the Chapter 7 threat package:

- **Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus** — a large grief-crystal elemental modeled from an earth elemental chassis and scaled for a five-character level 8 party.
- **The Hollow Below** — an ancient foreign bridge predator built from a roper chassis and scaled for a five-character level 8 party.
- **Crystal Shardlings** — two small grief-crystal nuisance creatures that emerge only when The Hollow Below activates on the Queen's Bridge.

---

## Grief-Crystal Creature Family

Grief-crystal creatures are malignant resonance growths spawned by corrupted crystal formations inside Stillpeak.

They are **not** directly controlled by the king. The king's grief created the conditions that allow them to form, but he does not command them, perceive through them, or deploy them tactically. They are more like geologic cancers set free by negative energy: pain, pressure, and corrupted resonance taking on motion.

They should feel different from both dwarf spirits and ordinary monsters:

- **Dwarf spirits** are memory-bound victims.
- **Grief-crystal creatures** are malignant resonance growths.
- **The Hollow Below** is a foreign ancient predator unrelated to the grief-source.

### Attunement Rule — Erny and Maelreth

Erny and Maelreth's mountain attunement becomes an explicit disadvantage around grief-crystal creatures.

Grief-crystal creatures instinctively fixate on attuned characters. They do not know Erny or Maelreth as individuals, but the attuned characters resonate at the same wounded frequency. The creatures are pulled toward them like a tremor finding a fault line.

The attuned characters also feel an uncomfortable sympathy for these creatures. They can sense that the creatures are pain and pressure given form, not evil in the way a thinking villain is evil. This sympathy should never remove player agency, but it should create friction, hesitation, intrusive thought, and psychic pressure.

Use this distinction carefully:

- Against grief-crystal creatures, Erny and Maelreth feel painful recognition and hesitation.
- Against The Hollow Below, Erny and Maelreth feel cold absence and foreign predation.

## Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus

**Design Anchor:** Earth elemental-style bruiser / tremor-being with psychic resonance effects.

**Role:** Witness Halls major encounter / Chapter 7 mid-boss.

**Recommended Use:** One Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus as the primary combat threat before the Queen's Bridge. It may be paired with environmental resonance hazards, but should not be paired with multiple additional full-strength monsters unless the party is very fresh.

**Origin:** A large grief-crystal formation in the Witness Halls has taken on motion after centuries of exposure to the king's grief-resonance. It is not a guardian and not a servant. It is an unstable tremor-being: seismic pain trying to become anatomy.

**Behavior:** It moves like a quake becoming a body. It slams, pulses, sheds crystal splinters, and instinctively pursues Erny and Maelreth. It is not tactically intelligent, but it is resonance-driven and difficult to distract from attuned targets.

**Tone:** Tragic, violent, warped, and painful to look at. It should feel like striking a wound that has grown limbs.

### Table Reveal

Use when the Animus awakens in the Witness Halls:

> The crystal seam does not crack.
>
> It unfolds.
>
> A shape pulls itself out of the wall in grinding angles: shoulders of rose-white stone, arms of jagged blue mineral, a headless mass of pulsing crystal where a heart should be.
>
> The floor trembles under it.
>
> Not footsteps.
>
> Aftershocks.
>
> Erny and Maelreth, you feel it before it turns. A pressure behind the eyes. A pulse in the teeth. A grief that is not asking for mercy, but still somehow recognizes you.
>
> Then it comes for you.

## Stat Block — Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus

**Large elemental, unaligned**

**Armor Class:** 16 (crystal hide)  
**Hit Points:** 152 (16d10 + 64)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., burrow 20 ft. through stone or crystal only

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 19 (+4) | 5 (-3) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws:** Str +8, Con +7, Wis +4  
**Skills:** Athletics +8, Perception +4  
**Damage Resistances:** cold, necrotic, poison; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** thunder  
**Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, unconscious  
**Senses:** darkvision 90 ft., tremorsense 90 ft., passive Perception 14  
**Languages:** understands no language; communicates through pulses, intrusive feelings, and seismic pressure  
**Challenge Target:** approximately CR 8–9 as a solo mid-boss for five level 8 PCs, especially with attunement pressure and terrain hazards

### Traits

**Earth Glide.** The Animus can burrow through nonmagical, unworked stone or crystal. While doing so, it does not disturb the material it moves through. It cannot pass through worked dwarven seal-stone, consecrated mourning wards, or metal-reinforced doors unless the DM chooses otherwise.

**Faultline Fixation.** If Erny or Maelreth is within 90 feet of the Animus and the Animus can sense them with tremorsense, the Animus must include one of them among its targets whenever it can do so without obvious self-destruction. This is instinctive resonance attraction, not tactical intelligence.

**Painful Sympathy.** The first time on each of their turns that Erny or Maelreth targets the Animus with an attack or harmful spell, that character must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, the attack or spell is made with disadvantage, and the character takes 5 (1d10) psychic damage as the Animus's pain echoes through the attunement. On a success, the character ignores this effect until the start of their next turn and may briefly read the creature's rhythm; the next attack roll against the Animus before the end of that character's next turn has advantage.

**Resonance Body.** When the Animus takes thunder damage, it has vulnerability to that damage as normal, but the violent resonance also causes a pulse. Each creature within 10 feet of the Animus must succeed on a **DC 14 Strength saving throw** or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone. Erny and Maelreth have disadvantage on this saving throw if they failed Painful Sympathy since the end of their last turn.

**Crystal Shedding.** The area within 5 feet of the Animus is difficult terrain for creatures other than grief-crystal creatures. A creature that starts its turn in this area takes 3 (1d6) piercing damage from grinding crystal shards.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Animus makes two Slam attacks. It may replace one Slam with Psychic Seismic Pulse if that action is available.

**Slam.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) psychic damage. If the target is Erny or Maelreth, the psychic damage increases to 9 (2d8).

**Crystal Shard Hurl.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a **DC 14 Constitution saving throw** or its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

**Psychic Seismic Pulse (Recharge 5–6).** The Animus releases a pulse of grief-resonance through the floor. Each creature of the Animus's choice within 20 feet must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, a creature takes 18 (4d8) psychic damage and cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and can take reactions normally.

Erny and Maelreth make this saving throw with disadvantage. If either fails, they also experience an intrusive flash of pressure, falling stone, and wordless mourning; until the end of their next turn, the first 10 feet of movement they use must be toward the Animus unless another creature uses an action to steady or call them back.

**Faultline Stomp (Recharge 4–6).** The Animus slams the ground and opens a jagged line of crystal in a 30-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw**, taking 21 (6d6) bludgeoning/piercing damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. The line becomes difficult terrain until cleared with an action and a successful **DC 14 Athletics, Acrobatics, or tools check**.

### Bonus Actions

**Resonant Turn.** The Animus turns toward the nearest mountain-attuned creature it can sense and moves up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. It can use this bonus action only if Erny or Maelreth is within 90 feet.

### Reactions

**Sympathetic Fracture.** When Erny or Maelreth hits the Animus with an attack, the Animus may force that character to make a **DC 15 Charisma saving throw**. On a failure, the character takes 7 (2d6) psychic damage and hears, not words, but the sensation of stone trying to remember a scream. On a success, the character takes no damage and learns one brief tactical truth: the Animus is vulnerable to thunder, but thunder makes it pulse violently.

### Bloodied Behavior

When reduced to 76 hit points or fewer, the Animus becomes less body and more quake.

- Its speed increases by 10 feet.
- It loses resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
- Psychic Seismic Pulse recharges on 4–6 instead of 5–6.
- At the start of each of its turns, loose crystals in the room pulse; Erny and Maelreth each take 3 (1d6) psychic damage if they are within 60 feet and conscious.

### Defeat

When reduced to 0 hit points, the Animus collapses into inert crystal and stone. The final pulse should feel sorrowful, not triumphant.

> The crystal body breaks apart in pieces too heavy to fall naturally.
>
> For a moment, each shard glows with the same dim pulse.
>
> Then the light leaves it.
>
> Erny and Maelreth, the pressure behind your eyes vanishes so suddenly it almost feels like loss.

## Encounter Context — Queen's Bridge

The Queen's Bridge is the major Chapter 7 set piece. The party approaches a ghostly reenactment of the queen's fall. As they draw close, the spirits reliving the tragedy turn to look at them, the crystals lining the bridge chamber flare, and something ancient beneath the span awakens.

The bridge enemy is **not** the king's grief, not a dwarven spirit, and not a grief-crystal elemental.

It is a foreign, ancient darkness that took up residence beneath this section of the mountain because the tragedy made the area spiritually rich and comparatively safe. It feeds on repeated fear, grief, and living witnesses drawn toward the fall-memory.

The grief-crystal shardlings are separate: they are malignant resonance growths spawned by corrupted crystal formations. They appear as nuisance pressure when the bridge chamber wakes, not as commanded minions.

## The Hollow Below

**Design Anchor:** Roper-style bridge predator.

**Role:** Set-piece controller / forced-movement threat.

**Recommended Use:** One Hollow Below plus two Crystal Shardlings, bridge hazards, and ghost-memory pressure for a five-character level 8 party.

**Origin:** Ancient foreign darkness that nested beneath the Queen's Bridge after the fall. It is not controlled by the king and does not act on his behalf.

**Behavior:** The Hollow Below does not stand openly on the bridge. It clings to the underside of the span, reaches through cracks and over edges, and uses tendrils of shadowed stone to restrain, drag, and isolate prey.

**Tone:** Predatory, patient, ancient, and wrong. It exploits the queen's fall-memory but did not cause it.

### Table Reveal

Use when the bridge encounter activates:

> The spirits turn toward you.
>
> The crystals lining the bridge flare in answer, each vein brightening like an opened eye.
>
> For one breath, the bridge is still.
>
> Then something beneath the span moves.
>
> Not stone settling. Not the memory. Not the queen.
>
> A darkness that has been hiding under the grief reaches up through the cracks.
>
> Roll initiative.

## Stat Block — The Hollow Below

**Large aberration, chaotic evil**

**Armor Class:** 15 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 138 (12d10 + 72)  
**Speed:** 10 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 19 (+4) | 8 (-1) | 22 (+6) | 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 8 (-1) |

**Saving Throws:** Str +7, Con +9, Wis +6  
**Skills:** Perception +9, Stealth +5  
**Damage Resistances:** cold, necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks while in dim light or darkness  
**Condition Immunities:** prone  
**Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., passive Perception 19  
**Languages:** understands Deep Speech and Dwarvish but cannot speak; communicates through pressure, whispers, and bridge vibrations  
**Challenge Target:** approximately CR 8–9 as a set-piece controller when paired with bridge hazards and two shardlings

### Traits

**False Span.** While motionless and clinging to the underside of a bridge, ceiling, ledge, or stone overhang, the Hollow Below is indistinguishable from a mass of hanging shadow, mineral growth, and old cracked stone.

**Underbridge Predator.** The Hollow Below can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings and bridge undersides, without needing to make an ability check. It has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while attached to the underside of the Queen's Bridge or inside a bridge fissure.

**Many Grasping Tendrils.** The Hollow Below can have up to six tendrils active at one time. Each tendril can be attacked separately (AC 15; 15 hit points; immunity to poison and psychic damage). Destroying a tendril deals no damage to the Hollow Below, but that tendril releases any creature it is grappling. A destroyed tendril regrows at the end of the Hollow Below's next turn unless the Hollow Below has taken radiant damage since the start of that turn.

**Foreign to the Mountain.** Mountain-attuned characters sense the Hollow Below as an absence or cold gap in the mountain's resonance, not as grief. Erny and Maelreth do not feel sympathy for it the way they may for grief-crystal elementals.

**Light-Hating Lair.** Bright light within 20 feet of the Hollow Below suppresses its resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the start of its next turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Hollow Below makes four Tendril attacks, uses Reel, and then makes one Chasm Bite attack against a creature within reach if possible.

**Tendril.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 50 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15). Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the Hollow Below cannot use the same tendril on another target.

**Reel.** The Hollow Below pulls each creature grappled by it up to 20 feet straight toward itself. If this movement would pull a creature over a bridge edge, through a broken span, or into a dangerous gap, the creature may make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw** to catch the edge, seize a railing, or grab nearby stone. On a success, the creature stops at the edge and falls prone. On a failure, it is pulled into the hazardous position determined by the DM.

**Chasm Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. If the target is grappled by the Hollow Below, the attack has advantage.

**Memory-Lure (Recharge 5–6).** The Hollow Below distorts the queen's fall-memory into a predatory call. Up to three creatures of the Hollow Below's choice within 60 feet that can see or hear the bridge memory must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, a creature takes 14 (4d6) psychic damage and must use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed toward the nearest visible bridge edge, fall-point, or ghostly reenactment figure. This movement does not force a creature to step directly into a fall unless no other movement is possible, but it can place the creature in danger. On a success, the creature takes half damage and does not move.

### Bonus Actions

**Extinguish the Witness-Light.** The Hollow Below dims exposed light sources within 30 feet. Nonmagical flames are extinguished unless shielded. Magical light is reduced by half its radius until the end of the Hollow Below's next turn.

### Reactions

**Underbridge Retreat.** When the Hollow Below takes 20 or more damage from a single attack or spell, it may shift up to 15 feet along the underside of the bridge without provoking opportunity attacks. This movement cannot move it onto the top of the bridge.

### Bloodied Behavior

When reduced to 69 hit points or fewer, the Hollow Below becomes more desperate. Its tendrils lash wider, but its hiding discipline weakens.

- Its Tendril reach decreases to 40 feet.
- Its Chasm Bite damage increases by 4 (1d8) piercing damage.
- It can no longer use False Span.

### Defeat / Retreat

If reduced to 0 hit points, the Hollow Below collapses into the chasm as a mass of blackened tendrils and broken stone-shadow. The bridge chamber briefly becomes quieter, but the sense remains that this thing was a tenant of the wound, not the wound itself.

If the party drives it below 30 hit points and stops pursuing it, the DM may have it retreat into the depths instead of dying. If it retreats, it should not immediately return in Chapter 7.

## Crystal Shardlings

**Design Anchor:** Small nuisance minions using simple elemental/blight-style logic.

**Encounter Rule:** Two Crystal Shardlings emerge from crystal formations on the far side of the Queen's Bridge only when the Hollow Below activates. They do not appear before initiative and do not act as scouts.

**Origin:** Malignant grief-resonance growths spawned by corrupted crystal formations. They are not controlled by the king and are not commanded by the Hollow Below. They awaken because the bridge chamber's resonance spikes.

**Behavior:** Shardlings harass, block movement, and punish careless rushing. They are not the main encounter threat.

### Emergence Cue

> Across the bridge, two crystal growths crack open from within.
>
> Something small pulls itself free of each one: all jagged limbs, pulsing mineral light, and the sound of stone grinding against teeth that are not there.

## Stat Block — Crystal Shardling

**Small elemental, unaligned**

**Armor Class:** 14 (crystal hide)  
**Hit Points:** 27 (6d6 + 6)  
**Speed:** 25 ft., climb 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 3 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills:** Stealth +4  
**Damage Resistances:** cold, necrotic, poison  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** thunder  
**Condition Immunities:** poisoned  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages:** —  
**Challenge Target:** nuisance minion for level 8 party

### Traits

**Crystal Climb.** The shardling can climb difficult stone or crystal surfaces without needing to make an ability check.

**Faultline Attraction.** If Erny or Maelreth is within 30 feet of a shardling and the shardling can sense them, the shardling prioritizes that character as its target unless another creature is directly preventing movement or damaging it.

**Resonant Pull.** The first time on a turn that Erny or Maelreth targets a shardling with an attack or harmful spell, that character must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or suffer disadvantage on that attack or spell attack. If the effect forces a saving throw instead of making an attack roll, the shardling has advantage on its first saving throw against that effect. On a success, the character is immune to this shardling's Resonant Pull for 24 hours.

**Shatter-Burst.** When the shardling drops to 0 hit points, it explodes in a burst of jagged crystal. Each creature within 5 feet must make a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw**, taking 5 (2d4) piercing damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. If thunder damage reduced the shardling to 0 hit points, the burst radius increases to 10 feet.

### Actions

**Shard Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) psychic damage. If the target is Erny or Maelreth, the psychic damage increases to 5 (2d4).

**Crystal Spit.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) psychic damage.

**Snagging Growth (Recharge 5–6).** The shardling causes crystal splinters to erupt from the bridge floor in a 10-foot square within 30 feet. The area becomes difficult terrain until the end of the shardling's next turn. One creature in the area must succeed on a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** or take 4 (1d8) piercing damage and have its speed reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. Erny and Maelreth have disadvantage on this saving throw if they are within 30 feet of the shardling.

## Queen's Bridge Encounter Package

Recommended encounter package for five level 8 PCs:

- 1 **Hollow Below**
- 2 **Crystal Shardlings** emerging on the far side of the bridge when initiative begins
- Bridge tremor / memory hazard running as a separate environmental pressure system
- Ghostly reenactment of the queen's fall as the narrative trigger and emotional centerpiece

### Balance Guidance

The Hollow Below is dangerous because of forced movement and bridge terrain. Do not play it as a stationary bag of hit points. Its job is to restrain, pull, isolate, and make the bridge crossing terrifying.

The shardlings should complicate movement from the far side, not overwhelm the action economy. If the party is struggling badly, do not add additional shardlings. If the party is dominating, a tremor pulse may crack another crystal but should create a terrain hazard before spawning more creatures.

The Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus is a separate earlier encounter. Do not run the Animus and Hollow Below in the same encounter.

### Attunement Guidance

Erny and Maelreth should experience a clear distinction between the threats:

- **Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus:** overwhelming sympathy, intrusive hesitation, psychic pressure, and fixation.
- **Crystal Shardlings:** smaller painful sympathy, resonance hesitation, and tactical nuisance.
- **The Hollow Below:** cold absence, foreign predation, a gap where the mountain should be.

This distinction teaches that not every threat in Stillpeak is grief-born.
