# Scene Packet 7 Supplement — Bridgeward Way Discoveries

## Canon Status

This file is a canon treasure and discovery supplement for Scene Packet 7 / Chapter 7: Bridgeward Way.

It defines optional rewards, clue-bearing objects, and check-based discoveries for Bridgeward Threshold, Empty City Verge, Witness Halls, Queen's Bridge, and the Far-Side Mourning Threshold.

Packet 7 treasure should be symbolic, functional, and route-relevant. Avoid royal treasure hoards.

---

## Treasure Design Rule

Chapter 7 is not a loot chapter. Its main reward is understanding.

Good reward types:

- processional relics
- witness tokens
- mourning beads or cloth
- lantern charms
- bridge-route markers
- crystal residue from grief-crystal creatures
- small tools that help interpret grief-resonance or throneward route markers

Avoid:

- royal crowns, queen/king relics, or major regalia
- large coin piles
- items that reveal the final solution
- items that bypass the throneward descent or future king confrontation

## Featured Discoveries

### Bridgeward Route Chip

A small broken route stone found near the Bridgeward Threshold.

**Use:** Grants advantage once on a Dwarvish, Investigation, History, or Survival check to interpret Bridgeward or throneward route markers.

**Boundary:** It does not reveal the throne room location by itself.

---

### Witness Token

A palm-sized bronze token carried by formal witnesses during sacred/civic processions.

**Use:** If shown respectfully during a witness-spirit scene, it grants advantage once on Insight, Religion, Persuasion, or Dwarvish to interpret a memory-bound witness.

**Emotional Note:** The token should feel like proof of attendance, not currency.

---

### Veil Scrap

A brittle remnant of darkened cloth used to cover queenly imagery after the tragedy.

**Use:** Once, while holding the veil scrap during a memory echo, dangerous remembrance, or mourning-related effect, a character may gain advantage on one Wisdom saving throw, Religion check, or Insight check.

**Boundary:** It does not hide the party from the king and does not prevent the bridge memory from unfolding.

---

### Animus Crystal Residue

Powdery crystal residue left behind after the Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus collapses.

**Use:** A character proficient in Arcana, Religion, or relevant tools can preserve a small amount. Once, the residue grants advantage on an Arcana or Religion check to identify grief-crystal growths, distinguish grief-crystal creatures from foreign entities, or understand crystal resonance behavior.

**Attunement Note:** While carried, Erny and Maelreth may feel mild pressure behind the eyes near major crystal growths.

---

### Torn Procession Cloth

A strip of pale ceremonial fabric found on or beyond the Queen's Bridge.

**Use:** Grants advantage once on Insight, Religion, or History to interpret the queen's procession or immediate aftermath spirits.

**Narrative Use:** The cloth can tie the far-side spirit to the phrase: "Someone must tell him. Someone must survive telling him."

---

### Shardling Crystal Splinter

A jagged splinter left after a Crystal Shardling dies.

**Use:** Once, a character may use the splinter as a focus while making an Arcana or Religion check to understand grief-crystal shard activity, gaining advantage.

**Risk:** If Erny or Maelreth carries the splinter for more than one hour, they experience intrusive resonance pressure. No standing penalty unless the DM wants to foreshadow later danger.

---

### Throneward Route Marker Fragment

A chipped piece of worked stone from the Far-Side Mourning Threshold bearing a lower royal route sign.

**Use:** Grants advantage once on a future Dwarvish, Investigation, History, or Survival check to follow throneward route signs or distinguish royal/civic route markings from residential or evacuation routes.

**Boundary:** This points toward Chapter 8 but does not reveal the throne room directly.

## DC Guidance for Improvised Searching

Use these defaults:

- **DC 10:** Notice obvious ceremonial debris, broken lanterns, dead wicks, and route chips.
- **DC 12:** Read basic route markings or identify simple processional objects.
- **DC 13:** Find a useful symbolic token or route-relevant fragment.
- **DC 14:** Understand mourning etiquette, witness function, or sacred/civic procession logic.
- **DC 15:** Preserve crystal residue, identify subtle grief-resonance clues, or distinguish overlapping threat types.

Suggested skills:

- Investigation for searching.
- Perception for spotting partially hidden items.
- Dwarvish or History for route markers and civic use.
- Religion for mourning/processional significance.
- Arcana for crystal resonance and grief-crystal residue.
- Insight for spirit behavior and emotional function.

## Suggested Minimum Rewards

If running Chapter 7 quickly, guarantee three discoveries:

1. **Witness Token** in the Witness Halls.
2. **Animus Crystal Residue** after the Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus is defeated.
3. **Throneward Route Marker Fragment** at the Far-Side Mourning Threshold.

If the party engages strongly with the bridge memory, also offer:

- **Torn Procession Cloth**.

If the party is tactically thoughtful about lighting or route-reading, offer:

- **Lantern Hook Charm** or **Bridgeward Route Chip**.

---

## DM Caution

Do not overload Chapter 7 with treasure. The chapter is about recognition, not acquisition.

Avoid giving the party anything that:

- names the queen if that is not yet canonically established
- reveals the king's immortality
- reveals the final solution
- bypasses Chapter 8 route pressure
- turns spirits into controllable allies

The best Chapter 7 rewards help the party understand, endure, or navigate what comes next.
