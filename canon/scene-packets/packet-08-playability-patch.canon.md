## BEAT 1 Patch — Plainspoken Descent Cue

This patch supersedes overly elevated Beat 1 narration that makes the descent feel too formal or artificial.

### Replace / Prefer This Cue

Use after the party leaves the far side of Queen's Bridge and commits to the throneward route.

> The route ahead was built for people walking toward the throne.
>
> You can tell right away. The steps are wide. The walls are marked with royal signs. Even damaged, the place still tries to make you stand a little straighter and feel a little smaller.
>
> But the crystals have broken that order.
>
> They split the royal markings, push through the corners, and glow from places that were never meant to hold light.
>
> Behind you is the bridge where the queen fell.
>
> Ahead, the stone keeps pointing down.

### DM Note

Keep Beat 1 grounded. The party should feel royal ruin and pressure, but the spoken word should sound like a human DM explaining what the characters see.

## BEAT 2 Patch — Advisor Recognition Prop

Add this player-facing discovery to **Advisor Records**.

### Druid Advisor Seal Pair

Among the royal records are two palm-sized stone-and-bronze seal pieces set into a cracked wall niche. They are not treasure in the normal sense. They are office markers or ceremonial advisor seals.

- Dhurak's seal shows a split mountain line braced by a hammer-shaped fault mark.
- Veyra's seal shows branching roots descending into a crystal vein.

The seals match symbols that later appear on the bodies / beam anchors of Dhurak and Veyra in the King's Chamber.

### Suggested Checks

- **Investigation DC 14:** Notice that these two seals are more worn than the others, as if handled often or removed and replaced many times.
- **Perception DC 14:** Notice the same paired symbols repeating in the crystal growth around the chamber.
- **History DC 15:** Understand these were active court authority symbols, not memorial decorations.
- **Religion or Nature DC 15:** Recognize the symbols as Stone Druid authority marks tied to fault-reading and deep-root crystal interpretation.

### Success Result

On success, tell the party:

> These were not just names in a record. These were people with authority, access, and trust. If you see these marks again, you will know whose work you are looking at.

### Final Arena Payoff

If the party identified the seals in Beat 2, they automatically recognize Dhurak and Veyra's marks in the final arena. They may gain advantage on one check to identify a beam anchor, predict a Druid's role, or explain the Druid protection logic to the group.

## BEAT 4 Patch — Crystal Mouse Interlude

Add a small comic-horror beat to **Mining Decree / Greed Spiral** to break up the lore-heavy middle of the chapter.

### Crystal Mouse

While the party examines the king's decrees, a tiny crystal mouse emerges from a crack between two command tablets.

At first it looks almost cute: a little translucent mouse-shape made of green-white crystal, with oversized ears, twitching whiskers, and feet that click softly on stone.

It noses at a decree, squeaks once, and looks up at the party.

Then its jaw opens much too wide.

### Mechanics

This is not a real combat encounter unless the party escalates.

- The crystal mouse lunges at the nearest exposed boot, finger, tail, cloak edge, or dangling strap.
- Attack: automatic harmless nuisance or **+4 to hit**, DM choice.
- Hit: **1 piercing damage**.
- Miss: it skitters, bonks into a tablet, and leaves a tiny green scratch on the stone.
- AC 12, HP 1.
- If struck, stepped on, or otherwise damaged, it cracks apart with a tiny *tink* and releases no death burst.

### Table Purpose

The mouse should get a laugh, then immediately remind the table that even tiny life near the heart has been made wrong.

### Suggested Cue

> A tiny crystal mouse pokes its head out from behind one of the decree tablets.
>
> For one second, it is almost adorable.
>
> Then it opens its mouth wider than its whole body should allow and lunges at your boot.
>
> It deals 1 damage, mostly to your dignity.

### Optional Discovery

A character who examines the broken mouse with **Nature or Arcana DC 13** learns:

> This is not a natural creature trapped in crystal. It is crystal imitating a living thing because the chamber is close enough to the heart that the corruption is trying to make life badly.

## BEAT 5 Patch — Optional Session Break Point

The Implosion Threshold is a valid place to end a table night if time is running short.

### Preferred End-Night Point

End after:

1. the party understands the threshold evidence,
2. the Crystal Tremorborn / Shardlings encounter is resolved,
3. the green light drains toward the King's Chamber,
4. the party reaches the sealed royal doorway or final threshold breath.

Do not end before the lore lands. Do not end in the middle of the threshold fight unless unavoidable.

### End-Night Cue

> The last shard-body breaks apart.
>
> For a moment, the pieces lie still.
>
> Then their green light drains across the floor in thin lines, all flowing the same direction.
>
> Toward the sealed royal doorway ahead.
>
> Beyond it, something pulses once.
>
> Then again.
>
> The King's Chamber is on the other side.

### Next-Session Restart Cue

Use at the beginning of the next session if the boss fight is held for a later date.

> You stand at the final royal doorway.
>
> Behind you are the records, the decrees, and the burst threshold that showed you what Stillpeak became.
>
> Ahead is the source.
>
> The green light under the door pulses slowly, like a heart that has forgotten it should have stopped long ago.

### DM Runtime Note

This pause point is useful if the table has less than 90 minutes remaining. The final boss encounter deserves a fresh mental runway.

## BEAT 6 Patch — Temporary Inert Attunement Option

The default final-arena rule remains: Erny and Maelreth are over-attuned while the beams remain active.

However, the DM may allow a temporary inert-attunement window if the party combines the right protective factors. This gives the group a heroic counterplay option without nullifying the affliction system.

### Name

**Grounded Silence**

### Trigger Conditions

Grounded Silence may occur if the party creates a clear release-focused grounding moment using at least **two** of the following factors:

1. A valid mourning/restraint relic: **Soot-Black Prayer Bead**, **Torn White Procession Cloth**, or **Queen's Lantern Charm**.
2. An ally spends an action to steady Erny or Maelreth using the ally counterplay rules.
3. Erny or Maelreth willingly gives up offense for the round and focuses on resisting the beam.
4. The party has already broken one Druid beam.
5. A character clearly frames the act as release, restraint, or letting go rather than restoration, revenge, or control.
6. A successful **Religion, Arcana, Persuasion, or Medicine DC 15** check anchors the moment.

### Effect

Choose one over-attuned character, or both if the table needs a heroic reset and the party spent meaningful action economy.

Until the start of that character's next turn:

- they are not required to make the Final Attunement Crisis save,
- their next offensive action does not trigger full Attuned Offense Backlash,
- if backlash would trigger, reduce it to the one-beam weakened version or suppress it entirely at DM discretion,
- they may act with clear agency and no movement-pull consequence.

Grounded Silence should not last more than one round unless both beams are already broken.

### Limits

- This is not a permanent cure.
- It cannot be spammed for free.
- It should cost actions, relic use, positioning, or a meaningful tactical choice.
- It is strongest after one beam has already broken.
- Once both beams break, normal over-attunement crisis ends as already defined in canon.

### Suggested Cue

> For one breath, the green light loses its grip on you.
>
> The pressure is still there. The grief is still there.
>
> But it is outside you again.
>
> You can choose what you do next.

## Final Boss Round Script — DM Runtime Aid

The DM Runtime Project should read this section before running the King's Chamber.

This is not a rigid initiative script. Use it as a pacing guide so the final fight remains readable.

### Before Initiative

Goals:

- Make the arena shape clear.
- Make the King-as-vessel clear.
- Make Druid protection clear.
- Make the beams obvious.

Must say plainly:

- The beams burn **into** the King.
- Dhurak and Veyra are protecting him, not obeying him.
- The King is fastened to the throne and does not rise.

### Round 1 — Teach the Room

Use Round 1 to show the rules, not to overwhelm the party.

Recommended beats:

1. Dhurak demonstrates tremor / bowl movement pressure.
2. Veyra demonstrates root restraint or beam-preservation pressure.
3. Erny and Maelreth feel the first full over-attunement pulse.
4. If one of them attacks, trigger Attuned Offense Backlash once, then explain the pattern through narration.

DM reminder:

> The first backlash should feel like discovery, not punishment.

### Round 2 — Reward Pattern Recognition

The party should now understand:

- target the Druids / beams,
- the King cannot be cleanly resolved yet,
- Erny and Maelreth need support or restraint,
- the bowl matters but should not dominate every movement.

Recommended beats:

1. Let focused attacks meaningfully damage a Druid.
2. Use only one major recharge-style boss pressure if possible.
3. Offer an ally counterplay opening for Erny or Maelreth.
4. If the party identified Beat 2 advisor seals, let that recognition help them read which Druid is doing what.

### When the First Beam Breaks

Pause for a clear momentum shift.

Say plainly:

> One beam snaps.
>
> The King jerks forward in the throne.
>
> The pressure on Erny and Maelreth eases, but it does not end.
>
> The surviving Druid turns more fully toward the King, like it is afraid of losing him.

Mechanical reminders:

- Final Attunement Crisis DC drops from 15 to 13.
- Psychic damage option drops from 2d6 to 1d6.
- Attuned Offense Backlash weakens or becomes discretionary.
- The King protection weakens.

### Middle Rounds — Keep It Moving

Do:

- make Druid damage matter,
- use Grounded Silence if the party earns it,
- remind players that the beams are the fight's visible logic,
- soften optional hazards if the party is already struggling.

Do not:

- add minions,
- overuse bowl-slide checks,
- trigger backlash on every Eldritch Blast beam,
- run Dhurak/Veyra as normal Stone Golem multiattack bags,
- let the King become a new full boss phase.

### When the Second Beam Breaks

Stop the combat engine immediately.

Say plainly:

> The second beam breaks.
>
> The chamber goes quiet.
>
> The King takes one real breath.
>
> For the first time, he looks like a man trapped in the middle of what he helped make.

Mechanical reminders:

- Druid interception ends.
- Attuned Offense Backlash ends.
- Final Attunement Crisis ends.
- The King enters the clarity window.
- Shift to mercy / relic resolution.

### Clarity Window

Do not monologue.

Use two to four short King lines from existing canon, then ask what the party does.

If the party stalls too long, use the refusal/delay pressure cue from `packet-08-ending-execution.canon.md`.

### Ending / Aftermath

After mercy death or successful relic release:

1. Use the plainspoken release cue from `packet-08-ending-style-and-relic-patch.canon.md`.
2. Allow inspection of the King's body and throne.
3. Reveal the First Green Heart pendant.
4. If the party identifies it, use `packet-08-first-green-heart-artifact.canon.md`.
5. Let the final emotional note be sad satisfaction, not triumph.
