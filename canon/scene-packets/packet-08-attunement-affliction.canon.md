# Scene Packet 8 Supplement — Final Attunement Affliction

## Canon Status

This file defines the final-act mountain attunement pressure for Scene Packet 8 / Chapter 8: Throneward Descent.

It applies primarily to **Erny** and **Maelreth**, who are mountain-attuned.

Use with:

- `packet-08-throneward-descent.canon.md`
- `packet-08-final-encounter.canon.md`
- `packet-08-monsters.canon.md`
- `packet-08-treasure-discoveries.canon.md`

---

## Design Purpose

The affliction should nearly cripple Erny and Maelreth in the final boss arena, but it must remain interactive.

They should feel uniquely vulnerable because their attunement makes them receptive to:

- the Mourning King's grief
- Dhurak and Veyra's Abbathor-touched crystal corruption
- Stillpeak's wounded resonance
- the green sustaining beams
- the crystal boss arena itself

The goal is not to bench two players. The goal is to make the party actively protect, steady, and manage them during the final battle.

---

## Over-Attunement State

Inside the King's Chamber / Crystal Boss Arena, Erny and Maelreth become **over-attuned**.

Symptoms:

- pressure behind the eyes
- teeth vibrating in time with the green beams
- grief that feels borrowed but physically real
- flashes of the queen falling, the king refusing, and stone roots opening
- intrusive certainty that the throne is calling them
- physical pull toward beams, crystal nodes, or the king
- offensive power feeling like it plugs directly into the geode chamber

---

## Final Attunement Crisis — Baseline Rule

While both green beams remain active, Erny and Maelreth suffer the following:

- Disadvantage on saving throws against the Mourning King's grief-resonance effects.
- Disadvantage on saving throws against Dhurak or Veyra's crystal-resonance effects.
- At initiative count 20 each round, each over-attuned character must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**.
- Offensive actions against bosses may trigger **Attuned Offense Backlash** as defined in `packet-08-final-encounter.canon.md`.

On a failure, choose one:

1. Take 7 (2d6) psychic damage.
2. Lose reactions until the start of the character's next turn.
3. The first 10 feet of movement on the character's next turn must be toward the nearest active beam, crystal node, or the king.
4. The character has disadvantage on the first attack roll or ability check made before the end of the next turn.

On a success, the character resists the pulse and may sense one useful tactical truth about the arena, beam, or boss behavior.

---

## Attuned Offense Backlash Summary

This is the final-arena escalation of the earlier shaft behavior.

While at least one green beam remains active, if **Erny** or **Maelreth** uses an offensive action, attack, damaging spell, smite, or damaging class feature against any final-arena boss, the geode chamber reacts after the offense resolves.

Default summary:

- Trigger at most once per round.
- All crystals in the geode flash white-green.
- The bowl tremors.
- Crystal shards release from the jagged geode shell overhead.
- All party members make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.
- Failure: 7 (2d6) piercing/force damage.
- Success: half damage.
- Creatures on slopes may also need a bowl-slope slide check if position matters.

Beam stage adjustment:

- **Both beams active:** full backlash applies.
- **One beam active:** backlash may be reduced to 3 (1d6), limited to exposed areas, or used only when dramatically appropriate.
- **Both beams broken:** Attuned Offense Backlash ends.

This mechanic should create a meaningful choice, not forbid Erny and Maelreth from participating. Their offense still matters, but while the beams remain active, the room punishes attuned aggression because their resonance is tied directly into the same system sustaining the king.

---

## Beam Weakening Stages

### Both Beams Active

Full Final Attunement Crisis applies.

The king is heavily sustained.

Erny and Maelreth are at maximum pressure.

Attuned Offense Backlash applies at full strength.

### One Beam Broken

When either Dhurak or Veyra is destroyed and one beam breaks:

- Final Attunement Crisis DC drops from 15 to 13.
- Psychic damage option drops from 7 (2d6) to 3 (1d6).
- Erny and Maelreth no longer suffer automatic disadvantage against the destroyed Druid's effects.
- The king's protection weakens.
- Attuned Offense Backlash weakens or becomes discretionary.

### Both Beams Broken

When both Dhurak and Veyra are destroyed:

- Recurring Final Attunement Crisis ends.
- Attuned Offense Backlash ends.
- Erny and Maelreth may still feel echoes, but they are no longer mechanically pressured every round.
- The Mourning King enters the clarity window.

## Ally Counterplay

Any ally may use an action to steady an over-attuned character within 5 feet.

Suggested checks:

- **Persuasion DC 14:** Call them back by name or anchor them emotionally.
- **Medicine DC 14:** Ground them physically through breathing, pain response, or posture.
- **Religion DC 15:** Invoke mourning restraint, release, or anti-possession language.
- **Arcana DC 15:** Disrupt the resonance pattern around them.
- **Athletics DC 13:** Physically hold them back from a pull effect.

On success, the over-attuned character gains one of the following:

- Advantage on their next Final Attunement Crisis save.
- Immediate end of one current movement-pull effect.
- Resistance to the next psychic damage instance before the end of their next turn.
- Ability to reroll one failed save against a beam/crystal effect before the end of their next turn.
- Permission for the next offensive act by that character to trigger reduced backlash or no backlash, at DM discretion, if the table needs a heroic push.

On failure, the attempt does not worsen the condition unless the DM wants to escalate pressure dramatically.

---

## Relic Counterplay

The following relics provide counterplay against over-attunement in the final arena:

1. **Soot-Black Prayer Bead**
2. **Torn White Procession Cloth**
3. **Queen's Lantern Charm**

The Queen's Lantern Charm is valid for attunement/fear counterplay only. It is not a valid slim-salvation relic.

A character using one of these relics may spend an action to reduce pressure on one over-attuned character.

Suggested effect:

- The target automatically succeeds on the next Final Attunement Crisis save.
- Or the target clears one affliction consequence immediately.
- Or the target gains advantage on all saves against the king's grief-resonance until the end of the next round.
- Or the target's next offensive act triggers reduced Attuned Offense Backlash, not full backlash.

Do not make relic usage a complicated puzzle. It should be powerful but limited by action economy, proximity, or one-use resonance.

---

## Failure Tone

Affliction failures should feel intrusive rather than punitive.

Good failure narration:

- The throne breathes through your ribs.
- You know the queen is gone, but your body wants to walk toward the lie that she can return.
- The green beam hums in your teeth like a second heartbeat.
- For one instant, the king's grief feels like your own memory.
- Your power lands, but the room answers like you struck every crystal at once.

Avoid:

- long forced-control scenes
- repeated lost turns
- player removal from the finale
- unclear penalties that feel arbitrary
- backlash on every individual attack roll or Eldritch Blast beam

---

## Design Boundary

This system is meant for the final boss arena unless later Packet 8 design explicitly extends it.

Earlier Chapter 8 beats may foreshadow the affliction with headaches, false certainty, intrusive flashes, or resonance pressure, but should not fully cripple Erny and Maelreth before the final encounter.
