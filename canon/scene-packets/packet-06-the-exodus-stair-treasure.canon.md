# Scene Packet 6 Treasure Supplement — Exodus Stair Discoveries

## Canon Status

This file is a canon treasure and discovery supplement for `canon/scene-packets/packet-06-the-exodus-stair.canon.md`.

It covers treasure, useful items, and check-based discoveries that are found through exploration, investigation, respectful interaction, or clever use of the environment.

Monster-kill treasure belongs in `packet-06-the-exodus-stair-hostile-encounters.canon.md`, not this file.

---

## Treasure Design Rule

Packet 6 treasure should feel like recovered dwarven emergency infrastructure, survivor keepsakes, survey tools, or salvageable practical equipment.

Avoid large coin piles or conventional dungeon hoards. The Exodus Stair was an evacuation route, not a treasury.

Good treasure types:

- dwarven route tokens
- emergency lamps
- chalks, wedges, seal tools, pitons, and climbing aids
- survivor tags and family marks
- minor protective keepsakes
- useful nonmagical salvage
- one or two low-power magic or semi-magical tools tied to resonance

Avoid:

- royal treasure
- queen/king relics
- major lore-revealing artifacts
- items that solve Bridgeward Way too early

## Featured Discoveries

### Weather-Seal Wedge

A squat wedge of dark dwarven alloy found near the summit door's inner threshold.

**Use:** As an action, a character may place the wedge into a door seam, lift track, pressure groove, or unstable stone slot. The next check to brace, hold, jam, or stabilize that mechanism has advantage.

**Limit:** One meaningful use before the wedge bends or becomes lodged.

**DM Note:** This item rewards players who think structurally. It should not open locked doors by itself.

---

### Emergency Lantern Casing

A dented but intact dwarven lantern frame with a cracked crystal lens and a wind-shielded oil chamber.

**Use:** With oil and 10 minutes of repair, it functions as a hooded lantern. It is unusually resistant to drafts and does not gutter in moderate wind or moving air.

**Extra Use:** In areas with cursed seam-glow, the lantern's ordinary flame helps distinguish real color from resonance-distorted color. Once in Packet 6 or 7, it may grant advantage on a Perception or Investigation check involving lighting, shadows, or false seam-glow.

**Value:** 25 gp to a collector or dwarven craftsperson.

---

### Bronze Family Token

A small bronze token bearing a family mark from the survivor slate.

**Use:** If presented respectfully to a nonhostile exodus spirit, the token grants advantage once on a Persuasion, Religion, Insight, or Dwarvish check to calm, communicate with, or interpret that spirit.

**Boundary:** The token does not command spirits and does not prove the bearer is a descendant.

---

### Slate Child-Tag

A palm-sized slate tag with a drilled cord-hole and a hand-cut family symbol. It belonged to a child evacuee.

**Use:** If shown to the Child Under the Bench or another frightened exodus spirit, it can anchor the conversation and grant advantage once on a gentle social or religious check.

**Emotional Note:** This should feel weighty, not like loot.

---

### Pressure-Seal Prybar

A short dwarven prybar with a hooked end designed for pressure-seal grooves.

**Use:** Grants advantage once on an Athletics, Investigation, or mason's tools check to shift a stuck pressure door, pry open a maintenance panel, or brace a seal mechanism.

**Combat Use:** Counts as an improvised weapon, but using it this way risks bending it and losing its special use.

---

### Soot-Black Prayer Bead

A smooth blackened bead found near the prayer niche. It is warm for one breath when lifted, then cold.

**Use:** Once, while holding the bead during a memory echo or fear/grief effect, a character may gain advantage on a Wisdom saving throw, Religion check, or Insight check tied to mourning residue.

**Boundary:** It does not protect against the king directly and does not reveal the queen's death.

---

### Surveyor's Chain Link

A loose engraved chain link from the Chain-Lift Void, marked with dwarven load and anchor runes.

**Use:** When combined with Resonance Chalk, it grants advantage once to identify a stable anchor point, safe chain segment, or load-bearing stone feature.

**Future Hook:** In Chapter 7, it may help interpret bridge machinery or weight-distribution rules, but it should not bypass major hazards.

---

### Route Token: Bridgeward

A bronze route token stamped with the old Dwarvish word for Bridgeward.

**Use:** Grants advantage once on a Dwarvish, Investigation, History, or Survival check to follow Bridgeward route markings, distinguish official route signage from later warning marks, or identify the correct descent path.

**DM Note:** This is the cleanest Packet 6 endpoint reward. It points forward without solving Chapter 7.

## Suggested Minimum Treasure for Tonight

If running Packet 6 quickly, place at least three tangible discoveries:

1. **Resonance Chalk** in the Last Breath Refuge.
2. **Emergency Lantern Casing** or **Weather-Seal Wedge** near the door/refuge.
3. **Route Token: Bridgeward** at the Exodus Landing.

If the party engages strongly with spirits, add one emotional token:

- **Bronze Family Token** from the Ledger Keeper, or
- **Slate Child-Tag** from the Child Under the Bench.

If the party plays tactically around the shaft, add one practical tool:

- **Surveyor's Chain Link**, or
- **Pressure-Seal Prybar**.

## DC Guidance for Improvised Searching

Use these defaults when the party searches areas not explicitly covered:

- **DC 10:** Notice obvious salvage, broken lamp hardware, dead hooks, scattered tags, or discarded wedges.
- **DC 12:** Find usable mundane equipment among debris.
- **DC 13:** Identify an object as dwarven emergency infrastructure rather than junk.
- **DC 14:** Recover an object without damaging it or understand its likely use.
- **DC 15:** Find a route-relevant or future-useful tool hidden among clutter.

Suggested skills:

- Investigation for searching and sorting.
- Perception for spotting hidden or partially buried objects.
- Dwarvish or History for identifying route tokens, family marks, or civic use.
- Arcana or Religion for resonance-sensitive objects.
- Athletics or Sleight of Hand for extracting wedged or fragile objects.

---

## DM Caution

Do not overload Packet 6 with treasure. The chapter's reward is entry, recovery, orientation, and the first clear interior truth.

Treasure should support the next phase rather than distract from it.

The best items are modest tools that make players feel clever later.
