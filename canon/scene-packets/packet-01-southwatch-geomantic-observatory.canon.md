## 2. Narrative Setup (Canonical Text)

**Stillpeak’s Grieving Heart**

A bleak, survival-driven D&D campaign set in the eastern Spine of the World, south of Icewind Dale.

**Core conceit:** grief has become geological. Stillpeak’s heart pulses with sorrow, and the land responds—first with stillness, then with cold, then with the dead who do not fall.

**Design commitments:**
- Encumbrance matters.
- Altitude and cold matter.
- Extradimensional storage becomes inert beyond the Karagvorn Line.
- The Tremorscope remains the only reliable guide where other orientation tools and spells drift.

### Key Terms

**Stillpeak:** The cursed mountain whose summit fissure leads into a hollow core.

**Karagvorn Line ("Stone-Sorrow Line"):** A dwarven term for the invisible boundary where reality subtly reinterprets itself.

**Tremorscope:** A lantern-like seismograph that displays deep-earth movement and vibrates within 5 miles of a tremor source. It always guides toward the ultimate source.

### Scene Purpose

Establish the observatory as credible authority and set the bleak tone through restrained urgency.

Make the party’s delivery of the hunter’s journal the direct catalyst for the adventure (no coincidence).

Introduce the Tremorscope and the idea that the mountain’s pulse is deliberate, mournful, and spreading.

Foreshadow the Karagvorn Line and the broader mythos of emotionally resonant mountains.

### Prelude — The Hunter’s Final Journey (Party-Directed Delivery)

Two nights ago, a lone hunter staggered into a roadside lodge south of the Spine of the World. His skin was pale with frostburn, his breath shallow, his eyes wide with the kind of terror only the high wilds can carve. Before collapsing, he seized one of the characters (or a trusted courier who immediately sought the party out) and pressed a battered journal into their hands. The leather cover was stiff with ice, the pages swollen from snowmelt.

With the last strength in his body, he whispered:

> "Southwatch… take it to the geomancers… they’ll understand the lines… before the mountain stops us all…"

Then he died. The lodgekeeper explains the hunter returned from the lower passes near Cinderwatch, muttering about shadows that froze in place and a mountain that “beat like a wounded heart.” The journal was meant for the geomancers alone; the party is the intended courier by explicit request.

### Opening Scene — Arrival & The Journal Is Read

Snow clings to the stone ramparts of the Southwatch Geomantic Observatory as the party ascends the final steps. The wind at this height carries a hollow, distant moan—echoes rolling down from the Spine like a warning whispered through ice.

Inside, the observatory’s vaulted hall glows with lamplight reflected off brass instruments and sprawling seismic charts. Scholars in heavy robes pause their work as the party enters, their attention drawn immediately to the frost-stiffened journal carried in the characters’ hands.

Elder Geomancer Orven Duskslate steps forward. His expression tightens when he recognizes the hunter’s sigil carved into the journal’s leather.

Orven opens the journal. Pages crackle like thin ice. Jagged sketches fill the entries—mountain silhouettes warped by fear, empty treelines, and trembling lines that match the very seismic patterns the observatory has been recording for months.

He clears his throat and reads aloud:

> "The stillness follows me. The trees whisper nothing. The dead do not fall. The mountain cries through the stone."

Closing the journal, Orven looks to the party. "This hunter felt the same rhythm we’ve been tracking for three months. Whatever he fled from did not let him go easily. We must understand what woke beneath Stillpeak—and why it mourns."

### NPC — Orven Duskslate (Elder Geomancer)

**Race:** Dwarf  
**Role:** Observatory lead

**Personality:** Calm, analytical, quietly haunted; distrusts hasty conclusions

**Motivation:** Understand and stabilize the pulse before the region collapses into panic and death

**DM cue:** Orven speaks of the mountain like a person in pain; he avoids melodrama but cannot hide concern

### Key Props

#### The Hunter’s Journal

- Frantic sketches of the pass, rough altitude notes, and repeated references to ‘the stillness.’
- Several pages show upright figures with dark smears beneath them—shadows frozen into snow.
- Final entries become less coherent; linework trembles as though written while the ground pulsed.

#### The Tremorscope

Orven presents a lantern-sized device of blackened steel and copper ribs. At its center, a shard of volcanic glass is suspended by rune-etched prongs. When held or set down, faint jagged lines etch themselves across the glass—pulsing in real time with movements deep beneath the earth. When within 5 miles of a significant tremor source, it emits a slow, rhythmic vibration that intensifies as the party approaches the origin. If the source is stationary, the vibration subtly ‘leans’ the lantern in that direction.

The Tremorscope offers no protection and cannot predict tremors precisely. It merely listens—quietly, urgently—to whatever the earth is trying to say.

### Running This Scene — Skill Checks & Mechanical Beats

Use these checks to reward curiosity and give players actionable understanding. None are required to proceed.

#### Entering the Observatory

- **Passive Perception 13+:** A character notices chart lines on the wall that match the journal’s strokes exactly.
- **Perception (DC 12):** Feel a faint vibration beneath the boots—rhythmic, not random.
- **Nature (DC 13):** Tremors are not seasonal, weather-driven, or typical glacial settling.

#### Analyzing the Journal

- **Investigation (DC 14):** The hunter’s sketches increase in frequency and intensity closer to Stillpeak.
- **Insight (DC 12):** The handwriting shows panic and an eerie empathy, as though the hunter felt sorrow pressing down.
- **Arcana (DC 15):** No classic necromancy; instead, sympathetic resonance—emotional residue tied to the mountain.

#### Dialogue With Orven

- **Persuasion (DC 12):** Orven shares older seismic logs showing the moment the pattern began (3 months back).
- **History (DC 13):** Connect local events to broader records of strange mountain behavior in other regions.

#### Tremorscope Demonstration

- **Arcana or Tinker’s Tools (DC 15):** The Tremorscope is a passive listener; it amplifies what is already there.
- **Insight (DC 12):** Orven is frightened beneath professionalism; he believes the region is on the edge of something irreversible.

### Foreshadowing the Karagvorn Line (Wider Mythology)

Use these checks to make the ‘mountain grief’ premise feel like part of wider Realms folklore, not a one-off convenience.

- **Religion (DC 14):** Dwarven cosmology frames mountains as ‘stone-hearts’ that can mourn when their roots are disturbed; tales of ‘Weeping Peaks’ and rumbling sorrow in distant ranges.
- **History (DC 13):** Legends across Faerûn—Hotenow’s fury tied to buried spirit-wars; Cloud Peaks ‘Hushed Crags’; Untheric myths of mountains that roar, weep, or sleep.
- **Arcana (DC 12):** ‘Geo-empathic loci’ where ley lines and ancient spirits overlap; extradimensional magic destabilizes, memories linger in stone, emotion can shape reality.
- **Nature (DC 14):** Druidic traditions describe mountains as slow-thinking beings with moods; stories of ridges pulsing before rockfalls, cliffs groaning after landslides.
- **Insight (DC 12):** The journal reads like fear braided with empathy—the hunter felt sorrow leaking into his mind.

### Scene End — Mission Assignment

Orven commissions the party to travel through the Cold Pass to Cinderwatch and verify the source and spread of the affliction. He provides the Tremorscope and modest supplies. He asks for observations, sketches, and any confirmation of how the pulse correlates with the dead who do not fall.
