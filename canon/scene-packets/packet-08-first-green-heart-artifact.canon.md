# Scene Packet 8 Supplement — The First Green Heart Artifact

## Canon Status

This supplement is canon for Scene Packet 8 / Chapter 8: Throneward Descent.

It defines the artifact-style statistics, table handling, risks, and epilogue uses for **The First Green Heart**, the green crystal pendant recovered from the Mourning King after his death or full release.

## Item Card

**The First Green Heart**  
*Wondrous item, artifact, requires attunement by a creature that participated in the release of the Mourning King*

A thumb-sized green crystal pendant set on a blackened royal chain. It feels too heavy for its size. Deep inside it, green light moves slowly, as if something powerful has gone dormant but not dead.

This is the original source crystal: the first great Abbathor-touched reservoir of power found beneath Stillpeak. Dhurak, Veyra, and the King failed to understand it, then failed to fear it, then used it until it consumed the mountain.

It is not merely valuable. It is dangerous history made portable.

---

## Table-Facing Identification

A character can identify the broad truth of the pendant without a roll after the final release if they followed the Chapter 8 lore:

- this is the first source crystal
- it predates the Queen's death
- the Druids and King built the later catastrophe around this kind of power
- it is not safe to treat as normal treasure

A check can clarify risks and possible uses:

- **Arcana DC 16:** This crystal contains dormant city-scale resonance energy.
- **Religion DC 16:** The power carries Abbathor-touched greed and preservation instinct, even after the King is released.
- **Nature DC 16:** The crystal is no longer actively poisoning Stillpeak, but it still wants to root into stone, memory, and desire.
- **Insight DC 18:** The true danger is not that the crystal whispers evil commands. The danger is that it makes terrible ideas feel necessary.

On a failure, give the player only this: *It is powerful, old, and not safe to use casually.*

---

## Attunement

Only a creature that witnessed or participated in the King's release can safely attempt attunement.

Attunement requires 1 hour of quiet contact with the pendant. During that hour, the creature experiences fragments of Stillpeak's history: miners finding the first vein, Druids calling it a gift, the King touching the pendant after the Queen's fall, and the mountain slowly learning to hurt.

At the end of attunement, the bearer must make a **DC 18 Wisdom saving throw**.

- **Success:** The bearer attunes safely and understands that the artifact is a burden, not a prize.
- **Failure:** The bearer still attunes, but gains the flaw: *I can fix what others are too weak to face.* This flaw lasts until the bearer willingly gives up the artifact, receives a greater restoration, or completes a major act of renunciation.

A creature can choose not to attune after experiencing the memories.

## Dormant Properties

While attuned to the First Green Heart, the bearer gains the following benefits.

### Mountain Memory

While underground, inside a mountain, or within major worked stone, the bearer has advantage on one **History, Nature, Religion, or Survival** check per day related to ancient stonework, mining scars, buried ruins, magical resonance, or grief-haunted places.

This does not create a map. It gives emotional direction: pressure, dread, old damage, sealed places, or something that should have been left alone.

### Heart of the Deep Stone

The bearer gains resistance to force damage and thunder damage.

If the bearer already has resistance to one of those damage types, the DM may instead allow advantage on saving throws against magical tremors, forced movement caused by earth/stone effects, or collapsing-stone hazards.

### Voice in the Veins

The bearer can cast **commune with nature** once, without material components, but only underground, in mountains, or in major stone ruins. When cast this way, the spell communicates through pressure, warmth, memory, and vibration rather than words.

Once used, this property cannot be used again until the next dawn.

## Mythic Property — The Sleeping Reservoir

The First Green Heart contains enough dormant power to affect a city, mountain, or kingdom-scale magical structure.

This power should not be used like a normal spell.

Once, and only once, the bearer may awaken the artifact for a mythic act. Choose one of the following outcomes, subject to DM approval:

1. **Seal a Catastrophic Wound:** Close, stabilize, or suppress a mountain-scale magical rupture, planar crack, corrupted crystal heart, or collapsing ancient engine.
2. **Preserve a People for One Night:** Protect a settlement, army, or fleeing population from an otherwise unsurvivable environmental disaster for up to 8 hours.
3. **Break a Great Engine:** Destroy or permanently disable a city-scale magical machine, resonance network, false immortality engine, or comparable structure.
4. **Restore Breath to the Buried:** Rescue or stabilize living creatures trapped within a major collapse, sealed ruin, or petrified/encased stone disaster, provided they are not long dead.
5. **Bury It Forever:** Use the artifact's own power to seal itself beyond ordinary mortal reach, ending its usable existence unless a future campaign deliberately reopens it.

The mythic act requires a group skill challenge, not a single roll.

Suggested challenge: **6 successes before 3 failures, DC 18**.

Valid skills depend on the chosen act, but may include Arcana, Religion, Nature, History, Persuasion, Survival, Athletics, Mason's Tools, Smith's Tools, or another justified proficiency.

At least one success must come from a character choosing restraint, sacrifice, or release over control.

---

## Failure and Cost

The First Green Heart should be powerful enough to matter and dangerous enough to fear.

If the mythic skill challenge fails, choose one or more consequences:

- the intended effect happens only partially
- the artifact cracks and releases a dangerous pulse
- the bearer takes 8d10 force/thunder damage, ignoring resistance
- nearby stone becomes unstable or magically scarred
- the bearer gains a permanent flaw tied to certainty, preservation, greed, or control
- an outside power notices the artifact's use
- the artifact survives, but its next use will be worse

Do not use failure as an arbitrary instant party wipe. The cost should be serious, memorable, and morally pointed.

---

## Temptation Rule

Whenever the bearer tries to use the First Green Heart to preserve, restore, control, or empower something that should naturally end, the DM may call for a **DC 18 Wisdom saving throw**.

On a failure, the bearer does not lose control of their character, but the DM should tell them plainly:

> For one second, this feels like the right thing to do. Not easy. Not selfish. Necessary.

The player may still choose freely. The point is temptation, not mind control.

---

## Combat Use Guidance

Do not build the First Green Heart as a repeatable combat item unless the campaign unexpectedly continues.

If the DM wants one last over-the-top combat use before the campaign ends, use this limited option:

### Green Heart Pulse

As an action, the attuned bearer raises the pendant and releases a controlled pulse of dormant resonance.

Choose one effect:

- **Crushing Resonance:** One creature the bearer can see within 120 feet makes a **DC 18 Constitution saving throw**, taking 10d10 force damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.
- **Still the Collapse:** Up to six creatures within 60 feet gain 50 temporary hit points and cannot be knocked prone, pushed, pulled, or buried by stone or crystal effects for 1 minute.
- **Break the Engine:** A magical object, construct, crystal engine, or resonance structure within 120 feet takes 100 force damage, ignoring damage thresholds and resistances unless it is an artifact.

After using Green Heart Pulse, the bearer suffers one level of exhaustion and must make the Temptation Rule saving throw.

This pulse can be used once. Afterward, the artifact becomes dormant for one year unless the DM chooses otherwise.

---

## Destruction / Safe Disposal

The First Green Heart cannot be destroyed by ordinary force, dispel magic, remove curse, lava, mundane tools, or simple spell damage.

Known safe endings:

- willingly bury it beneath Stillpeak after the mountain has released its grief
- give it to a trusted divine, druidic, or geomantic order sworn never to use it
- use its mythic property to seal itself away
- carry it into a place where greed and preservation have no purchase, at the DM's discretion

Known unsafe endings:

- selling it
- giving it to a ruler
- giving it to miners, artificers, ambitious priests, or desperate scholars
- using it to resurrect, preserve, clone, bind, or restore the dead
- using it to power a kingdom

## DM Read-Aloud — First Inspection

Use when the party removes or studies the pendant after the King's release.

> The pendant is smaller than you expected.
>
> A green crystal, about the size of a thumb, hanging from a blackened royal chain.
>
> But when it rests in your hand, it feels heavier than gold.
>
> The light inside it moves slowly.
>
> Not brightly. Not violently.
>
> Just enough to make you understand that this thing is not empty.
>
> After everything you have seen here, the truth is hard to miss.
>
> This is not one more shard from the mountain.
>
> This is the first one.

## DM Read-Aloud — Understanding the Danger

Use after a successful identification check.

> You understand why they kept it.
>
> That may be the worst part.
>
> It is power that feels like an answer. Heat in the dark. Light under stone. Enough strength to hold back loss for one more day.
>
> And that is exactly how Stillpeak died.

---

## Campaign-End Handling

Because the current party is not expected to continue after this adventure, the First Green Heart may be treated as a mythic loot drop and epilogue object rather than a balanced long-term item.

If the players want to keep it, let them. Make the choice feel heavy.

If they want to destroy, bury, surrender, or seal it, treat that as a meaningful final epilogue decision.

If they want to use it as proof, trophy, bargaining chip, or power source, make clear that the story has already shown where that path leads.
