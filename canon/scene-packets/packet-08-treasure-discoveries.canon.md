# Scene Packet 8 Supplement — Throneward Discoveries and Relics

## Canon Status

This file defines Chapter 8 discovery categories, lore-bearing objects, and queen-linked relic logic.

It is a draft canon supplement for Scene Packet 8 / Chapter 8: Throneward Descent.

## Reward Philosophy

Chapter 8 rewards should be symbolic, useful, and lore-bearing.

Do not place ordinary treasure as the chapter's main payoff. The final act is about understanding, release, and survival.

Useful discoveries should help the party:

- understand Dhurak and Veyra's betrayal
- understand the king's culpability
- recognize the beam mechanic
- reduce attunement pressure
- make the mercy-death ending emotionally clear

The slim salvation path should remain secondary. Relics may make it possible, but Chapter 8 should not over-signal salvation as the expected or intended ending.

---

## Queen-Linked Mourning / Restraint Relics

The slim salvation route is possible but intentionally narrow.

Only the following relics count as valid queen-linked mourning/restraint relics for the slim salvation path:

1. **Soot-Black Prayer Bead**
2. **Torn White Procession Cloth**
3. **Queen's Lantern Charm**

A valid relic symbolizes mourning without possession — grief that honors the queen instead of dragging her back.

Do not treat unrelated beads, improvised objects, ordinary treasure, or later undefined relics as valid salvation relics unless the user explicitly revises this canon later.

The Soot-Black Prayer Bead is the strongest and clearest relic. Torn White Procession Cloth is an unavoidable but low-emphasis Chapter 8 discovery, present because it belongs to the tragedy rather than because the module is steering the party toward salvation. Queen's Lantern Charm remains optional.

## Discovery 1 — Stone Druid Advisor Tablet

**Location:** Advisor Records.

**Description:** A stone tablet naming Dhurak Stonevein and Veyra Deeproot as the king's trusted Stone Druid advisors.

**Lore:** They were high-trust confidants, not outsiders.

**Possible Use:** Grants advantage on one later check to interpret Dhurak/Veyra lore, identify their monster forms, or understand beam roles.

---

## Discovery 2 — Abbathor-Touched Crystal Fragment

**Location:** Druid-Centric Reveal chamber.

**Description:** A small green-black crystal shard veined with metallic gold flecks. It feels valuable in a way that makes the hand reluctant to release it.

**Lore:** The crystal corruption is linked to greed, hidden power, and Abbathor's influence.

**Possible Use:** A character who succeeds on **Religion or Arcana DC 15** may use the shard to recognize the green beams in the final chamber as the same corruption scaled to catastrophic size.

**Risk:** Carrying it too long may cause intrusive greed, possessiveness, or false certainty. Keep this as flavor unless later mechanics are desired.

---

## Discovery 3 — The King's Mining Decree

**Location:** Mining Decree / Greed Spiral.

**Description:** A royal tablet or scroll-slate bearing the king's seal and escalating orders to mine deeper, preserve resonance, and trust Dhurak and Veyra.

**Lore:** The king was manipulated, but he chose denial and command.

**Possible Use:** Grants advantage on one final clarity-window check involving truth, culpability, or mercy.

---

## Discovery 4 — Torn White Procession Cloth

**Location:** Implosion Threshold / final royal approach before the King's Chamber.

**Discovery Status:** Unavoidable, but not spotlighted as a solution.

**Description:** A strip of old pale ceremonial cloth preserved under crystal, similar to cloth seen in the Queen's Bridge memory. It may be caught beneath a cracked prayer mark, wrapped around a dead lantern bracket, or held by a fading responder-spirit that vanishes when the party approaches.

**Lore:** The queen's memory belongs to mourning, not possession.

**Runtime Use:** Present this as aftermath texture first. Do not announce that it is a salvation key. If the party keeps it, respects it, or later invokes it during the clarity window as release rather than restoration, it counts as a valid queen-linked relic for the slim salvation possibility.

---

## Discovery 5 — Queen's Lantern Charm

**Location:** Optional side niche or threshold shrine before the King's Chamber.

**Description:** A small charm shaped like a dwarven lantern, blackened by soot on one side and untouched on the other.

**Lore:** Lanterns guided witnesses and mourners; they were never meant to drag the dead back from darkness.

**Possible Use:** Valid queen-linked relic; can also grant advantage on one save against fear, grief, or final attunement pressure.

---

## Discovery 6 — Beam Recognition Carving

**Location:** Implosion Threshold.

**Description:** A half-melted engineering-priest diagram showing two crystal conduits feeding a central royal figure.

**Lore:** The green beams sustain the king.

**Possible Use:** Makes the final encounter beam mechanic obvious. The party should not need to infer this under fire.

**Suggested Cue:**

> The diagram is crude, frantic, and unfinished.
>
> Two giant figures.
> One throne.
> Two lines of green crystal light feeding inward.
>
> Someone carved the solution beside the horror and died before they could use it.

---

## Relic Use in Final Encounter

During the clarity window, one of the three valid queen-linked relics may enable the slim salvation route.

Recommended use:

- Present or invoke the relic.
- Frame the act as release, not restoration.
- Make **Religion, Persuasion, or Arcana DC 18**.

On success:

- the king is separated from the mountain without the party immediately killing him
- he becomes mortal or dies naturally in release
- the queen is not resurrected
- Stillpeak begins returning to neutrality

On failure:

- the king remains clear enough to beg for mercy death
- the party may still end him cleanly
- do not turn failure into an unfair wipe

## Mundane Treasure

Minimal optional treasure only.

If desired, use modest symbolic valuables:

- royal signet fragment worth 50 gp
- silver mourning clasp worth 35 gp
- cracked ceremonial lantern worth 40 gp if repaired
- polished crystal shard worth 25 gp, but visibly tainted

Do not place major royal hoards in Chapter 8 unless later campaign design deliberately expands beyond this final arc.
