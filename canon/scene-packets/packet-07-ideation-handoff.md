5. **Far-Side Mourning Threshold**

The party should end Chapter 7 after crossing the Queen's Bridge and receiving a short far-side scene pointing toward throneward descent / Chapter 8.

---

## Key Story Decisions

- The dwarven city should be mostly scenic/overlook-based in Chapter 7, not a broad city crawl.
- The city is empty and lifeless except for spirits and pulsing cursed crystals.
- The crystals should feel increasingly aware, especially near the Queen's Bridge.
- Spirits become more numerous closer to the bridge where the queen fell.
- The Queen's Bridge reveal occurs through layered spirit-scenes:
  - sacred/civic procession
  - sudden tremor
  - failed rescue
  - queen falling too deep to recover
  - immediate aftermath scattering
- The queen's fall was caused by a tremor, not murder, betrayal, enemy attack, or sabotage.
- Dwarves scatter immediately after the fall:
  - some to warn the king
  - some to rush toward the cavern bottom with ropes/lanterns
  - some to pray or manage panic
  - some to silence dangerous grief
- The path beyond the bridge points eventually toward the throne room and a future confrontation with the king.

---

## Reveal Boundaries

Do reveal in Chapter 7:

- The bridge was a sacred/civic royal crossing.
- The queen fell from the bridge during a tremor.
- The fall was witnessed.
- The aftermath immediately connected the tragedy to the king.
- The route ahead leads throneward.

Do **not** reveal yet:

- King immortality.
- Mountain-heart mechanism.
- Final solution.
- Full reactivation timeline.
- Whether the king is villain, victim, or both.
- Any geomancer-led explanation for the bridge tragedy.

---

## Geomancer Boundary

Geomancers are a separate order and are unrelated to the dwarven royal/religious/civic material in Chapter 7.

Do not frame the Queen's Bridge tragedy as a geomancer failure, geomancer ritual, or geomancer containment event.

## Monster / Threat Logic

### Spirits

- Memory-bound victims/witnesses.
- Not default combat targets.
- Increase in density near the bridge.

### Grief-Crystal Creatures

- Malignant resonance growths spawned by corrupted crystals.
- Not directly controlled by the king.
- Function like geologic cancer set free by negative energy.
- Especially difficult for Erny and Maelreth because of mountain attunement.

### Witness-Hall Grief-Crystal Animus

- Large earth-elemental-inspired grief-crystal creature.
- Main pre-bridge combat set piece.
- Fixates on Erny and Maelreth.
- Causes painful sympathy, hesitation, intrusive psychic pressure.

### Crystal Shardlings

- Two small bridge nuisance monsters.
- Pop from far-side crystal formations only when The Hollow Below activates.
- Also pressure Erny/Maelreth, but at smaller scale.

### The Hollow Below

- Ancient foreign bridge predator based on a roper chassis.
- Lives beneath the Queen's Bridge.
- Not related to the king's grief and not responsible for the queen's fall.
- Exploits the tragedy and feeds near the wound.
- Feels to Erny/Maelreth like cold absence, not grief.
