### Village Overview

Cinderwatch is a frontier settlement built from stone and heavy timber, perched where the lower pass levels out before rising toward Stillpeak. For roughly two months, the village has reported bodies found standing upright, shadows locked into snow, and wandering dead that do not behave like mindless predators.

Population is reduced and withdrawn. Most residents remain indoors, watching from frosted windows.

### Village Layout (DM Guidance)

- **Lower Road (Entrance):** snow-packed road, a single flickering lamppost, travel signs that stop appearing after the first wave of horrors.
- **Town Center:** longhall, tavern, inn, general store; minimal movement; voices low; no ambient animal noise.
- **Upper Row (Near the Slopes):** smaller homes and sheds; the Vault sits closest to the footpath toward Stillpeak; tools abandoned and rimed with frost.

**Wants:** answers and a way to let the dead ‘rest’ without the town having to watch them stand.

### The Karagvorn Line — Local Understanding

Locals don’t describe a wall. They describe a point where the world’s rules start slipping. Hunters say the air feels ‘heavier’ and that it becomes hard to trust direction. Dwarves call it the Karagvorn Line—a stone-sorrow boundary where grief saturates the land.

**Important:** villagers do not claim it is sentient. They claim the mountain notices you once you cross.

### The Cinderwatch Vault (Secure Storage)

To survive the ascent, villagers constructed a heavy timber-and-stone vault reinforced with glacial iron bands. Adventurers, traders, and hunters use it to secure heavy gear and unstable magic items before attempting any climb near Stillpeak.

**Security:** reinforced lock mechanism (non-magical), two custodians, ledger tracking deposits.

**Reason:** extradimensional storage becomes unreliable near the slopes; weight becomes dangerous; lost gear is common.

**DM Use:** this is the party’s natural offload point; it teaches encumbrance culture without lecturing.

- **Investigation (DC 12):** Frost forms geometric patterns inside—like grief crystallized.
- **Arcana (DC 14):** Extradimensional items feel stiff; minor magic feels ‘dragged.’
- **Insight (DC 12):** The town built this out of fear and necessity, not tradition.

### First Undead Encounter — The Frozen Dead (Mournful)

Place this encounter near the upper row or behind the tavern. A single corpse stands upright, coated with thin frost. Its shadow is frozen into the snow like a black stain. When approached, the head turns slowly—not aggressive, but aware.

**Behavior:** slow, dragging gait; soft moan; reaches out as if seeking something; does not attack unless provoked.

**If destroyed:** collapses into powdered frost like shattered glass.

**If spared:** it wanders aimlessly, always orienting subtly toward Stillpeak.

- **Medicine (DC 12):** No signs of struggle; death doesn’t match exposure.
- **Religion (DC 14):** This feels like resonance/anchoring, not classic necromancy.
- **Insight (DC 12):** The undead is sad, not malicious.
- **Arcana (DC 15):** A faint grief-based vector points upward toward Stillpeak.

### Investigation Clues (Optional, Quick)

- **Frozen Shadows:** Investigation (DC 13) reveals a three-dimensional crust; Arcana (DC 15) reveals emotional imprint.
- **Footprints That Stop:** Survival (DC 12) suggests flight, not struggle; Insight (DC 13) suggests collapse from fear or heaviness.
- **Night Sounds:** villagers report soft crying on still nights; Tremorscope pulses slightly more near upper row.

### Scene End — Transition to the Ascent

After the party secures gear, gathers testimony, and witnesses the Frozen Dead, the town delivers its blunt warning:

> “No one who crosses the Line returns the same. If you go up, do not linger. Do not trust easy paths. Stillpeak remembers its pain.”

The Tremorscope pulses—slow, steady, mournful—pointing toward the rising trail.
