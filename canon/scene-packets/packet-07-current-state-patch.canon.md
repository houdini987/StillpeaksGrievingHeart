## 4. Queen's Bridge Physical Continuity Rule

The Queen's Bridge is damaged but still crossable.

It may have cracked rails, edge gaps, broken parapets, fractured tiles, unstable sections, underside fissures, and a central fall-scar, but it must not be broken across or split into disconnected spans. The party's route across the bridge remains physically continuous.

This does **not** make the crossing safe. The bridge should remain dangerous because of:

- unstable stone
- Tremor Memory Events
- edge and gap risk
- The Hollow Below's tendrils and forced movement
- Crystal Shardling movement pressure
- grief-memory distraction and psychic resonance

Use crossing checks, hazards, and monster pressure to make the route perilous without making the map itself impassable.
