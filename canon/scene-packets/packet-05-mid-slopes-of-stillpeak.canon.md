# Scene Packet 5 - The Final Moraine Ascent

## 1. At-a-Glance Summary

**Location:** Packet 05 - The Shattered Moraine and Summit Face

**Current Table Baseline:** The party has completed the Wind-Carved Spine and is arriving at the Shattered Moraine. The Silent Echo Cairn has already been solved in actual play; do not replay, relocate, or reskin it here.

**Purpose:** Conclude the exterior climb in one decisive, high-pressure ascent sequence. The Shattered Moraine map is now the final pre-summit map: the party crosses unstable moraine, endures escalating altitude sickness, outruns the dead and the collapsing slope, and reaches the concealed summit entrance leading into Stillpeak.

**Expected Duration:** 90-150 minutes of play, depending on player hesitation, timer pressure, and whether they attempt to stand and fight.

**Encounter Types:** Timed traversal, environmental hazard, forced-movement pressure, psychological undead emergence, compressed emotional threshold, summit-door discovery.

**Key Mechanics:** Physical egg timer, visible progress track, altitude sickness phases, group movement checks, escalating terrain consequences, anti-stall pressure, optional short combat bursts that are explicitly inefficient.

**Key Discoveries:** The summit is not merely above the party; it contains a hidden constructed entrance. The Tremorscope no longer only points upward: near the summit face, it answers from inside the stone.

**Target Endpoint:** The party reaches the summit face and discovers or opens the ancient dwarven entrance into Stillpeak.

## 2. Narrative Setup

### Scene Purpose

This packet is the final exterior ascent. Do not extend the climb into additional standalone pre-summit maps unless the user explicitly asks.

The table has already spent substantial play time on mountain traversal. This scene should feel like the hardest and last ascent, not another repetitive climbing segment. Use the Shattered Moraine map as an anthropomorphic ascent map: the party begins at the bottom of the image and moves upward both visually and topographically.

### Design Intent

The scene should create urgency, not procedural delay.

Players should feel that they can fight, investigate, assist, and improvise, but standing still is no longer neutral. The moraine moves on its own rhythm. The dead are not a conventional encounter to clear; they are the mountain showing the party what happens to those who stop.

The physical timer is part of the encounter. It exists because this is an in-person session, not a Roll20-first session. Use the timer to make the table feel the mountain's rhythm.

### DM Runtime Instruction - Bespoke In-Person Mode

This scene uses a real physical timer and visible progress tracking. It is not standard turn pacing.

When running this scene:

- Do not default to long deliberation loops.
- Do not treat the dead as the main objective.
- Do not run the Silent Echo Cairn again; it is completed table history.
- Do not add another pre-summit map after this one.
- The mountain acts when the timer expires, whether players are ready or not.
- Use enemies as consequence and pressure, not as a fight-to-clear objective.
- Keep the party moving toward the summit face.
- End at the summit door, or with the door opening and the first interior reveal.

If using a DM assistant during play, instruct it:

> We are in a bespoke in-person final ascent sequence using a physical timer. Provide short reactive outputs only. Do not revert to standard turn-by-turn climbing, do not replay completed cairn content, and keep pressure moving toward the summit door.

## 3. Read-Aloud Scene Introduction

The slope ahead is not a path. It is what remains after too many paths failed.

Broken anchors jut from the scree. A snapped length of rope appears from under ice and vanishes again beneath sliding stone. Bone, gear, and collapse debris surface and sink by turns as if the mountain is slowly sorting what it means to keep.

The moraine rises above you in broken bands of stone and dirty ice. At the top, through blowing frost and fractured air, the peak waits close enough to feel reachable and far enough to feel cruel.

Then the ground shifts.

Not in a great avalanche. Not yet.

It settles, tightens, and settles again, as if the mountain is breathing beneath the debris.

The Tremorscope pulses sharply, out of rhythm with your own heartbeat.

There is a rhythm here.

And as you stand still long enough to notice it, the stone beneath your boots moves just slightly out from under you.

Not enough to fall.

Enough to understand.

You are not climbing a slope.

You are moving across something that will not hold still for you.

### Table Instruction to Players

Use after the read-aloud, while placing the timer on the table.

The mountain has a rhythm. I am putting a timer on the table. Each time it runs out, the mountain acts, whether you are ready or not.

You can move, fight, investigate, help each other, cast spells, or improvise. Nothing is forbidden. But standing still is no longer neutral. If you move decisively, you stay ahead of the moraine. If you hesitate, it catches up.

- Center: burden ridge, larger outcrops, and altitude pressure.

### Active Systems

## 5. Running the Location

### Core Progress Track

Track progress visibly if possible. Use Roll20 tokens on the map, paper marks, or both.

**Progress Goal:** 6 progress to reach the summit face.

Progress advances when the party succeeds on movement, pathfinding, stabilization, rescue, or clever problem-solving checks. Failure should usually produce complications rather than stopping progress entirely.

### Suggested Progress Bands

| Progress | Beat | Map Zone |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Beat 1 - Field of Failed Steps | Bottom edge |
| 1 | Beat 2 - Shifting Lanes | Lower-middle |
| 2 | Beat 3 - Burden Ridge | Center outcrops |
| 3 | Beat 4 - Dead Field | Upper-middle pockets |
| 4 | Beat 5 - Glassing Slope | Upper slope |
| 5 | Beat 6 - Summit Face | Top edge |
| 6 | Door reached/opened | Off-map threshold |

### Timer Rule

Use a physical egg timer or phone timer. Default: 2 minutes per cycle.

When the timer expires, the mountain acts. Reset the timer immediately after resolving the consequence.

If players are actively resolving a declared action when the timer expires, apply the consequence after that action resolves. If players are debating without committing, interrupt with the consequence immediately.

### Player Action Menu

On each pressure cycle, players may choose actions freely. Common useful actions include:

- **Advance:** Move toward the next progress band. Usually Athletics, Acrobatics, or Survival.
- **Find the Line:** Identify safer route. Usually Survival, Perception, or Investigation.
- **Stabilize:** Secure footing, rope, anchor, or formation. Usually Athletics, Sleight of Hand/tool use, or Survival.
- **Assist:** Help another PC recover, climb, or avoid separation. Usually Athletics, Medicine, Survival, or a relevant class feature/spell.
- **Read the Tremorscope:** Interpret direction, resonance, or hidden structure. Usually Arcana, Investigation, Religion, or character-specific reasoning.
- **Fight:** Attack something visible. Allowed, but inefficient. Fighting does not normally advance progress unless it directly clears an immediate obstacle.

### Baseline DCs

| Situation | DC |
|---|---|
| Stable but difficult movement | 14 |
| Unstable lane, bad footing, or pressure | 15 |
| Rescue, forced correction, or acting under timer consequence | 16 |
| Severe late-scene movement under altitude phase 3 | 17 |

Reward strong plans, rope use, class features, clever spells, and prior setup with advantage, lower DCs, or reduced consequences.

## 6. Altitude Sickness - Separate from Mountain Toll

Altitude Sickness is a three-phase internal degradation track. It affects movement and responsiveness, not cold exposure or general fatigue.

### Phase 1 - Breath Debt

**Trigger:** Beat 1, as soon as the party enters the Shattered Moraine.

**Effect:** The first time each character in a pressure cycle dashes, climbs aggressively, or corrects a failed movement action, they must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or lose 10 feet of movement on their next movement action.

**Feel:** The party can still function, but breath becomes a resource.

### Phase 2 - Oxygen Lag

**Trigger:** Beat 3, when the party reaches the Burden Ridge.

**Effect:** Each character's speed is reduced by 10 feet while in the moraine. If a character both moves and uses a bonus action in the same pressure cycle, they must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or lose their reaction until the next timer reset.

**Feel:** The body responds late. Recovery becomes harder than action.

### Phase 3 - Summit Hypoxia

**Trigger:** Beat 6, when the party reaches the Summit Face.

**Effect:** Characters have disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity checks made specifically to climb, balance, arrest a slide, or force movement across the summit face unless aided, anchored, magically supported, or moving through a previously secured lane. Once per character per pressure cycle, if they fail one of these movement checks, they lose remaining movement for that cycle.

**Feel:** The summit is close, but the body is unreliable.

## 7. Encounter Modules

### BEAT 1 - Field of Failed Steps

**Map Zone:** Bottom 20 percent of the Shattered Moraine map.

**Purpose:** Establish the final ascent, introduce timer pressure, and start Altitude Sickness Phase 1.

**What the Party Is Doing:** The party is establishing a stable ascent line through the first field of shattered scree and buried ice.

**Primary Challenge:** Decide who leads, how fast to move, whether to secure a line, and whether to stay tight or spread out.

**Suggested Checks:**

- Group Survival or Athletics DC 14 to establish the first line.
- Perception or Investigation DC 14 to identify old failed routes, broken anchors, and unstable pockets.
- Constitution DC 12 if a PC pushes aggressively under Breath Debt.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress. The party enters the lower shifting lanes in workable formation.

**Failure:** Gain 1 progress but add one complication: one PC loses footing, falls slightly behind, loses 10 feet of next movement, or starts Beat 2 separated from the cleanest lane.

**Timer Consequences:** Minor scree shift, sliding stone, or a brief loss of footing. Avoid damage unless the party ignores obvious danger.

**Spoken Cues:**

- "The first step holds. The second step changes the first."
- "A bootprint you left a moment ago slides three feet downhill and vanishes under fresh gravel."
- "The old rope frozen into the scree is not an aid. It is a warning."

---

### BEAT 2 - The Shifting Lanes

**Map Zone:** Lower-middle diagonal rubble bands and uneven ridges.

**Purpose:** Turn movement into route choice. The party should see that there is no single safe line.

**What the Party Is Doing:** Choosing between unstable lanes while keeping formation from stretching or breaking.

**Primary Challenge:** The path that looks safest may move. The path that looks fastest may split the party.

**Suggested Checks:**

- Survival DC 15 to choose the best line.
- Athletics or Acrobatics DC 15 to cross a moving rubble band.
- Assist action DC 14 to keep a slower PC from falling behind.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress and preserve formation.

**Failure:** Gain 1 progress but add one complication: split formation, one PC prone, one PC loses reaction, or next group movement check increases by +1 DC.

**Timer Consequences:** A lane collapses behind the rear-most PC, making retreat visibly worse. If no one is rear-most because the party is spread out, choose the most isolated PC.

**Spoken Cues:**

- "The safe-looking lane does not collapse. It turns."
- "Stone rolls under the snow in a line, like something moving beneath a blanket."
- "You can keep the group together, or you can take the cleanest path. The moraine does not offer both."

---

### BEAT 3 - The Burden Ridge

**Map Zone:** Central cluster of larger stones and outcrops.

**Purpose:** Start Altitude Sickness Phase 2 and make cohesion the central problem.

**What the Party Is Doing:** Climbing over larger outcrops while breathing becomes unreliable and movement desynchronizes.

**Primary Challenge:** Maintain cohesion while individual characters slow down at different moments.

**Choice Structure:** Each PC should declare one posture for the pressure cycle:

- Advance
- Stabilize
- Assist
- Read the Tremorscope
- Fight/guard

**Suggested Checks:**

- Athletics DC 15 to climb over the burden ridge.
- Constitution DC 13 for Oxygen Lag when movement and bonus action are both used.
- Survival or Perception DC 15 to keep the group on a shared line.
- Arcana, Investigation, or Religion DC 15 to interpret a Tremorscope pulse.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress and identify that the summit signal is beginning to answer from inside the upper mountain.

**Failure:** Gain progress only if at least half the party succeeds or spends meaningful resources. Otherwise, remain at this beat and trigger a complication.

**Subtle Dead Presence:** Do not present clear attack targets yet. Use partial, fleeting, embedded movement: a hand under scree, a skull turning under ice, a rope tightening around an ankle and then vanishing. These effects can force saves, reduce movement, or impose disadvantage, but should not invite a standard melee exchange.

**Timer Consequences:** Oxygen spike, dead-hand ankle snag, shifting outcrop, or anchor groan.

**Spoken Cues:**

- "Your breath arrives late, as if your body has to be reminded what it is for."
- "Something under the scree moves with you for three steps, then stops when you stop."
- "A hand appears between two stones, fingers curled around a piton too old to trust. Then the stones shift and it is gone."

---

### BEAT 4 - The Dead Field

**Map Zone:** Upper-middle open pockets and scattered large rocks.

**Purpose:** Make the dead unavoidable, but make fighting them a losing strategy.

**What the Party Is Doing:** Crossing through the accumulated remains of prior climbers while the moraine begins returning the dead to motion.

**Primary Challenge:** Keep moving while the dead interfere with footing, spacing, and morale.

**Unavoidable Presence, Diminishing Returns:** The dead appear here no matter what. They should not behave like enemies waiting to be cleared. They surface from collapse fans, grip from under ice, drag at ankles, and re-form when smashed unless radiant power, fire, collapse, or upward movement breaks contact.

**Anti-Combat Escalation:**

- **Soft:** Attacks can break an immediate grip or clear one PC, but do not reduce the overall pressure.
- **Medium:** Each pressure cycle spent primarily fighting increases the next movement DC by +1 and causes another dead contact.
- **Hard:** If the party tries to hold ground for multiple cycles, the ground behind and beneath them collapses. Force upward movement, separation, or a group Dexterity save DC 16 to avoid being dragged into unstable ground.

**Suggested Checks:**

- Group Athletics, Acrobatics, or Survival DC 15 to push through.
- Wisdom save DC 14 to resist fixation, panic, or the urge to answer the dead.
- Strength or Dexterity save DC 14 to escape a grip without spending the whole cycle.
- Radiant damage, fire, Turn Undead, or similar effects can reduce pressure for one cycle.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress. The party exits the dead field with the dead behind or beneath them, not defeated.

**Failure:** Add a complication and trigger one dead contact: speed reduced, prone, separated, or disadvantage on the next movement check.

**Timer Consequences:** A dead contact occurs. Choose the most isolated, rear-most, prone, or slowed PC.

**Read-Aloud:**

The scree ahead shivers in a pattern too deliberate to be settling.

Stone rolls aside. A hand comes up first, wrapped in ice and gravel. Then another, farther upslope. Then a shoulder, a jaw, the bent frame of someone still tangled in climbing rope.

They do not rise like warriors.

They rise like consequences.

Every shape that surfaces is turned uphill. Every broken hand reaches not for your throat, but for your boots, your ropes, your packs, your hesitation. The mountain is not sending them to kill you quickly.

It is showing you what stops here.

And then the ground behind you drops six inches with a sound like a door being barred.

**If the Party Tries to Stand and Fight:**

You can strike them. You can break hands, split skulls, burn the frost from old bones.

But the dead are not holding this ground.

The ground is holding them.

Every moment you spend proving you can win a fight, the moraine proves this is not a battlefield. It shifts under your stance, closes behind your heels, and makes the next step upward harder than the last.

This is not a place you win.

It is a place you cross.

---

### BEAT 5 - The Glassing Slope

**Map Zone:** Upper 20 percent of the map, where debris thins and ice becomes visible.

**Purpose:** Absorb the Ice-Locked Shelf and Frozen Reflection Shelf into a single compressed threshold beat.

**What the Party Is Doing:** Crossing from shifting moraine onto smoother, more treacherous reflective ice just below the summit face.

**Primary Challenge:** The danger changes from chaotic instability to precise punishment.

**Terrain Shift:**

- Fewer rocks.
- More exposed ice.
- Narrower safe lanes.
- Sliding replaces sinking.
- Reflection replaces noise.

**Reflection Moment:** Keep this brief. One group moment is enough unless the table wants character spotlights.

The ice catches each climber for a heartbeat. Not a perfect reflection. A burdened one. Each face looks older, lower, closer to the stone than it should. For a moment, the ice does not show who you are. It shows what this climb has been taking from you.

A sincere acknowledgment, roleplay beat, or DC 14 Insight, Religion, Arcana, or Survival check resolves the moment. Failure does not block progress; it imposes disadvantage on that PC's next movement or Tremorscope-related check.

**Suggested Checks:**

- Acrobatics or Athletics DC 16 to cross the glassing slope.
- Survival or Perception DC 15 to identify safe lanes.
- Assist DC 15 to prevent a slide or overcommitment.
- Insight/Religion/Arcana/Survival DC 14 for the reflection moment.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress. The summit face is reached.

**Failure:** Add a slide, separation, or lost movement. If the timer expires during this beat, combine the consequence with an ice fracture rather than dead emergence.

**Spoken Cues:**

- "The ice gives you room to move and then punishes the first step taken too quickly."
- "A short correction becomes a slide of boot leather and sparks of frost."
- "The reflection beneath you lags half a breath behind your body."

---

### BEAT 6 - The Summit Face

**Map Zone:** Top edge of the map and implied summit wall beyond it.

**Purpose:** Trigger Altitude Sickness Phase 3 and reveal the entrance into Stillpeak.

**What the Party Is Doing:** Making the final approach to a summit wall that is not fully natural.

**Primary Challenge:** Confirm and open the entrance while the body, terrain, and resonance all degrade.

**Read-Aloud:**

The moraine ends badly.

It does not flatten into a summit. It rises into a wall of black stone and dirty ice, too sheer to be natural and too weathered to be cleanly worked. The peak is above you, but the Tremorscope no longer points up.

It points forward.

Then it answers.

A pulse travels from the device into the stone, and a second pulse returns from within the mountain itself. Not an echo. A reply.

Under the ice, lines appear where no lines should be: vertical seams, old geometric cuts, a lintel buried beneath rime, dwarven stonework hidden under the mountain's own scar tissue.

The summit is not the destination.

It is the door.

**Altitude Phase 3:** Summit Hypoxia begins here.

**Suggested Checks:**

- Arcana, Investigation, Religion, or Tremorscope interpretation DC 15 to confirm the resonance seam.
- Athletics DC 16 to clear ice and stone from the door under pressure.
- Dwarvish, History, Stonecunning-like reasoning, or tool use DC 15 to identify dwarven construction logic.
- Group stabilization DC 16 if the timer expires before the door is opened.

**Opening the Door:** Do not make this a long puzzle. The party has earned the threshold.

Allow the door to open through any two of the following:

- Tremorscope aligned to the returning pulse.
- Ice cleared from the central seam.
- Dwarven construction recognized or interpreted.
- Radiant, thunder, fire, or force applied carefully to the resonance line.
- A PC accepts a cost: fatigue, damage, lost gear, or holding position while others work.

**Failure:** The door still becomes discoverable, but the party pays for delay: fatigue, damage, separation, dead pressure from below, or the first interior threat hearing them arrive.

**Timer Consequences:** The summit face shifts, the ice seam closes slightly, or the slope below collapses. Increase urgency rather than blocking access.

**Door Opening Read-Aloud:**

The seam does not crack open all at once.

First, the ice over it fractures in a single clean line. Then the stone beneath answers with a sound too deep to be heard only by the ears. It moves through your boots, your teeth, your ribs, and the Tremorscope shakes so hard it seems for a moment it might break apart.

The mountain exhales.

Not wind.

Air from within.

Warmer than the summit. Older than the storm.

The black seam widens into darkness, and beyond it stone steps descend into Stillpeak.

## 8. Player Curiosity Hooks

Use these to respond to investigation without derailing the ascent.

### Old Gear and Bodies

- Failed climbers came this way over many years, not all in one expedition.
- Some gear is dwarven-made or repaired in dwarven style.
- Many remains face uphill, suggesting they were still trying to reach the summit when they died.

### Tremorscope

- At lower progress, it pulses upward.
- At mid-progress, it begins to split and delay.
- At the summit face, it receives an answer from inside the mountain.
- Because the Harmonic Finder was missed, interpretation under pressure is harder; do not punish this constantly, but use it to justify DC 15-16 checks when the scene is chaotic.

### The Dead

- The dead are not organized guardians.
- They are climbers, failures, and bodies caught in the moraine's rhythm.
- They should feel tragic and horrifying, not tactical.

### The Door

- It is dwarven, but hidden by mountain deformation, ice, and time.
- It was designed to be found through resonance, not sight alone.
- It leads inward and downward, not onto an exterior summit plateau.

## 9. Escalation Signal

Use escalation when the timer expires, players hesitate, or the party tries to convert the scene into a conventional fight.

### Escalation Ladder

1. **Unstable Ground:** minor slides, lost movement, split formation.
2. **Breath and Delay:** altitude checks, lost reactions, slower correction.
3. **Dead Contact:** grips, ankle snares, prone threats, movement loss.
4. **Collapsing Behind:** retreat worsens, rear spaces become unsafe.
5. **Glassing Ice:** precise movement, slides, overcommitment.
6. **Summit Compression:** hypoxia, closing seam, final push.

Do not escalate by simply adding hit point damage every time. Escalate through position, pace, fatigue, formation, and loss of safe choices.
