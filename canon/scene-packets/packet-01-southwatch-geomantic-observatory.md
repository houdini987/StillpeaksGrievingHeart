# Scene Packet 1 — Southwatch Geomantic Observatory

## At-a-Glance Summary

**Purpose:** Establish observatory authority, introduce Tremorscope, and anchor the journal as the inciting event.

**Expected Duration:** 30–60 minutes

**Encounter Types:** Exploration, roleplay, environmental tension

---

## Narrative Setup

A dying hunter delivered a journal describing unnatural stillness, frozen shadows, and a mountain that "beats like a wounded heart." The party is tasked with delivering it to the geomancers.

---

## Key Elements

### Orven Duskslate
- Dwarf geomancer
- Calm, analytical, quietly haunted

### The Tremorscope
- Lantern-like seismic instrument
- Displays tremor lines and directional pull
