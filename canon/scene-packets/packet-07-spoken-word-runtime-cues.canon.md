# Scene Packet 7 Supplement — Spoken-Word Runtime Cues

## Canon Status

This file is a canon spoken-word support supplement for Scene Packet 7 / Chapter 7: **Bridgeward Way**.

It does not replace the read-aloud text in the main Packet 7 files. Use it as a quick draw-pile of table narration, transition lines, partial-success phrasing, and combat narration to keep Chapter 7 emotionally legible during live play.

## 1. Runtime Use Rule

Use these cues when the table needs momentum.

Do not read every line. Choose one or two that fit the moment, then return agency to the players.

Default rhythm:

1. One atmospheric line.
2. One concrete table cue.
3. Ask: **"What do you do?"**

---

## 2. Transition from Exodus Landing into Bridgeward Way

Use after the party has oriented at Exodus Landing and commits south / Bridgeward.

> The Landing does not close behind you, but it feels farther away with every step.
>
> Behind you are routes built for survival.
>
> Ahead is a route built for memory.

> The Bridgeward passage accepts you without welcome.
>
> The old dwarven signs remain precise. The stonework grows cleaner. The silence grows less empty.
>
> Somewhere below, something is keeping time.

> The Tremorscope beats once in your hand.
>
> Not pointing. Not spinning.
>
> Answering.

## 3. Empty City Verge — Extra Lines

Use if the party lingers at the overlook, asks what they can see, or needs stronger awe before moving on.

> The longer you look, the worse the silence becomes.
>
> A city should have mistakes in it: smoke, voices, animals, loose doors, footsteps, somebody late to something.
>
> Stillpeak has none.

> Streets descend in careful dwarven geometry, but the geometry no longer serves the living.
>
> Roads lead to sealed halls. Bridges meet dark balconies. Market squares hold their shape around nothing.

> You do not see destruction first.
>
> You see interruption.
>
> As if an entire people stepped out of their own lives and never returned.

> Far below, a pale figure crosses the same courtyard three times.
>
> On the fourth pass, you realize it is not walking to a place.
>
> It is walking to a moment.

### If They Want to Explore the City

> You can see ways down, but none look like safe routes.
>
> Collapsed spans, sealed galleries, crystal-choked streets, and dead air divide the districts below.
>
> The city is vast enough to lose days in. The Tremorscope gives you one meaningful direction.
>
> Bridgeward.

## 4. Witness Halls — Extra Lines

### Entering the Halls

> The first chamber does not threaten you.
>
> That somehow makes it worse.
>
> It was built for witnesses, not enemies.

> Your footsteps sound too loud here.
>
> The walls were shaped to carry voices, bells, vows, and public words.
>
> Now every sound feels like it has no right to be first.

### Hall of Witness Niches

> One spirit lifts a lantern that has no flame.
>
> Another lowers her eyes before anyone passes.
>
> A child copies the gesture half a heartbeat too late, forever learning reverence from a parent who is also dead.

> They do not block you.
>
> They do not bless you.
>
> They wait for someone else to arrive.

### Gallery of Covered Faces

> The covered faces are more disturbing than smashed ones.
>
> Hate is simple.
>
> This was careful.

> Soot darkens the queen's face in one relief. Cloth hides another. A third was left unfinished before the sculptor carved the eyes.
>
> Every choice says the same thing:
>
> Remember her. But not directly.

### If Players Misread the Damage as Vandalism

> At first glance it could be vandalism.
>
> But the marks do not have the violence of hatred.
>
> They have the repetition of ritual. The fear of people who loved what they covered.

## 5. Animus Encounter — Extra Round Cues

Use one per round or when the combat risks becoming a simple damage race.

### Round Cue — The Room Learns Pain

> Every impact against the crystal seems to teach the room a new way to hurt.
>
> Shards grind underfoot. The old processional grooves fill with pale light.

### Round Cue — Attuned Pressure

> Erny and Maelreth, the creature's movement arrives in your skull before it arrives in the room.
>
> It is not thought.
>
> It is pressure choosing a direction.

### Round Cue — Not a Guardian

> The thing does not protect the hall.
>
> It does not defend a door or obey a command.
>
> It moves like grief trying to become anatomy.

### Defeat Follow-Up

> When the last shard falls still, the room does not feel purified.
>
> It feels relieved of one pressure.
>
> Only one.

## 6. Last Arch Before the Span — Breath Before the Bridge

Use before moving from Witness Halls to Queen's Bridge.

> Beyond the arch, the mountain stops pretending to be a hallway.
>
> Space opens beneath you, around you, above you.
>
> The bridge waits across all of it.

> The spirits ahead are not scattered anymore.
>
> They have gathered into sequence.
>
> The dead are lining up for the moment they cannot stop remembering.

> The Tremorscope beats against your palm.
>
> Once.
>
> Then the crystals along the bridge answer, one after another, like eyes opening in the dark.

## 7. Queen's Bridge — Extra Combat and Hazard Cues

### When Initiative Begins

> The bridge does not become a battlefield.
>
> It becomes a memory with teeth.

### When The Hollow Below Uses Tendrils

> Something black and jointless lashes up through the crack.
>
> It does not grab like a hand.
>
> It hooks like the dark itself has found purchase.

### When a Character Is Reeled Toward an Edge

> Your boots scrape stone.
>
> The edge comes closer in pieces: crack, rail, open air, nothing below it.
>
> Make the save.

### When Bright Light Suppresses Its Resistance

> The light reaches under the span and the darkness recoils.
>
> For the first time, it looks less like stone and more like something hiding behind stone.

### When Shardlings Pressure the Far Side

> The far side should feel like escape.
>
> Instead, the crystals there split open and begin to move.

### Tremor Memory Event Cue

> The bridge remembers movement before the stone moves.
>
> The spirits stumble first.
>
> Then the living feel the lurch.

### If the Fight Is Overwhelming the Story

> Under the violence, the memory continues.
>
> The queen is still walking.
>
> The tremor has not happened yet.
>
> The bridge is making you wait for it.

## 8. Queen's Fall — Optional Intensifiers

Use only if the core fall reveal needs extra weight.

> The first scream is not fear.
>
> It is disbelief.

> For a heartbeat, everyone on the bridge reaches in the same direction.
>
> A whole kingdom becomes hands reaching too late.

> The lanterns fall slower than she does.
>
> Their light follows her down until even light gives up.

> No enemy steps forward.
>
> No assassin flees.
>
> No weapon is raised.
>
> The mountain moved, and that was enough.

## 9. Far-Side Mourning Threshold — Extra Closing Lines

Use after the bridge, before ending the chapter.

> Crossing the bridge does not end the memory.
>
> It only puts you on the side where the news had to be carried.

> The far side is not a victory point.
>
> It is where grief became a message.

> Every spirit here is going somewhere.
>
> None of them arrive.

> The downward route waits beneath a cracked royal mark.
>
> The stone does not say welcome.
>
> It says the next truth is lower.

## 10. Quick Failure / Partial Success Phrases

Use these after failed or partial checks so failed rolls still move play forward.

### History / Dwarvish Failure

> You cannot read the exact meaning, but the repetition matters.
>
> Whatever this place was, it was official. Public. Used by many people more than once.

### Religion Failure

> The rite eludes you, but the posture does not.
>
> Heads lowered. Lanterns raised. Silence maintained.
>
> This was reverence under rules.

### Arcana Failure

> The crystals do not give you a clean answer.
>
> They brighten as you study them, which may be the only answer they intend to give.

### Perception Failure

> You see enough to know there is more here than you can safely take in at once.
>
> The bridge, the spirits, the crystals, the dark below — all of them compete for your attention.

### Insight Failure

> You cannot tell what the spirits want.
>
> You are not even sure wanting is something they can still do.

## 11. Emergency Pacing Buttons

Use when the table stalls.

### Push Toward Witness Halls

> The city below offers a hundred dead directions.
>
> The Tremorscope offers one living pulse.
>
> The route ahead descends toward the Witness Halls.

### Push Toward Queen's Bridge

> The halls have shown you witnesses, covered faces, and a memory afraid of itself.
>
> Beyond the final arch, the bridge is no longer a clue.
>
> It is the place.

### End Chapter 7 Cleanly

> You have crossed where she fell.
>
> You have seen what the mountain remembers.
>
> The next path does not lead to the bridge, or the city, or the witnesses.
>
> It leads toward the one who had to be told.
