# Scene Packet 6 Encounter Supplement — Hostile Intruders of the Exodus Stair

## Canon Status

This file is a canon encounter supplement for `canon/scene-packets/packet-06-the-exodus-stair.canon.md`.

It defines two hostile creature encounter options for Chapter 6 / Packet 6: The Exodus Stair.

These creatures are **not** part of the dwarven tragedy, not manifestations of the king's grief, and not spirits trapped by the exodus. They are opportunistic hostile creatures that have moved into abandoned, cold, dark, sealed infrastructure.

Use them to contrast with the benign-unless-attacked dwarven spirits. The spirits are victims caught in memory. These creatures are simply dangerous.

---

## Encounter Tone Rule

Do not over-theme these monsters around grief.

They can exploit Stillpeak's environment, but they should not explain it. Their presence should feel like dungeon ecology: predators, squatters, vermin, and opportunists occupying a ruined dwarven route.

Good framing:

- "Something has moved in."
- "The dead are not the only things inside the mountain."
- "Not everything here is tragic. Some things are just hungry, cruel, or territorial."

Avoid framing:

- grief-born mephits
- sorrow parasites
- monsters made from the king's pain
- creatures that know the queen/king story

## Encounter 1 — Ice Mephit Squatters

### Summary

A cluster of ice mephits has nested in the broken cistern channels near the Last Breath Refuge or along a side drainage passage between the refuge and the Broken Muster Niches.

They are not tied to the dwarven story. They found a cold, abandoned, partially sealed ruin with old water infrastructure and took it over.

### Recommended Placement

Use this encounter near one of the following:

- broken cistern channels in or near the Last Breath Refuge
- a narrow drainage side-passage below the refuge
- a cold maintenance basin between the refuge and the muster niches

Avoid interrupting the party's first earned short rest unless pacing requires it. A better use is after the rest, when the party begins poking around the cistern channels or side passages.

### Fresh Level 7 Party Scaling

For a fresh five-character level 7 party, use:

- **6 Ice Mephit Squatters**
- **1 Frost-Spite Mephit** as a slightly tougher ringleader

If the party is depleted or you want a lighter encounter, use:

- **4 Ice Mephit Squatters**
- no ringleader

If the party is very fresh and tactically sharp, use:

- **6 Ice Mephit Squatters**
- **1 Frost-Spite Mephit**
- terrain complications from slick cistern channels

### Tactics

The mephits are nasty, cowardly, and unserious until hurt.

They:

- hide in cracked basins, frozen cistern channels, and drain slots
- throw ice shards from cover
- try to lure characters onto slick stone
- gang up on isolated or prone targets
- flee if the Frost-Spite Mephit is destroyed and half their number are gone

They do not understand the dwarven spirits except as things to mock, avoid, or imitate badly.

### Terrain Features

Use one or two:

- **Slick Cistern Stone:** A creature that dashes or is pushed across the cistern floor must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or fall prone.
- **Narrow Drain Slot:** Small creatures can move through a drain slot as difficult terrain. Medium creatures cannot enter without squeezing and spending an action.
- **Frozen Basin Lip:** Half cover for Small creatures hiding behind broken cistern stone.
- **Dead Lantern Hook:** A creature can knock a hanging hook or bracket loose as an action; one creature below must make a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw** or take 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage.

### Monster Treasure / Salvage

Ice mephits are not carrying meaningful coin, but they hoard shiny salvage and useful scraps in frozen basins and drain pockets.

After the fight, characters can search the nest.

Suggested checks:

- **Investigation DC 12:** Find the obvious hoard pocket.
- **Perception DC 13:** Notice a second stash wedged inside a drain slot.
- **Sleight of Hand or Dexterity DC 13:** Extract a fragile object from ice without cracking it.
- **Fire, tools, or 10 minutes of careful thawing:** Automatically free one frozen item without a check.

Default loot:

- 18 gp, 34 sp, and 61 cp in mixed old coinage and traveler scraps.
- Two small garnets worth 15 gp each, frozen inside a cracked basin lip.
- A silvered dwarven belt clasp worth 20 gp.
- A chipped but usable **cold-seal lens** from an old dwarven lantern.

**Cold-Seal Lens.** Once mounted into a lantern or held before a light source, this lens can help distinguish mundane frost from magical/cursed cold. Once in Packet 6 or 7, it grants advantage on an Investigation, Arcana, or Survival check involving cold-affected stone, frozen mechanisms, or ice-covered inscriptions.

Optional additional find if the Frost-Spite Mephit is present:

- **Frost-Spite Shard.** A sharp, translucent ice crystal left behind when the ringleader dies. It melts after 24 hours unless kept in magical or extreme cold. Before it melts, it can be used once as an improvised thrown weapon: range 20/60, +Dex to hit, 1d6 piercing + 1d6 cold on hit. It is not a permanent magic item.

## Encounter 2 — Chain-Lift Crawler

### Summary

A cold-adapted carrion crawler or cave scavenger has nested in the Chain-Lift Void. It feeds on bats, vermin, old carrion, and anything that falls into the shaft.

This creature is not part of the grief story. It is a predator occupying vertical ruin-space. It attacks because the party enters its hunting territory.

### Recommended Placement

Use this encounter in the Chain-Lift Void, especially if the party:

- makes sustained noise
- drops gear into the shaft
- sends one character too far ahead
- clusters on a ledge
- lingers after the Resonance Pulse Track has already advanced

### Fresh Level 7 Party Scaling

For a fresh five-character level 7 party, use:

- **1 Chain-Lift Crawler**
- **2 Shaft Scuttlers**
- active shaft terrain

If the party is depleted, use:

- **1 Chain-Lift Crawler** only
- no Shaft Scuttlers
- reduce the crawler's HP to 95

If the party is fresh and you want a harder vertical fight, use:

- **1 Chain-Lift Crawler**
- **3 Shaft Scuttlers**
- Resonance Pulse Track starts at 1 when the fight begins

### Tactics

The Chain-Lift Crawler is an ambush predator.

It:

- clings to the underside of broken stairs or the central chain
- lashes at isolated characters with tentacles
- attempts to paralyze a target and drag them toward a ledge or maintenance alcove
- avoids staying surrounded
- retreats down the shaft if bloodied and no prey is paralyzed

The Shaft Scuttlers harass ranged characters, cut off ledges, and make the vertical battlefield feel alive.

### Terrain Features

Use at least two:

- **Broken Stair Gap:** Crossing a missing stair segment requires **Acrobatics DC 14** or **Athletics DC 14**. Failure costs movement and leaves the creature off-balance; failure by 5 or more causes a short fall for 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.
- **Central Chain:** A creature adjacent to the chain can use an action to stabilize itself or another creature, granting advantage on the next check or save against falling or forced movement.
- **Swinging Chain Pulse:** If the Resonance Pulse Track triggers during combat, the chain swings. Creatures on exposed ledges must make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** or be knocked prone or pushed 5 ft.
- **Maintenance Ledge:** Narrow ledges count as difficult terrain for Medium creatures. A creature shoved while on a ledge must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or fall to a lower ledge, taking 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.

### Monster Treasure / Salvage

The crawler is not carrying treasure, but its nest contains debris dragged from the shaft and remains of older victims.

After the fight, characters can search the nest, ledges, or the jammed lift platform.

Suggested checks:

- **Investigation DC 13:** Sort useful gear from bones, old cloth, and debris.
- **Perception DC 14:** Spot a satchel caught beneath webbing or chain links.
- **Athletics or Acrobatics DC 14:** Reach a dangerous ledge containing salvage.
- **Survival DC 13:** Identify which remains are recent animal prey versus older humanoid remains.

Default loot:

- 42 gp in mixed coinage from old victims and broken belt pouches.
- One intact potion of healing wrapped in cracked leather.
- One climber's kit missing only one piton; still usable.
- A silver ring worth 25 gp, engraved in Dwarvish with the phrase "steady hands, safe return."
- One **Lift-Brake Pin** wedged in old grease near the jammed platform.

**Lift-Brake Pin.** A heavy dwarven locking pin used to hold a lift wheel or brake assembly in place. As an action, a character can use it to pin, jam, or stabilize one mechanical component such as a lift gear, chain housing, pressure lever, or rotating stone bracket. The next relevant Athletics, Investigation, or tool check has advantage. After one major use, the pin bends or becomes lodged.

Optional additional find if the party risks a dangerous ledge:

- **Unbroken Anchor Ring.** A detachable dwarven anchor ring worth 30 gp. When used with rope in worked stone, it grants advantage once on a group climbing, lowering, or rescue check.

**Damage Resistances:** cold

**Traits**

**Spider Climb.** The crawler can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Shaft Predator.** The crawler has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while clinging to chains, ledges, broken stairs, or shaft walls.

**Multiattack.** The crawler makes two attacks: one with Tentacles and one with Bite. It can replace Bite with Drag Prey if it has a creature grappled.

While poisoned this way, the creature is also paralyzed. At the end of each of its turns, the target can repeat the save, ending both conditions on a success.

**Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (3d8 + 4) piercing damage.

**Drag Prey.** One creature grappled or paralyzed by the crawler must succeed on a **DC 15 Strength saving throw** or be pulled up to 15 ft. toward the crawler. If this movement would pull the creature over an edge, it may make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** to catch the edge or chain.

**Reaction**

**Cling and Twist.** When the crawler would be pushed, pulled, or knocked prone while on the chain or a wall, it can reduce the forced movement by 10 ft. and remain standing/clinging.

## DM Guidance — Using Hostile Encounters in Packet 6

Use only one of these hostile encounters in a normal Packet 6 run unless the party is moving very quickly or skipped most spirit interaction.

Recommended pacing:

1. Let the party first meet at least one benign spirit encounter.
2. Then introduce one clearly hostile creature encounter.
3. The contrast should teach that Stillpeak contains both victims and predators.

Best default choices:

- Use **Ice Mephit Squatters** if you want a shorter, more mobile fight near the refuge or cistern channels.
- Use **Chain-Lift Crawler** if you want Chapter 6's main combat set piece in the vertical shaft.

Avoid placing both encounters before the party has had time to understand the ghostly spirits. Too much combat too early will flatten the chapter into a dungeon crawl and weaken the intended moral distinction.

---

## Balance Notes

These encounters are scaled for a fresh five-character level 7 party.

The mephit encounter is a moderate nuisance fight unless terrain and death bursts are used aggressively.

The Chain-Lift Crawler encounter is the more serious option because paralysis and vertical terrain can become dangerous quickly. Use the crawler's Drag Prey to create tension, not to instantly remove a character from play. Give players chances to grab chains, catch ledges, cut tentacles, or stabilize each other.

Neither encounter should be presented as grief-born. They are hostile because they are predators or cruel squatters, not because they understand Stillpeak's tragedy.
