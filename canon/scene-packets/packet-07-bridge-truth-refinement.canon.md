## Canon Status

This file is a targeted refinement patch for Scene Packet 7 / Chapter 7: Bridgeward Way.

It preserves the Chapter 7 table-facing truth while setting up a deeper Chapter 8 reveal.

## Purpose

Chapter 7 must still play as the party's first clear discovery of the queen's fall.

The visible truth remains:

- The queen crossed the bridge during a sacred/civic procession.
- A sudden tremor struck.
- The queen fell into the hollow below.
- Witnesses saw the fall as a catastrophic earthquake event.
- The city, court, priests, guards, and common witnesses had no awareness of sabotage.
- Immediate responders scattered to warn the king, search below, pray, preserve order, and begin crisis response.

This file adds a hidden deeper truth for Chapter 8:

- The tremor was secretly induced by two trusted dwarven Stone Druid advisors.
- No normal witness, bridge spirit, city-dweller, or ordinary memory-echo knows this.
- The Queen's Bridge spirit scenes should not reveal the sabotage.
- Chapter 8 may later expose the hidden cause through deeper royal, druidic, crystal, or throneward evidence.

## Refined Hidden Truth

The king once trusted two dwarven Stone Druid confidants:

- **Dhurak Stonevein, the Fault-Tender**
- **Veyra Deeproot, the Root-Seer**

They were not originally evil. They were self-deceived heretics who believed they had discovered forbidden mountain wisdom in a rare Abbathor-touched crystal seam.

Their corruption began as ambition: the desire for deeper authority over the mountain, greater command of stone and crystal, and power beyond the limits of their station.

Abbathor's influence did not seize them all at once. It tempted what was already present.

Dhurak and Veyra secretly used their stonecraft, druidic rites, and crystal resonance to induce the tremor beneath the Queen's Bridge during the sacred procession. To everyone present, it appeared to be a sudden natural earthquake.

The queen's death was therefore not publicly understood as murder, betrayal, sabotage, or ritual violence. It was remembered as a tragic tremor.

That is the only truth Chapter 7 should reveal.

## Chapter 7 Presentation Rule

When running Chapter 7:

- Do **not** imply that witnesses saw sabotage.
- Do **not** have spirits accuse Dhurak, Veyra, druids, priests, geomancers, or the king.
- Do **not** show visible spellcasting, ritual circles, conspirators, or malicious actors during the bridge fall memory.
- Do **not** present the queen's fall as assassination, battlefield action, or open betrayal.
- Do **not** reveal Abbathor's involvement.

Instead, emphasize:

- sudden stone movement
- bridge lurching
- loss of footing
- failed hands reaching
- torn ceremonial cloth
- lanterns going white
- immediate civic panic
- honest witness confusion
- desperate rescue response

The Chapter 7 emotional truth remains sincere: the city experienced the fall as disaster, not conspiracy.

## Chapter 8 Reveal Rule

Chapter 8 may reveal that the public truth was incomplete.

Possible reveal sources:

- sealed Stone Druid records
- crystal-memory strata below the bridge
- royal mourning chamber inscriptions
- Abbathor-tainted mineral growths
- Dhurak or Veyra's surviving consciousness within their monstrous forms
- the Mourning King's fragmented clarity
- throneward evidence showing mining orders, crystal accumulation, and post-fall manipulation

The reveal should land as:

> The bridge memory was not false. It was incomplete.
>
> The queen did fall during a tremor.
>
> The city did believe it was an earthquake.
>
> But the tremor was made.

## Post-Fall Historical Chain

Use this as the refined causal chain from Chapter 7 into Chapter 8:

1. The queen crosses the bridge during a sacred/civic procession.
2. Dhurak and Veyra secretly induce a tremor through Abbathor-touched crystal resonance.
3. The queen falls; witnesses understand the event as a sudden earthquake.
4. Immediate responders scatter to warn the king, seek ropes and lanterns, descend toward the cavern floor, pray, and manage panic.
5. The king enters maddening grief and denial.
6. Dhurak and Veyra exploit his mourning, convincing him that deeper crystal mining can recover, preserve, or return the queen.
7. The king orders or permits expanded mining of the corrupted seam.
8. Crystal accumulation reaches critical mass in the royal mourning / mountain-heart region.
9. The implosion transforms Dhurak and Veyra into enormous crystal-earth monsters and unnaturally sustains the king.
10. The transformed druids keep the king alive through visible sustaining beams, using him as a grief-engine.
11. Tremors, energy pulses, hostile crystal growths, preserved dead, and impossible mourning phenomena devastate the kingdom.
12. The surviving dwarves attempt containment and then evacuate the mountain in stages.

## Design Boundary

This refinement changes the deep cause of the queen's fall but does not invalidate the Chapter 7 bridge reveal.

Chapter 7 answers:

**What did the witnesses experience?**

Chapter 8 answers:

**Who made that experience happen, and how did grief become a mountain-wide catastrophe?**
