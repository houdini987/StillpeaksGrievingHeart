## Tone Targets

Chapter 8 should feel:

- royal but ruined
- tragic but culpable
- crystal-saturated
- morally heavy
- final-act direct
- not exposition-heavy at the boss fight
- grief and preservation, not undead evil

## Transition Cues

### From Queen's Bridge to Throneward Descent

> The bridge remains behind you, but the fall does not.
>
> It follows in the stone.
>
> Every royal marker ahead points down.

### Crystals Intensify

> The crystals here no longer look like seams in the stone.
>
> The stone looks like something the crystals have chosen to keep for shape.

### No City Crawl Reminder

> The side passages are cracked, sealed, or swallowed by crystal growth.
>
> The royal route remains open.
>
> Not safe.
>
> Open.

## Lore Reveal Cues

### Dhurak and Veyra Introduced

> Their names repeat too often to be ceremonial.
>
> Dhurak Stonevein.
> Veyra Deeproot.
>
> Not witnesses.
> Advisors.

### They Were Trusted

> The carvings place them near the king's seal.
>
> Not beneath it.
> Beside it.
>
> Trusted enough to stand near grief when grief first became dangerous.

### Druid Self-Deception

> The symbols do not read like villainy.
>
> That is what makes them worse.
>
> They read like certainty.

### Hidden Tremor Truth

> The bridge memory was not false.
>
> The queen fell during a tremor.
>
> But here, beneath the royal road, the stone remembers hands that helped the mountain move.

### King's Greed for Restoration

> The decree does not ask whether death can be undone.
>
> It commands the mountain to answer as if the question has already been settled.

### Critical Mass

> The crystal did not bloom.
>
> It detonated inward.
>
> A hunger so dense it became a heart.

## Partial Success Phrasing

### Advisor Records

**Success:**

> You understand the titles clearly: these were Stone Druid confidants trusted with royal counsel.

**Partial:**

> You cannot read every formal marker, but their names sit too close to the king's seal to be minor.

**Failure:**

> The exact titles resist you, but the chamber keeps repeating two names as if the mountain wants them remembered.

### Druid-Centric Reveal

**Success:**

> The sequence is clear: crystal, fault, bridge, tremor, concealment.

**Partial:**

> You cannot prove every step, but the carvings do not show prediction. They show preparation.

**Failure:**

> You do not understand the whole mechanism, but you know the room is not mourning the tremor. It is confessing to it.

### Mining Decree

**Success:**

> The king was manipulated, but the orders were his.

**Partial:**

> Grief fills the language, but so does command.

**Failure:**

> You cannot untangle motive from madness, but every decree points deeper.

## Final Arena Entry Cues

Use if the table needs more than, or a shorter version of, the main read-aloud in `packet-08-final-encounter.canon.md`.

### Geode Ground-Zero Reveal

> The doorway gives you a wall.
>
> The chamber beyond gives you a wound.
>
> Everything curves away from the center: floor, ceiling, broken reliefs, dead lamps, crystal scars.
>
> Whatever happened here did not fill a room.
>
> It made one.

### Central Engine Cue

> The throne is not at the end of the chamber.
>
> It is the center of it.
>
> Every crack, every root, every beam, every line of green-white light points inward.

### Obvious Druid Protection Cue

> The beams do not come from the king.
>
> They burn into him.
>
> Dhurak and Veyra stand angled around the throne, their bodies between you and the cage of crystal.
>
> Not servants awaiting command.
>
> Wardens protecting a vessel.

### Inert King Cue

> The king does not rise.
>
> He cannot.
>
> His hands are fused to the throne, his crown webbed into the crystal behind him.
>
> For a moment, his eyes find you through the light.
>
> Then the beams tighten, and the Druids move.

## Final Encounter Round Cues

Use at initiative count 20 or when pacing needs pressure.

### Full Engine Cue

> The green beams flare.
>
> The radial scars along the geode wall answer in light.
>
> The king does not command it.
>
> He endures it.
>
> Dhurak and Veyra do not look at him with loyalty.
>
> They look at him like a furnace they refuse to let go cold.

### Dhurak Cue

> Dhurak moves, and the round chamber remembers the bridge.
>
> Not as history.
>
> As threat.

### Veyra Cue

> Veyra's roots spread through the central floor in delicate, terrible lines.
>
> Nothing here wants to release what it holds.

### King Vessel Cue

> The king's head lifts against the crystal restraint.
>
> His mouth opens.
>
> No command comes out.
>
> Only a breath shaped like a name you do not know.

### Druid Interception Cue

> The attack never reaches the king.
>
> The nearest green beam snaps taut, and the Druid titan tears itself sideways into the blow.
>
> Crystal splits. Stone screams.
>
> The king remains pinned to the throne, untouched — not because he defended himself, but because they will break themselves before they let the vessel fail.

### One Beam Broken

> One green beam snaps into sparks.
>
> The king jerks against the central throne.
>
> Somewhere under the engine, something mortal gasps.
>
> The surviving Druid turns more fully toward him.
>
> Protective.
>
> Desperate.

### Both Beams Broken

> The second beam breaks.
>
> The geode chamber goes quiet enough to hear stone cooling.

## King's Clarity Window Lines

Use sparingly. Do not monologue.

> "What did I do?"

> "Where is she? No. No, I know. I knew. I would not know."

> "Do not let me become the mountain again."

> "If there is mercy left in this place, make it swift."

> "I would have kept her from death. Instead I kept death from finishing me."

> "End it. Before the grief remembers how to wear my voice."

## Mercy Death Cues

### If the Party Grants Mercy

> The king dies like a mortal.
>
> Not as stone.
> Not as thunder.
> Not as a law the mountain must obey.
>
> Just a grieving man, finally allowed to end.

### Mountain Release

> The crystals do not shatter all at once.
>
> They dim like embers denied air.
>
> Far away, somewhere above and below you at the same time, the mountain exhales.

### Spirits Release

> The spirits do not cheer.
>
> They lower their heads.
>
> Then, one by one, they stop repeating.

## Slim Salvation Cues

### Relic Presented

> The relic does not blaze.
>
> It steadies.
>
> In this room of hunger, that is almost impossible.

### Salvation Success

> The crystal releases him not because it is defeated, but because for one breath he stops asking it to hold him.

### Salvation Failure, Mercy Still Open

> The relic trembles in your hand.
>
> It is not enough to save him.
>
> But it is enough for him to understand why he must end.

## Emergency Pacing Buttons

### If the Party Is Stuck on Lore

> The route ahead answers the question more clearly than the room behind you can.
>
> The crystals pulse downward.
>
> The king's chamber is near.

### If the Party Wants More Exploration

> Side chambers exist, but they are sealed, collapsed, or consumed by crystal.
>
> The royal road is the only path still willing to be walked.

### If the Party Tries to Negotiate with the Bound King Before Breaking Beams

> His eyes move.
>
> That much of him is still there.
>
> But when he tries to answer, the beams brighten, and the words collapse into a low sound of grief.
>
> Whatever truth remains in him is trapped behind the engine.

### If the Party Is Unsure Whether to Target the Druids

> The answer is visible in the light.
>
> The beams do not protect the Druids.
>
> The Druids protect the beams.
>
> And the beams hold the king.
