## Tone Targets

Chapter 8 should feel:

- royal but ruined
- tragic but culpable
- crystal-saturated
- morally heavy
- final-act direct
- not exposition-heavy at the boss fight

## Transition Cues

### From Queen's Bridge to Throneward Descent

> The bridge remains behind you, but the fall does not.
>
> It follows in the stone.
>
> Every royal marker ahead points down.

### Crystals Intensify

> The crystals here no longer look like seams in the stone.
>
> The stone looks like something the crystals have chosen to keep for shape.

### No City Crawl Reminder

> The side passages are cracked, sealed, or swallowed by crystal growth.
>
> The royal route remains open.
>
> Not safe.
>
> Open.

## Lore Reveal Cues

### Dhurak and Veyra Introduced

> Their names repeat too often to be ceremonial.
>
> Dhurak Stonevein.
> Veyra Deeproot.
>
> Not witnesses.
> Advisors.

### They Were Trusted

> The carvings place them near the king's seal.
>
> Not beneath it.
> Beside it.
>
> Trusted enough to stand near grief when grief first became dangerous.

### Druid Self-Deception

> The symbols do not read like villainy.
>
> That is what makes them worse.
>
> They read like certainty.

### Hidden Tremor Truth

> The bridge memory was not false.
>
> The queen fell during a tremor.
>
> But here, beneath the royal road, the stone remembers hands that helped the mountain move.

### King's Greed for Restoration

> The decree does not ask whether death can be undone.
>
> It commands the mountain to answer as if the question has already been settled.

### Critical Mass

> The crystal did not bloom.
>
> It detonated inward.
>
> A hunger so dense it became a heart.

## Partial Success Phrasing

### Advisor Records

**Success:**

> You understand the titles clearly: these were Stone Druid confidants trusted with royal counsel.

**Partial:**

> You cannot read every formal marker, but their names sit too close to the king's seal to be minor.

**Failure:**

> The exact titles resist you, but the chamber keeps repeating two names as if the mountain wants them remembered.

### Druid-Centric Reveal

**Success:**

> The sequence is clear: crystal, fault, bridge, tremor, concealment.

**Partial:**

> You cannot prove every step, but the carvings do not show prediction. They show preparation.

**Failure:**

> You do not understand the whole mechanism, but you know the room is not mourning the tremor. It is confessing to it.

### Mining Decree

**Success:**

> The king was manipulated, but the orders were his.

**Partial:**

> Grief fills the language, but so does command.

**Failure:**

> You cannot untangle motive from madness, but every decree points deeper.

## King's Clarity Window Lines

Use sparingly. Do not monologue.

> "What did I do?"

> "Where is she? No. No, I know. I knew. I would not know."

> "Do not let me become the mountain again."

> "If there is mercy left in this place, make it swift."

> "I would have kept her from death. Instead I kept death from finishing me."

## Mercy Death Cues

### If the Party Grants Mercy

> The king dies like a mortal.
>
> Not as stone.
> Not as thunder.
> Not as a law the mountain must obey.
>
> Just a grieving man, finally allowed to end.

### Mountain Release

> The crystals do not shatter all at once.
>
> They dim like embers denied air.
>
> Far away, somewhere above and below you at the same time, the mountain exhales.

### Spirits Release

> The spirits do not cheer.
>
> They lower their heads.
>
> Then, one by one, they stop repeating.

## Slim Salvation Cues

### Relic Presented

> The relic does not blaze.
>
> It steadies.
>
> In this room of hunger, that is almost impossible.

### Salvation Success

> The crystal releases him not because it is defeated, but because for one breath he stops asking it to hold him.

### Salvation Failure, Mercy Still Open

> The relic trembles in your hand.
>
> It is not enough to save him.
>
> But it is enough for him to understand why he must end.

## Emergency Pacing Buttons

### If the Party Is Stuck on Lore

> The route ahead answers the question more clearly than the room behind you can.
>
> The crystals pulse downward.
>
> The king's chamber is near.

### If the Party Wants More Exploration

> Side chambers exist, but they are sealed, collapsed, or consumed by crystal.
>
> The royal route is the only path still shaped like an answer.

### If the Final Fight Is Too Hard

- Reduce beam absorption.
- Delay or skip one boss recharge power.
- Let a relic suppress one round of Final Attunement Crisis.
- Let destroying one beam visibly weaken both the king and the surviving Druid.

### If the Final Fight Is Too Easy

- Trigger a beam flare hazard.
- Let Dhurak create more difficult terrain.
- Let Veyra restrain one high-mobility character.
- Increase the king's control pressure, not raw damage.
