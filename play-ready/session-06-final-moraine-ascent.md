## 2. Opening Read-Aloud + Timer Explanation

### Read-Aloud

The slope ahead is not a path. It is what remains after too many paths failed.

Broken anchors jut from the scree. A snapped length of rope appears from under ice and vanishes again beneath sliding stone. Bone, gear, and collapse debris surface and sink by turns as if the mountain is slowly sorting what it means to keep.

The moraine rises above you in broken bands of stone and dirty ice. At the top, through blowing frost and fractured air, the peak waits close enough to feel reachable and far enough to feel cruel.

Then the ground shifts.

Not in a great avalanche. Not yet.

It settles, tightens, and settles again, as if the mountain is breathing beneath the debris.

The Tremorscope pulses sharply, out of rhythm with your own heartbeat.

There is a rhythm here.

And as you stand still long enough to notice it, the stone beneath your boots moves just slightly out from under you.

Not enough to fall.

Enough to understand.

You are not climbing a slope.

You are moving across something that will not hold still for you.

### Say to Players While Placing Timer

The mountain has a rhythm. I am putting a timer on the table. Each time it runs out, the mountain acts, whether you are ready or not.

You can move, fight, investigate, help each other, cast spells, or improvise. Nothing is forbidden. But standing still is no longer neutral. If you move decisively, you stay ahead of the moraine. If you hesitate, it catches up.

## 4. Timer, Altitude, and Escalation

### Timer Rule

Use a 2-minute timer. When it expires, the mountain acts. Reset immediately.

If players are resolving a declared action, apply the consequence after that action. If they are debating without commitment, interrupt immediately.

### Mountain Acts - Quick Consequence Menu

Use consequences appropriate to the current progress band.

| Progress | Timer Consequence |
|---|---|
| 0-1 | Scree shifts; one PC loses footing, falls behind, or loses 10 ft movement. |
| 2 | Oxygen spike; one PC makes CON DC 13 or loses reaction / movement. |
| 3 | Dead contact; most isolated/rear/prone PC is grabbed or slowed. |
| 4 | Ice fracture; forced reposition or slide risk. |
| 5 | Summit seam shifts; group stabilization DC 16 or added cost to open door. |

### Altitude Sickness - Separate from Mountain Toll

Altitude Sickness is internal degradation. Mountain Toll is external environmental pressure.

**Phase 1 - Breath Debt**
- Trigger: Beat 1.
- First time each character dashes, climbs aggressively, or corrects a failed movement action in a pressure cycle: CON DC 12 or lose 10 ft on next movement.

**Phase 2 - Oxygen Lag**
- Trigger: Beat 3.
- Speed reduced by 10 ft while in the moraine.
- If a character both moves and uses a bonus action in the same pressure cycle: CON DC 13 or lose reaction until next timer reset.

**Phase 3 - Summit Hypoxia**
- Trigger: Beat 6.
- Disadvantage on STR/DEX movement checks unless aided, anchored, magically supported, or using secured lane.
- Once per character per cycle, failed movement check causes loss of remaining movement for that cycle.

### Anti-Combat Escalation

Start soft, escalate only if the party tries to hold ground.

1. **Soft:** Attacks break grips or clear one PC, but do not reduce overall pressure.
2. **Medium:** Each cycle spent primarily fighting increases next movement DC by +1 and causes another dead contact.
3. **Hard:** If they hold ground for multiple cycles, terrain collapses behind/beneath them. Force upward movement, separation, or group DEX save DC 16.

Use this line if needed:

> This is not a place you win. It is a place you cross.

## 5. Beat Run Sheet - Beats 1 to 3

### BEAT 1 - Field of Failed Steps

**Map zone:** Bottom 20 percent.

**Challenge:** Establish a stable ascent line through shattered scree and buried ice.

**Player decisions:** Who leads? Fast or careful? Rope/anchor or push? Tight formation or spread?

**Checks:**
- Group Survival or Athletics DC 14.
- Perception / Investigation DC 14 for failed routes and unstable pockets.
- CON DC 12 if pushing aggressively under Breath Debt.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress, workable formation.

**Failure:** Gain 1 progress but add one complication: lost footing, one PC behind, -10 ft movement, or separated starting lane.

**Spoken cues:**
- "The first step holds. The second step changes the first."
- "A bootprint you left a moment ago slides three feet downhill and vanishes under fresh gravel."

### BEAT 2 - The Shifting Lanes

**Map zone:** Lower-middle diagonal rubble bands.

**Challenge:** Choose a route while formation threatens to split.

**Checks:**
- Survival DC 15 to choose best line.
- Athletics / Acrobatics DC 15 to cross moving rubble.
- Assist DC 14 to keep slower PC from falling behind.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress and preserve formation.

**Failure:** Gain 1 progress but add complication: split formation, prone PC, lost reaction, or next movement DC +1.

**Timer consequence:** Lane collapses behind rear-most or most isolated PC.

**Spoken cues:**
- "The safe-looking lane does not collapse. It turns."
- "You can keep the group together, or you can take the cleanest path. The moraine does not offer both."

### BEAT 3 - The Burden Ridge

**Map zone:** Central outcrops.

**Trigger:** Altitude Phase 2 begins.

**Challenge:** Maintain cohesion while movement and breathing desynchronize.

**Player posture choices:** Advance, Stabilize, Assist, Read Tremorscope, Fight/Guard.

**Checks:**
- Athletics DC 15 to climb ridge.
- CON DC 13 for Oxygen Lag when moving + bonus action.
- Survival / Perception DC 15 to keep shared line.
- Arcana / Investigation / Religion DC 15 to interpret Tremorscope.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress. Tremorscope suggests summit signal is starting to answer from inside the upper mountain.

**Failure:** If fewer than half succeed and no meaningful resources are spent, remain here and trigger complication.

**Subtle dead only:** Hands under scree, skull under ice, rope tightening then vanishing. No clear attack targets yet.

**Spoken cues:**
- "Your breath arrives late, as if your body has to be reminded what it is for."
- "Something under the scree moves with you for three steps, then stops when you stop."

## 6. Beat Run Sheet - Beats 4 to 6

### BEAT 4 - The Dead Field

**Map zone:** Upper-middle open pockets.

**Challenge:** Cross through accumulated dead without trying to clear the field.

**Checks:**
- Group Athletics / Acrobatics / Survival DC 15 to push through.
- WIS save DC 14 to resist fixation, panic, or answering the dead.
- STR or DEX save DC 14 to escape a grip quickly.
- Radiant damage, fire, Turn Undead, or similar power reduces pressure for one cycle.

**Success:** Gain 1 progress. Dead are behind/beneath them, not defeated.

**Failure:** Add complication and dead contact: speed reduced, prone, separated, or disadvantage next movement check.

**Read-aloud:**

The scree ahead shivers in a pattern too deliberate to be settling.

Stone rolls aside. A hand comes up first, wrapped in ice and gravel. Then another, farther upslope. Then a shoulder, a jaw, the bent frame of someone still tangled in climbing rope.

They do not rise like warriors.

They rise like consequences.

Every shape that surfaces is turned uphill. Every broken hand reaches not for your throat, but for your boots, your ropes, your packs, your hesitation. The mountain is not sending them to kill you quickly.

It is showing you what stops here.

And then the ground behind you drops six inches with a sound like a door being barred.

**If they try to fight:**

You can strike them. You can break hands, split skulls, burn the frost from old bones.

But the dead are not holding this ground.

The ground is holding them.

Every moment you spend proving you can win a fight, the moraine proves this is not a battlefield. It shifts under your stance, closes behind your heels, and makes the next step upward harder than the last.

This is not a place you win.

It is a place you cross.

### BEAT 5 - The Glassing Slope

**Map zone:** Upper 20 percent.

**Challenge:** Cross reflective ice where sliding replaces sinking.

**Checks:**
- Acrobatics / Athletics DC 16 to cross.
- Survival / Perception DC 15 to find safe lanes.
- Assist DC 15 to prevent slide or overcommitment.
- Insight / Religion / Arcana / Survival DC 14 for reflection moment.

**Reflection moment:**

The ice catches each climber for a heartbeat. Not a perfect reflection. A burdened one. Each face looks older, lower, closer to the stone than it should. For a moment, the ice does not show who you are. It shows what this climb has been taking from you.

**Failure:** Does not block progress; adds slide, separation, lost movement, or disadvantage on next movement/Tremorscope check.

**Spoken cues:**
- "The ice gives you room to move and then punishes the first step taken too quickly."
- "The reflection beneath you lags half a breath behind your body."

### BEAT 6 - The Summit Face

**Map zone:** Top edge / off-map wall.

**Trigger:** Altitude Phase 3 begins.

**Challenge:** Confirm and open the entrance while hypoxia and terrain worsen.

**Read-aloud:**

The moraine ends badly.

It does not flatten into a summit. It rises into a wall of black stone and dirty ice, too sheer to be natural and too weathered to be cleanly worked. The peak is above you, but the Tremorscope no longer points up.

It points forward.

Then it answers.

A pulse travels from the device into the stone, and a second pulse returns from within the mountain itself. Not an echo. A reply.

Under the ice, lines appear where no lines should be: vertical seams, old geometric cuts, a lintel buried beneath rime, dwarven stonework hidden under the mountain's own scar tissue.

The summit is not the destination.

It is the door.

**Checks:**
- Arcana / Investigation / Religion / Tremorscope DC 15 to confirm seam.
- Athletics DC 16 to clear ice and stone.
- Dwarvish / History / Stonecunning-style logic / tool use DC 15 to read construction.
- Group stabilization DC 16 if timer expires before door opens.

**Door opens after any two:**
- Tremorscope aligned to returning pulse.
- Ice cleared from central seam.
- Dwarven construction recognized.
- Radiant/thunder/fire/force applied carefully to resonance line.
- PC accepts cost: fatigue, damage, lost gear, or holding position.

**Door opening read-aloud:**

The seam does not crack open all at once.

First, the ice over it fractures in a single clean line. Then the stone beneath answers with a sound too deep to be heard only by the ears. It moves through your boots, your teeth, your ribs, and the Tremorscope shakes so hard it seems for a moment it might break apart.

The mountain exhales.

Not wind.

Air from within.

Warmer than the summit. Older than the storm.

The black seam widens into darkness, and beyond it stone steps descend into Stillpeak.
