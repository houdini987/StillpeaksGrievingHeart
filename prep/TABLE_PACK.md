### Phase 3 — Initiative Crossing

**Tremor Memory Track [CANON].** Starts at 0. Add 1 when:

- a creature fails a bridge movement check by 5 or more
- thunder or force damage is used carelessly against bridge stone or crystal
- a character shouts grief, panic, or the queen's name *(no name exists; count "the queen")*
- The Hollow Below uses Memory-Lure
- a creature falls prone or is restrained on a broken bridge section
- the DM wants the memory to escalate

At **3**, trigger one Tremor Memory Event and reset to 0. Choose or roll d6:

1. **Bridge Lurch:** All creatures on the bridge make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw** or fall prone and slide 5 feet toward the nearest edge or crack.
2. **Stone Shear:** A 10-foot section becomes difficult terrain. A creature in that section makes **DC 14 Acrobatics or Athletics** or takes 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.
3. **Ghost Overlap:** A spirit reenactment crosses through living bodies. Each creature in a 15-foot line makes **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw** or loses reactions until its next turn.
4. **Crystal Flare:** Crystal light pulses. Erny and Maelreth make **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or take 9 (2d8) psychic damage; others take 4 (1d8) on a failure.
5. **Gap Opens:** A crack widens. Crossing it requires **Athletics or Acrobatics DC 15**.
6. **The Fall Repeats:** Advance immediately to the next fall-memory spoken segment.

**Bridge movement checks [CANON].** Require one when a creature crosses a broken section, is pulled near an edge, dashes across unstable stone, helps another creature on a fractured span, or crosses through a Tremor Memory Event.

- **Acrobatics DC 14:** cross cracked or tilting stone.
- **Athletics DC 14:** leap, grab, or pull another creature back.
- **Survival DC 13:** identify the safest immediate route.
- **Perception DC 13:** spot a stable rail, handhold, or intact seam.
- Fail by 1–4: lose movement, fall prone, or take 1d6 bludgeoning/piercing.
- Fail by 5+: add 1 Tremor Memory and place the creature at an edge/gap risk.

**Counterplay to reward [CANON]:** rope; pitons or climber's kit; Resonance Chalk; Lift-Brake Pin or other dwarven tools (*Lift-Brake Pin: as an action, pin or stabilize one mechanical component; the next relevant Athletics, Investigation, or tool check has advantage; bends after one major use*); Soot-Black Prayer Bead during memory/fear/grief effects (*once: advantage on one Wisdom save, Religion, or Insight check*); teamwork actions to anchor or steady each other.

**Round cues at initiative 20 [CANON]**, one per round:

> **A.** Beneath the fight, the bridge continues its memory. Spirit feet move through living legs. Lanterns pass through broken stone. The queen walks toward a place where the bridge is no longer whole.

> **B.** The darkness under the bridge does not join the memory. It feeds beside it. Its tendrils climb through cracks like roots seeking warmth.

> **C.** Across the span, the two crystal creatures skitter along the far-side stone, their bodies ticking in time with the bridge's pulse.

Situational cues [CANON]: initiative begins, *"The bridge does not become a battlefield. It becomes a memory with teeth."* Reeled toward an edge, *"Your boots scrape stone. The edge comes closer in pieces: crack, rail, open air, nothing below it. Make the save."* Bright light under the span, *"The light reaches under the span and the darkness recoils. For the first time, it looks less like stone and more like something hiding behind stone."* If the fight is overwhelming the story: *"Under the violence, the memory continues. The queen is still walking. The tremor has not happened yet. The bridge is making you wait for it."*

### The Hollow Below [CANON stat block]

*Large aberration, chaotic evil.* Ancient foreign predator nested under the bridge after the fall. Not the grief-source; "an opportunistic tenant." Clings to the underside; reaches through cracks and over edges.

**AC** 15 (natural armor) · **HP** 138 (12d10 + 72) · **Speed** 10 ft., climb 30 ft.
STR 19 (+4) · DEX 8 (−1) · CON 22 (+6) · INT 11 (+0) · WIS 16 (+3) · CHA 8 (−1)
**Saves** Str +7, Con +9, Wis +6 · **Skills** Perception +9, Stealth +5
**Resistances** cold, necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks while in dim light or darkness · **Condition immunities** prone
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., passive Perception 19 · **Languages** understands Deep Speech and Dwarvish but cannot speak

- **False Span.** While motionless on an underside, ceiling, ledge, or overhang, indistinguishable from hanging shadow, mineral growth, and old cracked stone.
- **Underbridge Predator.** Climbs difficult surfaces, including upside down, without checks. Advantage on Stealth while on the underside of the Queen's Bridge or inside a fissure.
- **Many Grasping Tendrils.** Up to six tendrils active. Each can be attacked separately (AC 15; 15 HP; immune to poison and psychic). Destroying a tendril deals no damage to the Hollow Below but releases its grapple. A destroyed tendril regrows at the end of the Hollow Below's next turn **unless it has taken radiant damage since the start of that turn.**
- **Foreign to the Mountain.** The attuned pair sense it as an absence or cold gap. No sympathy.
- **Light-Hating Lair.** Bright light within 20 feet suppresses its nonmagical B/P/S resistance until the start of its next turn.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Four Tendril attacks, uses Reel, then one Chasm Bite against a creature within reach if possible.
- **Tendril.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 50 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) bludgeoning, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15). Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained, and that tendril can't be used on another target.
- **Reel.** Pulls each grappled creature up to 20 feet straight toward itself. If this would pull a creature over an edge, through a broken span, or into a dangerous gap, it may make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw** to catch the edge, seize a railing, or grab stone. Success: stops at the edge and falls prone. Failure: pulled into the hazardous position the DM determines.
- **Chasm Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d8 + 4) piercing plus 7 (2d6) necrotic. Advantage if the target is grappled by it.
- **Memory-Lure (Recharge 5–6).** Up to three creatures within 60 feet that can see or hear the bridge memory make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. Failure: 14 (4d6) psychic and must use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed toward the nearest visible edge, fall-point, or reenactment figure. It isn't forced to step directly into a fall unless no other movement is possible. Success: half damage, no movement.

**Bonus Action — Extinguish the Witness-Light.** Dims light within 30 feet. Nonmagical flames go out unless shielded; magical light is reduced to half radius until the end of its next turn.

**Reaction — Underbridge Retreat.** When it takes 20+ damage from one attack or spell, it shifts up to 15 feet along the underside without provoking. Cannot move onto the bridge top.

**Bloodied (69 HP or fewer):** Tendril reach drops to 40 ft.; Chasm Bite +4 (1d8) piercing; loses False Span.

**Defeat / retreat:** at 0 HP it collapses into the chasm. If driven below 30 HP and the party stops pursuing, it may retreat into the depths and does not return this chapter.

**Running it [CANON + REC]:** Its job is to restrain, pull, isolate, and make the crossing terrifying; don't play it as a stationary bag of hit points. **[REC]** Its body is targetable by a creature at an edge or over a fissure (half cover). ### C9.3 Bowl-slope terrain [CANON]

- **Center / throne-engine zone:** normal movement unless roots, beams, or boss powers say otherwise.
- **Lower slopes:** difficult terrain.
- **Upper slopes / side walls:** difficult terrain plus unstable footing at DM discretion.
- **Check only when position matters** (ending a turn high on the wall, dashing/jumping/climbing a steep slope, shoved/pulled/prone/tremored on a slope, holding high ground): **Athletics DC 13** (brace, climb, hold) · **Acrobatics DC 13** (balance across jagged curve) · **Dexterity saving throw DC 14** (avoid sliding after a tremor, shove, beam flare, or shard impact).
- **On failure, the least fussy fit:** slide 10 feet toward center; fall prone; lose 10 feet of movement; drop from upper to lower slope and take 3 (1d6) only if visually meaningful.
- Don't punish melee characters for approaching the center. "The center should be dangerous because of the Druids and beams, not because every step requires a roll."
- **Dhurak weaponizes the bowl:** when he uses Fault-Tender Stomp, Bridgefall Pull, or a major tremor, creatures on slopes may need the slope check or **DC 14 Dexterity** to avoid sliding 10 feet toward center and/or falling prone. Cue: *"Dhurak strikes the floor, and the bowl answers. The crystal slope flashes beneath your feet. For one sickening breath, the whole chamber wants to pour you back toward the throne."*

### C9.4 Beams, vessel protection, interception [CANON]

**Both beams active:** King inert; both Druids active; King protected by beam absorption and Druid interception; full Final Attunement Crisis; Attuned Offense Backlash live.

**Attacks on the King:**
1. A living Druid titan **intercepts by default** (the one whose beam best fits the visual path). The attack/effect is redirected to that Druid, or the Druid takes the damage/effect in the King's place. No reaction required unless using the stricter version (it spends its reaction; if it has none, the King gets beam protection only).
2. If damage still reaches the King: **both beams absorb the first 30 damage he takes each round**; **one beam: first 15**; **both broken: none.**
3. Make the interception visibly sacrificial.

Warning before the attack [CANON]: *"The line is open only in the way a trap is open. Both beams tighten when your weapon turns toward him. Dhurak and Veyra shift at once, not to save themselves, but to put their bodies between you and the throne."*

### C9.5 Erny and Maelreth — over-attunement [CANON]

**Final Attunement Crisis (both beams active).** At **initiative count 20 each round**, each makes a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. Disadvantage on saves against Dhurak's or Veyra's crystal-resonance effects. *(The "Mourning King's grief-resonance effects" clause is void: A4-C4.)* On a failure, the DM chooses one:
1. Take 7 (2d6) psychic damage.
2. Lose reactions until the start of their next turn.
3. The first 10 feet of movement on their next turn must be toward the nearest active beam, crystal node, or the king.
4. Disadvantage on the first attack roll or ability check before the end of their next turn.

On a success: they resist and may sense one useful tactical truth about the arena, a beam, or a boss.

**One beam broken:** DC drops to **13**; psychic option drops to **3 (1d6)**; no automatic disadvantage against the destroyed Druid's effects; backlash weakens or becomes discretionary. **Both broken:** crisis ends.

**Attuned Offense Backlash.** While at least one beam is active, if **Erny or Maelreth** uses an offensive action, attack, damaging spell, smite, or damaging class feature against **Dhurak, Veyra, or the King**, resolve it first (even if intercepted), then:
- **At most once per round** (not per hit, not per Eldritch Blast beam).
- All crystals flash white-green; the bowl tremors; shards fall.
- **All party members make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw.** Failure: 7 (2d6) piercing/force. Success: half.
- Creatures on slopes may also check for Bowl-Slope Slide.
- **One beam active:** may reduce to 3 (1d6) or limit to exposed areas. **Both broken:** ends.
- Optional escalation only if the party is cruising: 10 (3d6) and a blinding flash (failed save → disadvantage on the next attack roll). Never when several PCs are low.

Cue [CANON]: *"The moment your attack bites into the engine, every crystal in the chamber notices. The geode ignites. For one instant there is no room, no throne, no floor — only white-green light. Then the mountain shakes, and shards begin to fall."*

**"The first backlash should feel like discovery, not punishment."** Trigger it once in Round 1 if one of them attacks, then explain the pattern in narration.

**Ally counterplay [CANON].** Any ally, as an action, steadies an over-attuned character within 5 feet: **Persuasion DC 14** (call them back by name) · **Medicine DC 14** (breathing, pain, posture) · **Religion DC 15** (mourning restraint, release, anti-possession language) · **Arcana DC 15** (disrupt the resonance pattern) · **Athletics DC 13** (physically hold them back from a pull). On success the target gains one: advantage on their next Crisis save; immediate end of one movement-pull; resistance to the next psychic damage before the end of their next turn; reroll one failed save against a beam/crystal effect before the end of their next turn; or (DM discretion) their next offensive act triggers reduced or no backlash. Failure doesn't worsen anything.

**Relic counterplay [CANON].** Soot-Black Prayer Bead, either torn procession cloth (A0.5), Queen's Lantern Charm. As an action, reduce pressure on one over-attuned character: auto-succeed the next Crisis save; **or** clear one affliction consequence; **or** advantage on saves against beam/crystal effects until end of next round *(A4-C4 reading)*; **or** their next offensive act triggers reduced backlash. Powerful but limited by action, proximity, or one-use resonance. Not a puzzle.

**Grounded Silence [CANON — playability patch].** Triggers if the party creates a release-focused grounding moment with **at least two** of: (1) a mourning/restraint relic (bead, cloth, or lantern charm); (2) an ally steadies Erny or Maelreth via ally counterplay; (3) Erny or Maelreth gives up offense for the round to resist the beam; (4) one Druid beam already broken; (5) a character frames the act as release, restraint, or letting go; (6) a successful **Religion, Arcana, Persuasion, or Medicine DC 15**.
**Effect** on one over-attuned character (both if the party spent real action economy), until the start of that character's next turn: no Crisis save; next offensive action doesn't trigger full backlash (reduce to one-beam version or suppress); acts with clear agency, no movement pull. Never longer than one round unless both beams are broken. Cue: *"For one breath, the green light loses its grip on you. The pressure is still there. The grief is still there. But it is outside you again. You can choose what you do next."*

**Failure tone [CANON].** Intrusive, not punitive. Lines: *The throne breathes through your ribs.* · *You know the queen is gone, but your body wants to walk toward the lie that she can return.* · *The green beam hums in your teeth like a second heartbeat.* · *For one instant, the king's grief feels like your own memory.* · *Your power lands, but the room answers like you struck every crystal at once.* **Avoid** long forced-control scenes, repeated lost turns, benching a player, or backlash on every attack.

### C9.7 Dhurak Stonevein, the Fault-Tender [CANON]

Stone Golem chassis for AC/HP/saves only; no Magic Resistance, Slow, or weapon immunity (A0.4). **HP ~180, AC ~17. Legendary Resistance 1/day** (fault-lines brace him; the beam locks him back into place). **No multiattack, no druid spell list** (the beam consumes his concentration; destroying him is the only way to break it). Beam: while Dhurak lives, one beam stays active.

- **Stone Druid Strike (action).** One melee attack with the chassis attack bonus. *Hit:* about 16–18 bludgeoning/piercing + crystal force. Target makes **DC 15 Strength saving throw** or is pushed 10 feet, preferably downhill toward the center, a crack, or a beam lane.
- **Fault-Tender Stomp (Recharge 5–6).** 30-foot line or 15-foot cone. **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw.** Fail: 21 (6d6) bludgeoning/piercing and prone. Success: half, not prone. Area becomes difficult terrain; slope checks may follow.
- **Fault Resonance (reaction, max once/round, only while his beam is active).** Trigger: 20+ damage from one source, a crit, or a focused strike. One cracked floor line within 30 feet trembles; one creature on or near it makes **DC 14 Dexterity**. Fail: slides 10 feet toward center or falls prone. Skip it if Stomp already made the round busy.

### C9.9 The Mourning King (inert boss)

**Brian's ruling (A0.6), consistent with canon:** the King is a boss on the map. He has a token and **can take damage**, but he is **visibly inactive**: he never rises, moves, dodges, casts, speaks a command, or takes a turn. The two Druid titans **sacrifice themselves so he isn't injured.**

- **Any attack or effect aimed at him, area effects included,** is taken by a living Druid in his place (interception, C9.4). Make it visibly sacrificial every time.
- **Damage reaches him** only if something slips past interception (for example, if you use the stricter reaction-based version and the Druid has spent its reaction). Beam absorption then applies first: 30 per round with both beams, 15 with one.
- **[REC] The fight can't kill him.** While any beam holds, he can't drop below 1 HP; the beams hold him at the edge. His death belongs to the clarity window, as mercy.
- **Token:** Lich sheet for AC, HP, and saves only. [REC] 135 HP. No lich traits, spells, legendary actions, or legendary resistances.
- **Showing he's inactive [CANON cue]:**

> The king does not rise.
>
> He cannot.
>
> His hands are fused to the throne, his crown webbed into the crystal behind him.
>
> For a moment, his eyes find you through the light.
>
> Then the beams tighten, and the Druids move.

- **If damage does reach him:** see F5 for a [NEW] line. He shows pain, never power.

**Canon background:**

**Not undead, not fiend, not celestial, not a clean evil aura.** A living-preserved monarch, throne-bound and crystal-sustained. **Divine Sense read:** overwhelming grief, ancient royal will, crystal preservation, oath-pressure, refusal to release; wrongness, but not a clean evil aura. *(Dhurak and Veyra are also not undead or fiends.)*

While any beam is active he cannot move, takes **no turns**, has **0 legendary actions, 0 legendary resistances, no spells**, and does **not** command the Druids. Any magic near him comes from the engine, beams, arena, or Druids. **He can speak:** at the top of the round or initiative 20, one short fragment (plea, denial, queen-memory, clarity flash, or the throne using old royal language through him). Never tactics, never orders. Canon cue: *"The king's head lifts against the crystal restraint. His mouth opens. No command comes out. Only a breath shaped like a name you do not know."*

**Never:** phylactery language, soul-devouring, undead hunger, lich-duelist behavior, Power Word or Disintegrate effects, a second caster phase after the beams break.

### C9.10 Round script [CANON — playability patch]

>
> The King jerks forward in the throne.
>
> The pressure on Erny and Maelreth eases, but it does not end.
>

Mechanics: Crisis DC 15 → 13; psychic 2d6 → 1d6; backlash weakens/discretionary; only the survivor intercepts; absorption drops to 15. The survivor becomes more desperate and single-minded.

- **Middle rounds — keep it moving.** Make Druid damage matter; use Grounded Silence when earned; remind them the beams are the logic; soften optional hazards if they're struggling. **Don't** add minions, overuse slide checks, trigger backlash per Eldritch Blast beam, run the Druids as golem multiattack bags, or give the King a boss phase.

Interception, backlash, and the Crisis all end; absorption ends; go to **D1, the clarity window**.

# D. The Ending

`packet-08-ending-execution.canon.md` is the single canon source; everything in D1–D6 comes from it or the artifact file. **Ending style rule [CANON]:** plain emotional language, short natural sentences, clear cause and effect, grief without melodrama, satisfaction without cheering, closure without reunion. Avoid stacked poetic fragments, abstract declarations, "not as / not as" constructions, queen dialogue, reunion speeches, resurrection framing. "The ending may be mythic in consequence, but the words at the table should be human."

## D1. Clarity window [CANON]

> The second beam snaps.
>
> For the first time since you entered, the geode chamber is not humming.
>
> The King gasps. Not like a monster. Like an old man waking up in a room he does not recognize.
>
> His eyes move across the broken crystal, the shattered Druids, the throne that has held him, and the blast-scarred chamber around him.
>
> When he speaks, it is just his voice. The mountain is not speaking through him anymore.
>
> "What did I do?"

The scene's four jobs: show he is mortal and no longer the mountain's voice; confirm he understands enough to ask for release; leave room for mercy or the relic attempt; prevent a negotiation loop. Don't re-explain the lore.

## D2. The mercy plea [CANON] — use two to four, no monologue

> "Where is she? No. No, I know. I knew. I would not know."

> "Do not let me become the mountain again."

> "If there is mercy left in this place, let it be swift."

> "I tried to keep death from taking her. Instead I taught it to wait for everyone else."

> "I would have called it love, once. It was not."

> "Let the mountain release what I would not."

> "Before the grief remembers how to wear my voice — finish what the beams began to break."

Then ask the table what they do. He never says "kill me" or "end it."

**[REC] pairing:** "What did I do?" → "Where is she…" → "I tried to keep death…" → "Let the mountain release what I would not." If the party answers him with questions, give "I would have called it love, once. It was not." and nothing more.

## D4. Mercy Death and Release [CANON — verbatim]

>

**Fallbacks [CANON]:**

- Erny or Maelreth down or absent → the other gets the full attuned line. Both down → skip that paragraph.
- None of Alina, Sorin, Bilbo conscious → the attuned pair see the procession as the green clears from their eyes.
- Bilbo is the DM's character: address Bilbo in the line, but let Alina and Sorin carry the player reaction.
- It is a completed rite, not a reunion. The queen doesn't speak, turn to the King, or come back. The King doesn't reach for her.
